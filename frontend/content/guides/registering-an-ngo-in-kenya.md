If you want to run a charity, community project or advocacy group, there
are several legal forms in Kenya. The right one depends on the size and aim
of your work.

## The options

- **Public Benefit Organisation (PBO)** - the national form for NGOs, under
  the **Public Benefit Organizations Act 2013**, regulated by the **PBO
  Regulatory Authority**.
- **Community-based organisation (CBO)** or **self-help group** -
  registered with the Department of Social Development, for local groups.
  Simpler to set up.
- **Society** - such as clubs and associations, registered with the
  Registrar of Societies.
- **Trust** or **foundation** - often used by families and companies for
  charitable giving.
- **Company limited by guarantee** - a non-profit company registered with
  the Business Registration Service.

## The PBO Act

The PBO Act came into force in **May 2024**, replacing the old NGO
Coordination Act and the NGO Board. Existing NGOs are being moved into the
new system and must apply to the PBO Authority within a transition period
that has been extended. If you run an existing NGO, check the Authority's
latest notices for your deadline.

## Registering a PBO

1. **Choose a name** and check it is available.
2. **Prepare a constitution** setting out the organisation's purpose,
   governance, officials and how money is managed.
3. **Gather details** of the officials and members - IDs, [[KRA PIN]] numbers and
   contacts.
4. **Apply** to the PBO Regulatory Authority, mostly online, and pay the
   fee.
5. Once registered, apply for a [[KRA]] PIN for the organisation and open a
   bank account. See [Opening a bank account](/guides/opening-a-bank-account).

## Running it properly

- **Annual reports and returns** to the regulator.
- **Accounts** - keep proper books; larger organisations need audits.
- **Tax** - registered charities can apply to KRA for tax exemption.
- **Employees** - PAYE, NSSF, SHA and the housing levy apply as for any
  employer. See [Hiring your first employee](/guides/hiring-your-first-employee).
- **Governance** - hold meetings, keep minutes, and avoid conflicts of
  interest.

## Smaller groups

A **self-help group** or **CBO** suits a group of neighbours, a youth group
or a women's group. Registration is through the local social development
office. For saving and lending together, see [Chamas in Kenya](/guides/chamas-in-kenya).

## Get advice

The rules are changing as the PBO Act is put in place. An advocate or an
experienced NGO accountant can help. See
[Finding a lawyer in Kenya](/guides/finding-a-lawyer-in-kenya).
