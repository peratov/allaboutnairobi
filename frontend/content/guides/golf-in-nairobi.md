Nairobi has some of the oldest golf courses in East Africa, with tree-lined
fairways and mild weather all year. Playing at altitude means the ball flies
further than at sea level - a nice surprise for visitors.

## The clubs

Nairobi and its edges have more than a dozen courses, many of them private
members' clubs founded in the colonial era. Among the best known are:

- **Muthaiga Golf Club** - a historic championship course in Muthaiga, which
  has hosted the Kenya Open.
- **Royal Nairobi Golf Club** - close to the city centre, near Upper Hill.
- **Karen Country Club** - in Karen, with a parkland course.
- **Windsor** - a hotel and country club on the northern edge of the city.
- **Sigona** and **Limuru Country Club** - on the cooler, greener higher
  ground north-west of Nairobi.
- **Railway Golf Club** - near the CBD, with lower fees.

## Playing as a visitor

- Many clubs accept **visitors** for a day's **green fee**, especially on
  weekdays; weekends are often for members and their guests.
- Some clubs require you to be introduced by a member, or to show a
  handicap certificate from your home club.
- **Call ahead** to book a tee time and ask the visitor rules and fees.
- Respect the **dress code**: collared shirts, tailored shorts or trousers,
  and golf shoes.

## Caddies

Most clubs have **caddies**, who carry your bag and know the course well.
Caddie fees are paid to the club or caddie master, and a tip is usual for
good service. See [Tipping in Kenya](/guides/tipping-in-kenya).

## Membership

Joining a club usually involves an **entrance fee**, annual subscriptions,
and being proposed by existing members. Some clubs have waiting lists, and
corporate memberships are common. Ask about **country or overseas
membership** if you will not be in Nairobi all year.

## Learning

Most clubs have **teaching professionals** and driving ranges, with lessons
for adults and juniors. Junior programmes are a good way for children to
start.

## Golf around Kenya

Kenya has courses in the highlands, at the coast and on the shores of Lake
Victoria, and golf safaris combine the two. The **Kenya Open**, part of
international professional golf's tour, is held in Nairobi.
