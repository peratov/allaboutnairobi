Kenya produces the world's best distance runners, and Nairobi is a good city
to run in once you know where. The roads are hard work - traffic, fumes and
few pavements - so most people run in the forests and parks, early. See also
[Sport and fitness in Nairobi](/guides/sport-and-fitness-in-nairobi).

## Where to run

- **[Karura Forest](/map#place/karura-forest)** - Nairobi's favourite:
  marked trails of several lengths through forest, with waterfalls and
  caves, gates on Limuru Road and Kiambu Road, and a small entry fee. Busy and
  safe on weekend mornings.
- **[Ngong Road Forest](/map#place/ngong-road-forest)** - quieter, hillier
  trails between Ngong Road and Karen, popular for longer runs and mountain
  biking.
- **[Nairobi Arboretum](/map#place/nairobi-arboretum)** - short, shaded loops
  near State House and Kileleshwa, good for a quick run before work.
- **Oloolua Nature Trail** in Karen - forest and river paths.
- **Karen and Langata roads** at dawn - wide verges and less traffic.
- **The Ngong Hills** for long hill runs and hikes. See
  [Hiking near Nairobi](/guides/hiking-near-nairobi).

## The altitude

Nairobi sits high, and the air is thinner than at the coast or most of
Europe. Coming from lower ground, expect easy runs to feel hard for the first
week or two and your times to be slower for longer. Build up slowly, drink
more than you think you need, and use the sun - it is strong even when the
day feels cool. Many athletes train here for exactly this reason.

## Clubs and groups

Running groups meet at Karura, the Arboretum and in the suburbs, most on
weekend mornings, and many are free to join. The Nairobi Hash House Harriers
mix running with a social, and several gyms and shoe shops host weekly group
runs. They are the easiest way to find safe routes and running partners.

## Races

Nairobi has road races most months, from charity 5Ks to full marathons - the
biggest is the Nairobi Marathon each year, with half marathon and shorter
races alongside. Popular races sell out, so register early. The
[events page](/events) lists the big fixtures, including the Kip Keino Classic
for track athletics.

## Staying safe

- **Run in daylight**, in company, on routes others use.
- **Carry little** - a phone and a little money. Leave earphones out or low on
  roads and quiet paths.
- **Face the traffic** on roads; matatus pull in without warning.
- **Tell someone your route** for long runs.
- Avoid deserted stretches and very early or late hours in the forests -
  stick to opening times.
