AFCON 2027 is spread across **three countries and seven cities**. Fans
following their team may play in Nairobi one week and Kampala or Dar es Salaam
the next. Plan journeys early: flights and buses will fill up.

## The host cities

| City | Country | Stadium | From Nairobi |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Nairobi | Kenya | Raila Odinga, Kasarani, Nyayo | - |
| Kampala | Uganda | Mandela National Stadium | About 1 hour by air; a long day by road |
| Hoima | Uganda | Hoima City Stadium | Via Kampala, then road or a domestic flight |
| Lira | Uganda | Akii Bua Stadium | Via Kampala, then road |
| Dar es Salaam | Tanzania | Benjamin Mkapa Stadium | About 1.5 hours by air |
| Arusha | Tanzania | Samia Suluhu Hassan Stadium | About 1 hour by air, or 5-6 hours by road |
| Zanzibar | Tanzania | Amaan and Fumba stadiums | About 1.5 hours by air |

## Flying

Flying is the only practical way to cover most of these distances within a
tournament schedule.

- **From JKIA**, Kenya Airways, its low-cost sister Jambojet, and other
  airlines fly to **Entebbe** (for Kampala), **Dar es Salaam**, **Kilimanjaro**
  (for Arusha) and **Zanzibar**.
- **Entebbe airport** is about an hour from central Kampala, more in traffic.
- **Book early** and check baggage rules on smaller aircraft.

See [flights from Nairobi](/flights) and the [JKIA airport guide](/guides/jkia-airport-guide).

## By road

- **Nairobi to Arusha** is the shortest cross-border drive, about five to six
  hours via the **Namanga** border post. Shuttle buses run daily.
- **Nairobi to Kampala** is a long journey via the **Malaba** or **Busia**
  borders - a day or an overnight bus.
- **Nairobi to Dar es Salaam** by road takes most of a day or more.

Long-distance coach companies run these routes. Travel by day where you can,
and allow extra time at borders during the tournament.

## By rail

There is no direct passenger rail between the host countries. Within Kenya, the
SGR connects Nairobi and Mombasa. See [Nairobi to Mombasa](/guides/nairobi-to-mombasa).

## Visas and borders

- **Kenya** - the [[eTA]] for most visitors; most Africans are exempt. See
  [Applying for an eTA](/guides/kenya-eta-step-by-step).
- **Uganda** - a visa-fee waiver for visitors around the tournament has been
  announced.
- **Tanzania** - its own e-visa for most nationalities.
- **East African Community citizens** travel freely between the three.
- **The East Africa Tourist Visa** covers Kenya, Uganda and Rwanda, but **not
  Tanzania**.
- **A single AFCON visa** for all three hosts has been discussed but not
  confirmed.

Check the official immigration sites for your passport before booking, and
carry your match tickets and any Fan ID - they may be asked for at the border.

## Money and phones

- **Each country has its own currency** - Kenyan, Ugandan and Tanzanian
  shillings. Cards work in cities; carry some local cash.
- **Mobile money** works across borders in some cases, but check fees.
- **Roaming** can be expensive; a local SIM or a regional eSIM is cheaper. See
  [eSIMs for visitors](/guides/esims-and-sim-cards-for-visitors).

## Health

**Malaria** is a greater risk in parts of Uganda, coastal Tanzania and
Zanzibar than in Nairobi. Take advice from a travel clinic before you go.
