The **Consumer Protection Act 2012** and the Constitution give shoppers in
Kenya real rights - to goods that are safe and as described, honest
information, and a way to complain. Knowing them makes it easier to get a
problem fixed.

## Your rights

- **Goods must be of good quality**, fit for their purpose, and match their
  description or sample.
- **Services must be done with reasonable care and skill**, as agreed.
- **Information must be honest** - prices, ingredients, origin, what a
  product does. False or misleading advertising is not allowed.
- **Unfair contract terms**, especially in small print you could not
  negotiate, can be challenged.
- **You do not have to pay for goods you did not order.**
- **Safety** - goods must meet Kenyan standards and be safe to use.

## When something goes wrong

1. **Stop using it** if it is faulty or unsafe.
2. **Go back to the seller** quickly, with the receipt - ideally an eTIMS
   receipt with the seller's KRA PIN.
3. **Ask for what you want**: a repair, a replacement or a refund. For goods
   that were faulty when sold, a seller should put it right.
4. **Put it in writing** if they refuse, and keep copies.
5. **Escalate** to the right body (below).

Change-of-mind returns are up to the shop's own policy - ask before you buy.

## Where to complain

| Problem | Who to contact |
| --- | --- |
| Faulty goods, misleading adverts, unfair terms, refunds refused | The **Competition Authority of Kenya** (CAK), which handles consumer complaints |
| Unsafe or substandard products, missing standard marks | The **Kenya Bureau of Standards** (KEBS) |
| Counterfeit goods | The **Anti-Counterfeit Authority** |
| Fake or unsafe medicines | The **Pharmacy and Poisons Board** - see [Pharmacies and medicines](/guides/pharmacies-and-medicines-in-nairobi) |
| Phones, internet and mobile services | The **Communications Authority** |
| Banks, loan apps and M-Pesa | The CBK - see [Complaining about a bank](/guides/complaining-about-a-bank-in-kenya) |
| Insurance | The IRA - see [Insurance claims and complaints](/guides/insurance-claims-and-complaints-in-kenya) |
| Electricity | Kenya Power, then **EPRA** |

The **Consumers Federation of Kenya** (COFEK), a consumer group, can also
advise.

## Going to court

If you lose money, you can claim it back in the
[Small Claims Court](/guides/small-claims-court-in-kenya) - up to
KES {{ SMALL_CLAIMS_LIMIT|shillings }}, without a lawyer.

## Shopping online

The same rights apply online. Prefer pay-on-delivery, check the seller, and
keep screenshots of the listing and messages. See
[Online shopping in Kenya](/guides/online-shopping-in-kenya).

## Tips

- **Always ask for a receipt.**
- **Check standard marks** on electrical goods, food and building
  materials. See [Plugs, voltage and appliances](/guides/plugs-voltage-and-appliances-in-kenya).
- **Read warranties** - what is covered, for how long, and where repairs are
  done.
- **Take photos** of faults and of the product when it arrives.
