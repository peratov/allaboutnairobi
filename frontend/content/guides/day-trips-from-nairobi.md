Leave early. Getting out of Nairobi is the slow part, and a 6am start can halve
the drive. Journey times below are rough and assume moderate traffic.

## Ngong Hills

A ridge of seven peaks south-west of the city, about an hour away, with views
over the Rift Valley on one side and Nairobi on the other. The ridge walk takes
most of a morning. Go in a group or with a guide, start early before it gets
hot, and pay the entry fee at the Kenya Forest Service gate.

## Lake Naivasha and Hell's Gate

About two hours north-west, down the Rift Valley escarpment. The viewpoints on
the way down are worth a stop.

- **Hell's Gate National Park** - one of the few parks where you can walk or
  cycle among zebra, giraffe and buffalo, with a gorge walk at the end. Hire
  a bike at the gate.
- **Lake Naivasha** - boat rides among hippos and birds, and walking safaris
  on **Crescent Island**.

It is a long day, and many people stay overnight.

## Mount Longonot

A dormant volcano near Naivasha, about 90 minutes from Nairobi. The climb to
the crater rim is steep and dusty. Walking all the way round the rim makes it
a full day, so take plenty of water and a hat.

## Lake Magadi and Olorgesailie

South of Nairobi through the Kajiado plains, about two hours away. **Lake
Magadi** is a hot, strange soda lake with flamingos and hot springs.
**Olorgesailie** on the way is a prehistoric site with hand axes left where
early humans made them. It is very hot, so bring water.

## Limuru tea country

About an hour north-west, the tea estates around Limuru are green, cool and
peaceful, and several farms offer tea tours and lunch. Book ahead.

## Thika and Fourteen Falls

Up the Thika Superhighway, about an hour away. Fourteen Falls on the Athi
River is at its best after the rains.

## Better done overnight

For getting further afield, see [Travelling around Kenya](/guides/travelling-around-kenya).

- **Amboseli National Park** - elephants beneath Kilimanjaro, four to five hours
  away.
- **The Maasai Mara** - about five to six hours by road, or a short flight
  from Wilson Airport.
- **Ol Pejeta Conservancy** near Nanyuki - rhinos, including the last two
  northern white rhinos.
- **Mombasa and the coast** - by the SGR. See
  [Getting around Nairobi](/guides/getting-around-nairobi#trains).

## Before you go

- Parks run by the Kenya Wildlife Service take payment online and by card or
  M-Pesa, not cash.
- Malaria risk is higher at lower altitudes than in Nairobi; see
  [Healthcare in Nairobi](/guides/healthcare-in-nairobi#staying-healthy).
- Check the road and the weather in the rainy seasons, when murram roads can
  become impassable.
