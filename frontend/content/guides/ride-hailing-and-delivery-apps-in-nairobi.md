Ride-hailing apps are the easiest way to get around Nairobi for many
residents and visitors, especially at night. Delivery apps bring food,
groceries and parcels to your door. Here is how to use them well.

## The main ride-hailing apps

- **Uber** and **Bolt** - the largest, with cars across the city and to
  and from JKIA. Both offer cheaper small cars, standard cars, larger
  vehicles and, in some areas, **motorbike (boda) rides**.
- **Little** - a Kenyan app with cars, bodas and a corporate service used by
  many companies.
- Other local apps come and go; most drivers use more than one.

Compare fares across two apps: prices change with demand.

## Fares

- The app shows an **estimated or fixed fare** before you book.
- Fares rise at **rush hour**, in the **rain**, and late at night.
- Traffic adds to the time, and on some services to the fare.
- Work out what a regular commute costs in the
  [commute cost calculator](/tools/commute-cost-calculator).

## Paying

- **Cash** and **M-Pesa** are both common, and some apps take cards.
- When paying by M-Pesa, pay the amount shown in the app, and check the name
  on the confirmation.
- Some drivers ask for **cash only** or cancel cash trips; you can choose
  another driver.

## Safety

- **Check the car and number plate** match the app before getting in, and
  ask the driver who they are picking up rather than giving your name.
- **Share your trip** with a friend from the app.
- **Sit in the back**, and keep your phone and bag out of sight at
  traffic lights.
- If anything feels wrong, end the trip in a busy, lit place and report it
  in the app.
- For boda rides, **wear the helmet** offered and ask the rider to slow down
  if needed. Bodas are quick in traffic but carry far more risk than cars.

See [Safety in Nairobi](/guides/safety-in-nairobi).

## To and from the airport

Ride-hailing is usually cheaper than airport taxis. Pickups at JKIA are from
designated areas. See [JKIA airport guide](/guides/jkia-airport-guide).

## Delivery apps

- **Food** - delivery apps and many restaurants' own riders deliver across
  most of the city, usually by boda.
- **Groceries** - supermarkets and grocery apps deliver; the
  [[mama mboga]] down the road may also deliver by WhatsApp.
- **Parcels** - ride-hailing apps and courier companies send small items
  across town by boda, often within the hour. For longer distances, bus and
  shuttle companies carry parcels between towns.
- **Pharmacies** - several chains deliver medicines. See
  [Pharmacies and medicines](/guides/pharmacies-and-medicines-in-nairobi).

## Tipping

Tips are not expected on the apps, but rounding up or tipping in the app for
good service is appreciated. See [Tipping in Kenya](/guides/tipping-in-kenya).
