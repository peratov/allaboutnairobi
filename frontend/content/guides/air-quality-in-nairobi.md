Nairobi's air looks clear on most days, but monitoring shows fine-particle
pollution (PM2.5) often above the World Health Organization's guideline,
especially along busy roads and near dumpsites. It matters most for
children, older people, and anyone with asthma or heart disease.

## Where it comes from

- **Traffic** - old vehicles, diesel matatus and lorries, and congestion.
  Studies name traffic as one of the biggest sources.
- **Dust** - from unpaved roads, construction and the dry seasons.
- **Burning rubbish** - open burning of waste, and the Dandora dumpsite,
  near which some of the city's highest readings have been recorded.
- **Cooking fuels** - charcoal, firewood and paraffin indoors.
- **Industry** - especially around Industrial Area and Embakasi.

## When and where it is worst

- **Rush hours**, morning and evening, beside main roads and at jams.
- **The dry seasons**, when dust stays in the air.
- **Near dumpsites** and wherever rubbish is burned.
- **Indoors**, where people cook on charcoal or paraffin without ventilation.

## Checking the air

Low-cost sensors across Nairobi feed public readings. Search for live air
quality maps for Nairobi - AirQo and sensors.AFRICA publish readings - and
look at the station nearest you rather than a city average.

## Protecting yourself

- **Choose your route** - walk, run or cycle on quieter roads and in parks
  rather than beside main roads. See
  [Running in Nairobi](/guides/running-in-nairobi).
- **Keep car windows up** and the fan on recirculate in heavy traffic.
- **Ventilate when cooking** - cook outside or with windows open, and move to
  gas or electric cooking if you can.
- **Don't burn rubbish** - use a collector instead. See
  [Waste and recycling in Nairobi](/guides/waste-and-recycling-in-nairobi).
- **A well-fitted mask** (N95 or similar) helps on dusty or smoky days.
- **Asthma** - keep your inhaler with you and see a doctor if symptoms get
  worse. See [Healthcare in Nairobi](/guides/healthcare-in-nairobi).

## Choosing where to live

If air quality matters to you, look at homes away from main roads, the
Dandora area and Industrial Area. See
[Neighbourhoods of Nairobi](/guides/neighbourhoods-of-nairobi).
