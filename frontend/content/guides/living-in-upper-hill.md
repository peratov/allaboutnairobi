**Upper Hill** sits on the ridge south-west of the CBD. It is mainly a
business district - bank and company headquarters, embassies, hospitals and
some of Nairobi's tallest towers - with a growing number of apartment
blocks for people who want to live close to work.

## Who it suits

- **People working in Upper Hill or the CBD** who want to walk or take a
  very short ride to work.
- **Medical staff** at the large hospitals nearby.
- **Short-stay professionals** in serviced and furnished apartments.
- **Less so** families wanting gardens, or anyone wanting lively streets
  in the evening.

## Rents

Rough asking rents for flats in 2026 listings:

| What | Rough rent (KES a month) |
| --- | --- |
| One bedroom | 50,000 - 90,000 |
| Two bedrooms | 80,000 - 160,000 |
| Furnished or serviced flat | 140,000 and up |

Many blocks are newer, with lifts, gyms, backup power and parking. Furnished
and serviced flats are common and cost much more. See
[Serviced apartments in Nairobi](/guides/serviced-apartments-in-nairobi) and the
[move-in cost calculator](/tools/move-in-cost-calculator).

## Getting around

- **Walking** - many offices are within walking distance, and the CBD is a
  short ride.
- **Roads** - Upper Hill sits between Ngong Road, Mbagathi Way, Haile
  Selassie Avenue and the Expressway, so most of the city is reachable.
  Office traffic makes the roads busy at rush hour.
- **Ride-hailing and matatus** along Ngong Road and Haile Selassie Avenue.
- The **Nairobi Expressway** is close, for JKIA and Westlands. See
  [Using the Nairobi Expressway](/guides/using-the-nairobi-expressway).

## Daily life

- **Quiet in the evening and at weekends**, when the offices empty.
- **Food** - office cafés, restaurants in the towers and hotels, and many
  more in nearby Kilimani.
- **Shopping** - small supermarkets locally; larger malls in Kilimani,
  Hurlingham and the CBD.
- **Health** - some of Nairobi's major hospitals are in or next to Upper
  Hill, including Kenyatta National Hospital. See
  [Hospitals in Nairobi](/guides/hospitals-in-nairobi).
- **Green space** - the Nairobi Arboretum and Uhuru Park are close. See
  [Parks and green spaces](/guides/parks-and-green-spaces-in-nairobi).

## Things to check

- **Service charge** in newer towers, and what it covers.
- **Water and backup power** - most new blocks have both; ask.
- **Weekend life** - if you want restaurants and nightlife on your doorstep,
  Kilimani or Westlands may suit better.
