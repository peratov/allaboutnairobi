The **Hustler Fund** (the Financial Inclusion Fund) is a government lending
scheme that gives small loans on your phone, mainly for traders and
low-income earners. It is cheaper than most mobile loan apps, but it is
still a loan - and late repayment affects your credit record.

## How to get it

- Dial **\*254#** from a registered line on Safaricom, Airtel or Telkom,
  and follow the prompts to opt in.
- You need a **Kenyan ID** and a registered mobile money account.
- Your first limit is small and set automatically.

## The terms

- **Interest** is **{{ HUSTLER_FUND_INTEREST_RATE|percent }}% a year**,
  charged daily - much lower than most mobile loans.
- A personal loan is due within **{{ HUSTLER_FUND_TERM_DAYS }} days**. A
  higher rate applies if you pay late.
- **{{ HUSTLER_FUND_SAVINGS_SHARE|percent }}%** of each loan goes into a
  **savings** account in your name, split between short-term and long-term
  savings.
- Personal loan limits go up to **KES {{ HUSTLER_FUND_MAX_PERSONAL_LOAN|shillings }}**
  for borrowers with a good repayment history.

Check the current terms on \*254# before borrowing; they have changed over
time.

## Loan types

- **Personal loans** - short-term loans to individuals.
- **Bridge loans** - larger, longer loans for top-rated borrowers.
- **Group loans** - for registered groups, such as a [[chama]].

## Raising your limit

- **Repay on time** or early, every time.
- **Borrow and repay regularly** - a steady record helps.
- Limits are reviewed automatically; there is no way to "buy" a higher one.

## Risks

- **Defaulting** is reported to credit bureaus and can block other loans.
  See [Checking your CRB status](/guides/checking-your-crb-status).
- **Borrowing to repay** other loans can trap you in debt.
- **Scams** - nobody can raise your limit for a fee. The only channel is
  \*254#. See [M-Pesa fraud and scams](/guides/mpesa-fraud-and-scams-in-kenya).

## Compared with other loans

The Hustler Fund is usually cheaper than mobile loan apps and overdraft
services, but SACCOs and [chamas](/guides/chamas-in-kenya) can
offer bigger loans for business. See
[The true cost of mobile loans](/guides/mobile-loans-true-cost) and
[SACCOs in Nairobi](/guides/saccos-in-nairobi).
