Kenyan food is famously meaty - [[nyama choma]] is a national pastime - but
Nairobi is an easy city to eat vegetarian or vegan in. Many everyday Kenyan
dishes are meat-free, and the city's long-established Indian community has
given it some excellent vegetarian restaurants.

## Kenyan dishes without meat

- **[[Ugali]] with [[sukuma wiki]]** - maize meal with braised greens.
- **Githeri** - maize and beans, often cooked with vegetables.
- **Mukimo** - mashed potatoes, greens, maize and peas.
- **Beans and lentils (ndengu)** - stewed, with rice or chapati.
- **Chapati, mandazi** and **viazi karai** (battered potatoes).
- **Fruit** - mangoes, pineapples, bananas and avocados are cheap and good.

Ask whether stews are cooked with meat stock or animal fat, and whether
greens have meat added.

## Indian vegetarian food

**Parklands** and **Westlands** have Nairobi's best-known vegetarian
restaurants, many fully vegetarian and serving Gujarati, South Indian and
Jain food. **Diamond Plaza** in Parklands has a busy courtyard of
vegetarian-friendly eateries. Bhajia, samosa, dosa and thali are easy wins,
and many places mark vegan options or will adjust dishes if you ask - check
for ghee, butter and paneer.

## Other options

- **Ethiopian restaurants** serve fasting (vegan) dishes on injera.
- **Cafés and restaurants** in Westlands, Kilimani and Karen increasingly
  offer plant-based menus. See
  [Coffee and tea in Nairobi](/guides/coffee-and-tea-in-nairobi).
- **Street food** - roasted maize, fruit, chips and vegetable samosas. See
  [Street food in Nairobi](/guides/street-food-in-nairobi).

## Cooking at home

- **Markets and the [[mama mboga]]** sell fresh vegetables, beans and
  lentils cheaply. See [Markets in Nairobi](/guides/markets-in-nairobi).
- **Indian grocers** in Parklands stock spices, pulses and specialist items.
- **Supermarkets** carry plant milks, tofu and meat substitutes, at a price.

## Eating out tips

- Say **"bila nyama"** (without meat). See
  [Swahili for daily life](/guides/swahili-for-daily-life).
- Ask about **stock, fat and fish** - "vegetarian" is sometimes read as "no
  red meat".
- At weddings and events, tell the host in advance.
