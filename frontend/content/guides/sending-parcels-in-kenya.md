Sending something [[upcountry]], across Nairobi or abroad is easy once you
know the options. Price, speed and security differ a lot between them.

## Within Nairobi

- **Boda and app deliveries** - the fastest way to send a parcel across
  town, often within the hour. Book on a ride-hailing app or use a trusted
  rider. See [Ride-hailing and delivery apps](/guides/ride-hailing-and-delivery-apps-in-nairobi).
- **Same-day courier** services for documents and business deliveries.

## Upcountry

- **Bus and matatu parcel services** - most long-distance bus companies and
  many matatu saccos run **parcel offices** at their Nairobi stages.
  Cheap and quick to towns on their routes; the recipient collects at the
  destination office with ID.
- **Courier companies** - door-to-door or office-to-office delivery across
  Kenya, with tracking. More expensive but better for valuables and
  documents.
- **Posta Kenya** - the national post office, with branches in most towns,
  for letters, parcels and its **EMS** express service.

## Abroad

- **Posta** sends international mail and parcels, including by EMS.
- **International couriers** are faster and track every step, at higher
  prices.
- Customs rules apply at the other end, and some items cannot be sent at
  all. For bringing things into Kenya, see
  [Importing goods into Kenya](/guides/importing-goods-into-kenya).

## Tips

- **Pack well** - strong boxes, tape, and padding for anything fragile.
- **Label clearly** with the recipient's name, phone number and town;
  phone numbers matter more than addresses in Kenya.
- **Get a receipt or waybill** with a tracking or reference number, and
  send it to the recipient.
- **Declare valuables** and ask about insurance; many cheap services limit
  their liability.
- **Don't send cash** in parcels - use [[M-Pesa]] or a bank.
- **Don't carry parcels for strangers** - you are responsible for what's
  inside.

## Addresses and P.O. boxes

Many Kenyan homes don't have a street address that couriers can use. A
**P.O. box** at Posta is still common for businesses and official mail;
otherwise give a clear landmark and a phone number.
