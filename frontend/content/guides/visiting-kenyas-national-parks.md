Kenya's national parks and reserves - from **Nairobi National Park** on the
city's edge to Amboseli, Tsavo and Lake Nakuru - are run mostly by the
**Kenya Wildlife Service** (KWS). The **Maasai Mara** is a national reserve
run by Narok County, with its own fees and system.

## Fees

- Fees are charged **per person per day**, with separate rates for **Kenyan
  citizens**, **East African citizens**, **residents** and **non-residents**.
  Children and students pay less.
- **Vehicles** pay an entry fee too.
- Parks are grouped into price tiers; the most popular parks cost most.
- KWS introduced new fees in 2025 and they have been **challenged in
  court**, so check the current rates on KWS's official site before you go.

Bring proof of status - a Kenyan ID, an [alien card](/guides/alien-card-for-foreigners-in-kenya)
or a permit for residents - as rangers check.

## Paying

- Payment is **cashless**. Pay **online** through KWS's official payment
  system, linked to [[eCitizen]], or by **M-Pesa** or card.
- You receive a **receipt with a QR code**, which is scanned at the gate.
- A **convenience fee** may be added to online payments.
- Pay only through official KWS channels - not to individuals at the gate.

## Booking

- Most parks don't need advance booking for day visits; just pay and go.
- **Accommodation** inside parks - KWS bandas and campsites, lodges and
  camps - should be booked ahead, especially in holidays.
- Tour operators usually include fees in the price. See
  [Choosing a safari company](/guides/choosing-a-safari-company).

## Rules inside the park

- **Stay in your vehicle** except at marked picnic sites, viewpoints and
  walking trails.
- **Stay on the tracks**; off-road driving is banned in most parks.
- **Keep to the speed limit**, and give animals space.
- **Don't feed animals** or leave litter.
- **Be out by closing time**, usually around dusk.
- **Drones** need permission.

## Saving money

- Kenyans and residents pay far less - go yourself rather than on a
  visitor-priced tour.
- Share a car with friends; the vehicle fee is split.
- Some parks offer **annual or multiple-visit** options; ask KWS.
- Visit in the **low season** for cheaper lodges.
