Karen and Lang'ata lie on Nairobi's south-western edge, beside Nairobi
National Park and the Ngong Forest. Karen feels semi-rural, with large plots
and horses; Lang'ata is more mixed, with estates and apartments. They are on
the [map](/map#constituency/langata).

## The difference

- **Karen** - large houses on half-acre to multi-acre plots, gated
  communities and townhouses, boutique shops and garden restaurants. Popular
  with families and people who want space.
- **Lang'ata** - middle-class estates, townhouses and apartment blocks, closer
  to the city and Wilson Airport, and more affordable.

## Rents

Rough asking rents in 2026 listings:

| What | Karen (KES a month) | Lang'ata (KES a month) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Two-bedroom flat or cottage | 80,000 - 150,000 | 35,000 - 70,000 |
| Three- or four-bedroom townhouse | 150,000 - 300,000 | 70,000 - 150,000 |
| Four-bedroom house with garden | 180,000 - 350,000 and up | Fewer available |

## Who they suit

- **Families** - space, gardens and many schools, including Hillcrest,
  Brookhouse and Banda. See [International schools](/guides/international-schools-in-nairobi).
- **People who work from home**, or in Karen or Lang'ata.
- **Nature lovers** - the national park, the Giraffe Centre, riding and forest
  walks.
- **Less so** people commuting daily to Westlands or Gigiri.

## Getting around

- **A car is essential in Karen.** Distances are long and matatus limited.
- **To the CBD and Upper Hill** - 30 minutes to over an hour on Lang'ata or
  Ngong Road.
- **To Westlands** - often 45 minutes or more; the Southern Bypass and the
  Expressway help.
- **To JKIA** - via the Southern Bypass, often quicker than from the north.

## Safety

Houses on large plots need good security: guards, alarms linked to a response
company, and electric fencing are normal. Residents' associations are active
and share security news. See [Safety in Nairobi](/guides/safety-in-nairobi).

## Daily life

- **Shopping:** The Hub Karen, Waterfront Karen, Karen Crossroads and Galleria
  on Lang'ata Road.
- **Health:** Karen Hospital, and clinics in the malls.
- **Outdoors:** Nairobi National Park, Ngong Forest, riding, and the Karen
  Blixen Museum.

## Water and power

Many Karen homes rely on **boreholes** and large tanks, and outages can last
longer on the edges of the grid. Ask about backup power and water. See
[Utilities in Nairobi](/guides/utilities-in-nairobi).
