Streaming services, app stores, cloud storage and shops abroad usually want
a card. In Kenya, you have a few ways to pay - each with its own costs.

## Your options

- **A bank debit or credit card.** Most Kenyan banks issue Visa or
  Mastercard cards that work online. Some cards must be **enabled for
  online or international payments** first, in the banking app or by
  calling the bank. See [Credit cards in Kenya](/guides/credit-cards-in-kenya).
- **M-PESA GlobalPay.** A **virtual Visa card** linked to your M-Pesa,
  set up in the M-Pesa app. It is for paying **international** merchants
  online, and money is taken from your M-Pesa balance.
- **M-Pesa directly.** Some services sold in Kenya accept M-Pesa at
  checkout. Check the payment options before adding a card.
- **Gift cards and vouchers**, for some app stores and games.
- **PayPal**, linked to a card. See
  [Receiving PayPal, Payoneer and Wise payments](/guides/receiving-paypal-payoneer-and-wise-payments).

## What it costs

- **Currency conversion.** Services priced in dollars or euros are
  converted to shillings. Your bank or Safaricom adds a margin: Safaricom
  has charged **3.5%** on GlobalPay payments, and banks charge their own
  fees. Check before you choose.
- **VAT.** Foreign digital services sold to people in Kenya - streaming,
  apps, software - are charged Kenyan VAT, usually included in the price.
- **Paying in shillings.** Where a service offers a price in shillings, it
  can be cheaper than paying in dollars and converting.

## Subscriptions and repeat payments

- Keep track of every subscription: they renew automatically.
- **Cancel in the service itself** (its website or app settings), not just by
  removing the card.
- On **GlobalPay**, you can turn off repeat payments in the GlobalPay menu
  in the M-Pesa app or on Safaricom's USSD menu.
- With a bank card, ask the bank to block a merchant if a cancelled service
  keeps charging.

## Staying safe

- Use a separate card - or GlobalPay - with a **small balance** for online
  shopping, rather than your main account.
- Turn on **notifications** for every card payment.
- Only enter card details on the service's own website or app. Fake
  "your subscription has expired" emails and texts are common. See
  [M-Pesa fraud and scams](/guides/mpesa-fraud-and-scams-in-kenya).
- If a payment you did not make appears, **block the card** in the app and
  call your bank.

## Shopping from foreign websites

Goods bought abroad and shipped to Kenya may be charged **import duty and
VAT** when they arrive. See [Online shopping in Kenya](/guides/online-shopping-in-kenya)
and the [import duty calculator](/tools/import-duty-calculator).
