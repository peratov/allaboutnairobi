Everyone with a [[KRA PIN]] must file an income tax return every year by
**{{ TAX_RETURN_DEADLINE }}**, even in a year with no income. If you had no
taxable income at all, that return is a **nil return**. It takes a few minutes
on [[iTax]].

## Should you file a nil return?

**Yes, if** you had no income in the year: a student, someone unemployed all
year, or someone who got a PIN for a car, land or a bank account but earns
nothing.

**No, if** you had any of these:

- **A salary.** File the employment income return with your P9 instead, even
  though PAYE was already deducted. See
  [Filing your return with a P9](/guides/filing-your-kra-return-with-a-p9).
- **Business or freelance income.** File a full return with your income and
  expenses. See [Freelancing in Kenya](/guides/freelancing-in-kenya).
- **Rent** that you did not already pay monthly rental income tax on.

Filing a nil return when you had income is a false return, and KRA can see
what your employer, bank and clients report against your PIN.

## Step by step

1. Go to **itax.kra.go.ke** and log in with your PIN (or ID number) and
   password. Solve the small sum on the screen.
2. Open **Returns**, then **File Nil Return**.
3. Choose **Income Tax - Resident Individual** as the tax obligation and click
   **Next**.
4. The return period is filled in for the previous year (January to
   December). Check it, then **Submit**.
5. Download the **e-Return acknowledgement receipt**, and keep it.

That is all. There is nothing to pay.

## Forgot your password?

Use **Forgot password** on the login page. The reset goes to the email address
registered on your PIN. If you no longer use that email, you have to update it
through KRA - by raising an issue on the iTax portal, calling the contact
centre, or visiting a KRA service office with your ID.

## The deadline and penalties

- **Deadline:** {{ TAX_RETURN_DEADLINE }} for the previous calendar year.
- **Late filing penalty:** KES {{ LATE_FILING_PENALTY|shillings }} for an
  individual, or 5% of any tax due if that is higher - even on a return that
  shows no tax.
- **Old years:** if you missed earlier years, you can file those nil returns
  too. Penalties may already have been charged against your PIN; you can
  apply on iTax for them to be waived, giving your reasons.

## Other tax obligations

When you file, iTax may list other obligations on your PIN, such as VAT or
turnover tax, that you signed up for by mistake. Each registered obligation
needs its own returns, including nil ones, until it is removed. If an
obligation does not apply to you, apply to deregister it on iTax rather than
leaving it to build up penalties.
