Nairobi has two rainy seasons, and heavy downpours can flood roads, estates
near rivers and low-lying areas within minutes. Knowing the risks and
following warnings keeps you safe.

## When the rains come

- The **long rains** usually fall from around **March to May**.
- The **short rains** usually fall from around **October to December**.
- Some years bring much heavier rain than usual. See
  [Weather in Nairobi](/guides/weather-in-nairobi).

Follow **Kenya Meteorological Department** forecasts and county warnings,
especially in the rainy months.

## Where floods hit

- **Riverbanks** - homes along the Nairobi, Mathare and Ngong rivers and
  their streams.
- **Low-lying estates** and roads with poor drainage.
- **Underpasses** and dips on roads.
- Floods can arrive **even where it isn't raining**, from rain upstream.

Before renting, ask whether the area floods. See
[Finding housing](/guides/finding-housing).

## On the road

- **Never drive into floodwater** - about a foot of moving water can sweep
  away a car.
- **Avoid flooded underpasses** and low bridges.
- Slow down - roads are slippery and visibility is poor.
- **Don't take boda bodas** in heavy rain.
- Expect very heavy traffic; leave early or wait it out.

See [Driving in Nairobi](/guides/driving-in-nairobi).

## On foot

- **Don't walk through moving water** - even shallow water can knock you
  over, and open drains and manholes are hidden.
- Keep children away from drains, rivers and floodwater.
- Stay away from fallen power lines.

## At home

- **Clear gutters and drains** before the rains.
- **Move valuables and documents** off the floor and keep them in
  waterproof bags.
- **Know your way out** to higher ground, and leave early if officials tell
  you to evacuate.
- Keep a **torch, charged phone, water and medicines** ready.

Insurance can cover flood damage in some policies - see
[Home and contents insurance](/guides/home-and-contents-insurance-in-kenya).

## Health

- **Floodwater is dirty** - it can carry sewage. Wash hands, and treat or
  boil drinking water. See [Water tanks and boreholes](/guides/water-tanks-and-boreholes).
- Watch for **cholera** and **typhoid** - seek care for severe diarrhoea.
- **Mosquitoes** breed in standing water; use nets. See
  [Vaccinations and malaria](/guides/vaccinations-and-malaria-in-kenya).
