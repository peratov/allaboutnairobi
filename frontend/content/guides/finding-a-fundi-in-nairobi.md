In Nairobi, a tradesperson is a **fundi**: the plumber, electrician, mason,
carpenter, painter or welder you call when something breaks. Good fundis are
found mainly by recommendation, and the best are busy.

## Finding one

- **Ask around** - neighbours, colleagues, your estate's WhatsApp group, or
  the caretaker of your building.
- **Your landlord or agent** - if you rent, repairs to the building, plumbing
  and wiring are usually the landlord's job. Report it in writing before
  hiring anyone yourself. See [Tenant rights in Kenya](/guides/tenant-rights-in-kenya).
- **Hardware shops** often know local fundis and can vouch for them.
- **Companies** - for bigger or specialist jobs (solar, boreholes, security
  systems, appliances), a registered company costs more but is accountable.

## Licences to ask for

- **Electricians** - electrical work should be done by an electrician
  licensed by the **Energy and Petroleum Regulatory Authority** (EPRA). This
  matters for safety, insurance and new connections to Kenya Power.
- **Solar installers** - solar PV technicians and contractors are also
  licensed by EPRA. See [Solar and backup power](/guides/solar-and-backup-power).
- **Builders and contractors** - contractors, and many skilled construction
  workers, are registered with the **National Construction Authority** (NCA).
  For a house, see [Building a house in Kenya](/guides/building-a-house-in-kenya).

Ask to see the licence or registration card, and check the name.

## Agreeing the job

1. **Get them to look first** and explain what is wrong.
2. **Agree a price in writing** - even a WhatsApp message - covering labour,
   materials, and how long it will take.
3. For larger jobs, **get two or three quotes**.
4. **Buy the materials yourself**, or ask for receipts, so you know what you
   paid for. Cheap fittings and cables are a common way to cut corners.
5. Agree what happens if the job is not finished, or something else breaks.

## Paying

- **Do not pay the full amount upfront.** For a small job, pay on
  completion. For a large one, pay in stages as work is done.
- **Pay by M-Pesa** so there is a record, and check the name on the
  confirmation.
- Test the work before the final payment: run the taps, switch on the
  circuits, check for leaks.
