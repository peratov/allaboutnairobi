Nairobi is full of small businesses, from market stalls to online shops. The
ones that last usually start small, keep costs low, and solve a problem
customers already have. This guide covers common ideas and what each needs -
not a promise of profit.

## Questions to answer first

- **Who will buy, and why from you?** Visit similar businesses and watch who
  buys and what they pay.
- **How much do you need to start**, and to keep going for six months without
  profit?
- **Where will you trade** - premises, a market, online, or mobile?
- **What licences apply?** See below.

Use the [budget planner](/tools/budget-planner) and
[loan repayment calculator](/tools/loan-repayment-calculator) before you
borrow.

## Common ideas and what they need

**Food businesses** - cafés, food kiosks, bakeries, catering, juice and snacks.
Need the county business permit, **food hygiene licence** and **medical
certificates for food handlers**, and fire safety approval.

**Retail** - a shop, [[duka]], cosmetics, phone accessories, clothes or
[[mitumba]]. Need the county business permit, and care with suppliers - buy
genuine goods and keep receipts.

**Beauty** - salons and barbershops. County permit and health requirements.

**Services** - cleaning, laundry, tutoring, photography, event planning, car
washing, repairs. Often need little money to start; skills and reputation
matter most.

**Online businesses** - selling through social media or a website, digital
services, design and writing. See
[Freelancing in Kenya](/guides/freelancing-in-kenya) and
[Online shopping](/guides/online-shopping-in-kenya).

**Transport and delivery** - [[boda boda]] and delivery services. Need NTSA
licensing, insurance and a good vehicle. See [Car insurance](/guides/car-insurance-in-kenya).

**Agribusiness** - poultry, vegetables, and supplying hotels and restaurants,
often on land outside the city.

## The basics for any business

1. **Register** a business name or company. See
   [Registering a business](/guides/registering-a-business-in-kenya).
2. **Get the county permit.** See [Nairobi business permit](/guides/nairobi-business-permit).
3. **Tax** - turnover tax or income tax, and eTIMS invoicing. See
   [Turnover tax vs income tax](/guides/turnover-tax-vs-income-tax).
4. **Separate money** - a business bank account or M-Pesa till, and records
   of every sale and expense.

## Costs people forget

- **Rent deposits and fit-out** for premises.
- **Stock you cannot sell** - start with small quantities.
- **Permits renewed every year.**
- **Your own pay** - the business must cover your living costs eventually.
