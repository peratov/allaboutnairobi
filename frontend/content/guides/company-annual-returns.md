Every limited company in Kenya must file an **annual return** with the
Business Registration Service (BRS), even if it is dormant or has only one
director. Companies that do not file can be **struck off the register** - and
the Registrar has been doing so in 2026.

## What the annual return is

The **CR29** annual return confirms the company's current details: directors,
shareholders, share capital and registered office. It is filed each year
after the anniversary of incorporation.

## Filing on eCitizen

1. Log in to [[eCitizen]] and open the **Business Registration Service**.
2. Choose the company, then **company filings** and the **annual return
   (CR29)**.
3. Check the pre-filled details, and update anything that has changed.
4. Download the form, have it signed by a director or the company secretary,
   and upload it.
5. Pay the fee of KES {{ ANNUAL_RETURN_FEE|shillings }}.

Late filing attracts penalties. If you have missed several years, file all the
outstanding returns.

## Other yearly filings

- **Company income tax return** on [[iTax]], within six months of the
  financial year end, even with no income.
- **Monthly tax returns** for obligations the company is registered for, such
  as VAT, PAYE or turnover tax - nil returns included.
- **Beneficial ownership** - update the register when owners change.
- **The county business permit** renewal. See
  [Nairobi business permit](/guides/nairobi-business-permit).

## When things change

File changes with the BRS within the time allowed, including:

- **Directors** appointed or leaving.
- **Shareholding** changes.
- **Registered office** address.

## Dormant companies

A company that is not trading must still file annual returns and tax returns.
If you no longer need it, apply to **deregister** it properly rather than
letting it lapse - a struck-off company's assets can pass to the state, and
directors can face penalties.
