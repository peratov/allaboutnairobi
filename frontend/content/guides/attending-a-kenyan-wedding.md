Kenyan weddings are big, joyful and often long - and guests are part of
making them happen. Whether you're invited by a colleague or a close
friend, knowing what to expect helps you join in.

## Before the wedding

- **Wedding committee meetings** - friends and family often form a
  committee that meets weekly in the months before, to plan and raise
  money. If you're added to the group, contributions are expected if you can.
- **Contributions** - pledges are made by [[M-Pesa]] to the committee's
  paybill or treasurer. Give what you can afford; it's a way of supporting
  the couple, much like a [[harambee]].
- **Other ceremonies** - many couples also hold traditional events, such as
  a **ruracio** or other dowry ceremonies, before the church or civil
  wedding. See [Dowry and customary marriage](/guides/dowry-and-customary-marriage-in-kenya).

## The day

- **The ceremony** - usually in a church, or a civil, Muslim, Hindu or
  garden wedding. Church services can be long, with songs and speeches.
- **The reception** - often outdoors in a garden or hall, with food,
  speeches, music, dancing and the cake. Expect it to go on for hours.
- **Timing** - weddings often start later than the invitation says, and
  run late. Eat beforehand if you can, and plan your ride home.

## What to wear

- **Smart and colourful** - dresses, suits or traditional outfits in
  kitenge or other fabrics. See [Fabric and tailors](/guides/fabric-and-tailors-in-nairobi).
- Some weddings have a **theme colour** - check the invitation.
- **Modest** for church and religious ceremonies; covered shoulders are
  safer.
- **Comfortable shoes** for grass and dancing, and something warm for
  evenings.

## Gifts

- **Cash** in an envelope or by M-Pesa is most common.
- **Household gifts** are welcome; some couples have a gift list.
- Many receptions have a **gift presentation** where guests dance up to
  present gifts.

## Etiquette

- **RSVP** if asked, and don't bring uninvited guests to a sit-down
  reception.
- **Follow the MC** - there is usually a programme, with speeches from
  family and friends.
- **Photos** - ask before posting the couple's photos online.
- **Leave after** the couple or the main programme ends, if you can.
