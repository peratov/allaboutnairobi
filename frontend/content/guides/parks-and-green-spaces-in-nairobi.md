For a crowded city, Nairobi has a surprising amount of green - forests with
waterfalls, old gardens and a national park with lions on the edge of town.
These are the places Nairobians go to walk, run, cycle and picnic.

## Karura Forest

A large urban forest on the north side of the city, off **Limuru Road** and
**Kiambu Road**. Well-kept trails for walking, running and cycling, a
**waterfall**, caves, a lake and picnic spots. It is managed by the **Kenya
Forest Service** with the community group **Friends of Karura**.

- It is open from **early morning to early evening**, with the last entry in
  the late afternoon.
- **Entry is paid by M-Pesa through eCitizen**, with different fees for
  citizens, residents and non-residents. Check the current fees before you go.
- Bicycles can be hired inside, and there are guided walks.

## Nairobi Arboretum

Off **State House Road**, near the University of Nairobi. Shady, old trees,
easy walking paths, and a popular spot for picnics, runs, and weekend
events. A good choice close to the city centre.

## City Park

In **Parklands**, off Limuru Road. One of Nairobi's oldest parks: remnant
forest, a maze, monkeys, and the grave of Joseph Murumbi, Kenya's second
vice-president. Quieter than Karura.

## Ngong Road Forest Sanctuary

Along **Ngong Road**, between the city and Karen. Forest trails for walking,
running and cycling, with a range of routes.

## Oloolua Forest

In **Karen**. A nature trail along a river through indigenous forest, with a
waterfall and caves. Calm, and close to the Karen and Lang'ata area. See
[Living in Karen and Lang'ata](/guides/living-in-karen-and-langata).

## Uhuru Park and Central Park

Next to the **CBD**. Open lawns, a lake with paddle boats, and space to sit -
busy at weekends and holidays. Best in daylight.

## Jeevanjee Gardens

A small, historic garden in the **CBD**, full of office workers at lunchtime
and preachers, debaters and musicians at other times.

## Nairobi National Park

Wildlife - lions, rhinos, giraffes and buffalo - with the city skyline
behind. See [Nairobi National Park](/guides/nairobi-national-park).

## Tips

- **Go in daylight**, and in a group for quieter forests.
- **Take water**, sun protection and a light jacket - Nairobi's mornings
  are cool.
- Keep valuables to a minimum, and leave nothing visible in the car.
- **Respect the forest rules**: stay on trails, take your rubbish with you,
  and do not feed monkeys.
