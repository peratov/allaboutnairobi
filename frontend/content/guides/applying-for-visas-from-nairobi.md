For Kenyans, visiting the UK, US, Europe or Canada means applying for a visa
in advance - usually online, then in person in Nairobi. The process is
detailed but manageable, and the key is a complete, honest application,
started early.

## The general process

1. **Apply online** on the country's official immigration website, and pay
   the fee.
2. **Book an appointment** - at a **visa application centre** for
   fingerprints and photos, or at the **embassy** for an interview.
3. **Attend** with your passport and documents.
4. **Wait for a decision**, and collect your passport.

Apply only through **official government websites** and their appointed visa
centres. Third-party sites charge extra, and some are scams.

## By country

- **United Kingdom** - apply online on the UK government's website; then
  give biometrics at the UK's visa application centre in Nairobi.
- **United States** - complete the **DS-160** online, pay the fee, and book
  an **interview** at the US Embassy in Gigiri. Waiting times for interviews
  can be long; check them before you plan travel.
- **Schengen (most of Europe)** - apply to the embassy of the country you
  will mainly visit (or enter first, if no main country), usually through
  its visa centre. A Schengen visa covers short stays across the Schengen
  area.
- **Canada** - apply online, then give biometrics at Canada's visa
  application centre in Nairobi.

## Documents to prepare

Requirements vary, but expect:

- a **passport** valid well beyond your trip, with blank pages - see
  [Applying for a Kenyan passport](/guides/applying-for-a-kenyan-passport)
- **bank statements** for several months, showing you can pay for the trip
- **proof of employment or business** - a letter from your employer, leave
  approval, payslips, or business registration and KRA returns
- **ties to Kenya** - property, family, studies or a job you return to
- **travel plans** - itinerary, accommodation and, for some, an invitation
  letter
- **travel insurance** for Schengen - see
  [Travel insurance from Kenya](/guides/travel-insurance-from-kenya)
- previous passports with travel history

## Tips

- **Apply early** - months ahead for busy periods.
- **Be consistent and truthful** - inconsistencies are a common reason for
  refusal, and false documents can lead to a ban.
- **Explain unusual items** - large recent deposits in your bank statement,
  for example - with supporting documents.
- **Keep copies** of everything you submit.
- If refused, read the reasons carefully before reapplying.

## Scams to avoid

- "Agents" who promise guaranteed visas, jobs abroad or "work permits" for a
  fee.
- Fake job offers that ask for money for visas or tickets.
- Anyone asking you to pay into a personal M-Pesa number for an embassy
  appointment.

See [M-Pesa fraud and scams](/guides/mpesa-fraud-and-scams-in-kenya).
