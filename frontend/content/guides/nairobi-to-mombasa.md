Mombasa and the beaches of the Kenyan coast are around 480 kilometres from
Nairobi. Most people take the train or fly; buses are cheapest, and driving
gives freedom on the coast.

## At a glance

| | Time | Cost one way | Best for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| SGR train | About 5 hours | From KES 1,500 | Most people |
| Flight | About 1 hour in the air | Varies widely | Short trips, Diani, Malindi |
| Bus | 8 to 10 hours | Cheapest | Tight budgets |
| Driving | 8 to 10 hours | Fuel and tolls | Families, exploring the coast |

## The SGR train (Madaraka Express)

The **Madaraka Express** runs several times a day between the **Nairobi
Terminus** near Syokimau and the **Mombasa Terminus** at Miritini, on the
mainland west of Mombasa island.

- **Fares:** economy KES {{ SGR_FARE_ECONOMY|shillings }}, first class
  KES {{ SGR_FARE_FIRST|shillings }}, one way. Children pay less.
- **Time:** around five hours; express trains are quicker than the
  inter-county trains that stop at every station.
- **Booking:** online through Kenya Railways' booking site, or by USSD from
  a Safaricom line, paying by M-Pesa. Book early for weekends and holidays -
  trains sell out.
- **You need the ID or passport** you booked with, and must arrive early for
  security screening.
- **Views:** the line passes through Tsavo, and you may see elephants and
  giraffes from the window.

Getting to the Nairobi Terminus takes time in traffic, so allow for it. See
[Getting around Nairobi](/guides/getting-around-nairobi#trains).

## Flying

Kenya Airways, Jambojet and other airlines fly from **JKIA** to Mombasa, and
light-aircraft operators fly from **Wilson Airport** to Mombasa, **Ukunda**
(for Diani), Malindi and Lamu. Fares vary a lot with the date - book early.
Flying to Ukunda avoids the Likoni ferry, the slowest part of reaching Diani
by road.

## Buses

Long-distance coach companies run day and night services from the city
centre. They are cheapest but slowest. Choose an established company, and
travel by day where you can. The Mombasa road carries heavy lorry traffic.

## Driving

The Nairobi-Mombasa road (A109) is busy with lorries and has speed limits and
police checks. Allow a full day, avoid driving at night, and break the
journey. A car is useful for exploring the coast. See
[Driving in Nairobi](/guides/driving-in-nairobi) and
[Car hire in Nairobi](/guides/car-hire-in-nairobi).

## At the coast

- **Diani** is south of Mombasa, across the **Likoni ferry** or the longer
  Dongo Kundu bypass.
- **Watamu, Kilifi and Malindi** are north, along the coast road.
- **Malaria** is present at the coast; ask a travel clinic about prevention.
