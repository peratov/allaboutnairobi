**Lake Nakuru National Park** is one of Kenya's best places to see
**rhinos**, both black and white, in a compact park around a Rift Valley
lake. It is about **160 kilometres** north-west of Nairobi - close enough
for a long day trip, better as an overnight.

## What you'll see

- **Rhinos** - the park is a fenced rhino sanctuary, and sightings are
  likely.
- **Rothschild's giraffes**, buffalo, zebras, waterbuck and, with luck,
  lions and leopards.
- **Birds** - hundreds of species. The famous **flamingos** come and go
  with lake conditions; numbers have fallen as the lake has risen, so don't
  count on huge flocks.
- **Viewpoints** - Baboon Cliff and Out of Africa lookout give views over
  the lake.

## Getting there

- **By road** - about **three hours** from Nairobi on the A104 through the
  Rift Valley, with views over the escarpment. Traffic and roadworks can add
  time. See [Driving in Nairobi](/guides/driving-in-nairobi).
- **By shuttle or bus** to Nakuru town, then a taxi or tour vehicle into
  the park.
- **On a tour** - many Nairobi operators run day and overnight trips, often
  combined with Lake Naivasha. See
  [Choosing a safari company](/guides/choosing-a-safari-company).

## Park fees

The park is run by the **Kenya Wildlife Service** and is one of its
higher-priced parks. Fees are lower for Kenyan citizens and residents, and
payment is **cashless** - pay online or by [[M-Pesa]] through KWS's
official payment system before or at the gate. Fees have changed recently,
so check the current rates. See
[Visiting Kenya's national parks](/guides/visiting-kenyas-national-parks).

## Self-drive or tour

- **Self-drive** works - the main tracks are passable in a normal car in the
  dry season, though a higher vehicle is better and some tracks need 4x4
  after rain. See [Car hire in Nairobi](/guides/car-hire-in-nairobi).
- **A tour or guide** helps you find rhinos and leopards and handles fees.

## Where to stay

Lodges and camps inside the park, and hotels in **Nakuru town**, cover all
budgets. Staying overnight lets you drive at dawn, the best time for
animals.

## When to go

Year-round. The **dry seasons** (roughly June to October and January to
February) make tracks easier. See [Weather in Nairobi](/guides/weather-in-nairobi).
