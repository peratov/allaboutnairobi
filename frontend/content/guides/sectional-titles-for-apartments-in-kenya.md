Buying a flat in Nairobi used to mean buying a **long lease** - often 99 or
999 years - from the developer, while the developer or a management company
kept the title to the land. The **Sectional Properties Act, 2020** replaced
that with **sectional titles**: each flat gets a title of its own. This is
general information, not legal advice.

## What a sectional title gives you

- **A title to your unit**, registered in your name, showing its boundaries
  on a registered **sectional plan**.
- **A share of the common property** - the land, stairs, lifts, parking,
  gardens and pool - in proportion to your unit's entitlement.
- **Membership of the corporation** that runs the building.

It can be mortgaged, sold and inherited like any other registered land, and a
search on [Ardhisasa](/guides/land-search-on-ardhisasa) shows who owns it and
what is charged against it.

## The owners' corporation

Once a sectional plan is registered, all the unit owners together form a
**corporation**. It owns and maintains the common property, insures the
building, sets the **service charge**, and makes **by-laws** - on pets,
renovations, parking, short lets and noise. A managing agent can run the day
to day, but answers to the owners.

Before you buy, ask for:

- the **by-laws** - check they allow what you plan, such as
  [Airbnb hosting](/guides/airbnb-hosting-in-nairobi)
- the last **accounts and budget**, and whether there is a **sinking fund**
  for big repairs like lifts and roofs
- the **service charge** history and any **arrears** on the unit you are buying

## Old leases and conversion

The Act required existing long leases on flats to be **converted** to
sectional titles, and set a window that ended in December 2022. In practice
many blocks have still not converted, because conversion needs the developer
or management company, the mother title and a surveyed sectional plan.

If you are buying in an older block, ask:

- Has the block **converted**? If not, what is holding it up?
- Is the **mother title** clean - no charges, caveats or disputes?
- Who holds the **reversion** and the management company shares that came
  with the old lease?

Your advocate should check all of this before you sign. See
[Buying property in Kenya](/guides/buying-property-in-kenya).

## Off-plan flats

When you buy [off-plan](/guides/buying-off-plan-in-kenya), you get a title
only after the building is finished and the sectional plan registered. The
sale agreement should say when, and who pays for it.
