**Lamu** is a slow, car-free island on Kenya's northern coast, with one of
East Africa's best-preserved **Swahili towns** - a UNESCO World Heritage
Site. Donkeys and boats replace cars, and the pace is unhurried. It is a
world away from Nairobi, but only a short flight.

## Getting there

- **Fly** - daily flights from Nairobi (JKIA and Wilson Airport) to
  **Manda Island**, about an hour and a half. A short **boat ride** crosses
  to Lamu town or Shela. See [JKIA airport guide](/guides/jkia-airport-guide).
- **By road** - buses run from Mombasa and Malindi to Mokowe on the
  mainland, then a boat. It is a long journey; check current travel advice
  for the route before going by road.

Flying is the usual choice.

## What to do

- **Lamu Old Town** - narrow lanes, carved wooden doors, mosques, the old
  fort and the Lamu Museum. Walk, get lost and look up.
- **Shela** - a quieter village with a long sandy beach and dunes, a walk
  or boat ride from town.
- **Dhow trips** - sailing, snorkelling and sunset trips, with seafood
  grilled on board or on a beach.
- **Nearby islands** - Manda, Kiwayu and the ruins at **Takwa**.
- **Lamu Cultural Festival** - usually in **November**, with dhow races,
  donkey races, music and crafts.

## Where to stay

Guest houses and boutique hotels in old Swahili houses in **Lamu town** and
**Shela**, from simple to luxurious. Book ahead for holidays and the
festival.

## Respecting local customs

- Lamu is a **conservative Muslim** community. Dress modestly in town -
  shoulders and knees covered. Swimwear is for the beach.
- **Ramadan** - eat and drink discreetly in public during the day.
- Ask before photographing people.
- Alcohol is available in some hotels and restaurants, not everywhere.

See [Culture and etiquette in Kenya](/guides/culture-and-etiquette-in-kenya).

## Practical tips

- **Cash** - carry some, as not everywhere takes cards; [[M-Pesa]] is
  widely accepted.
- **Boatmen and guides** - agree prices before setting off, and use
  recommended operators.
- **Heat** - it is hot and humid year-round; bring sun protection.
- **Safety** - Lamu island is generally calm for visitors. Check your
  government's travel advice for parts of Lamu County near the Somali
  border.
