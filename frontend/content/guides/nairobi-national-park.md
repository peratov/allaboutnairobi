Nairobi National Park is the only national park in the world on the edge of a
capital city. Lions, rhinos, giraffes and buffalo roam in front of the city
skyline, a short drive from the centre. It is on the
[map](/map#place/nairobi-national-park).

## Fees

The daily conservation fee for an adult in 2026 is:

- **Non-resident:** US$ {{ NNP_FEE_NONRESIDENT_USD }}.
- **Kenyan and East African citizens:** KES {{ NNP_FEE_CITIZEN|shillings }}.
- **Kenya residents** (with a permit or foreigner certificate): a shilling rate
  between the two.

Children and students pay less, and young children are free. Vehicles pay an
extra entry fee. Check the current schedule on the Kenya Wildlife Service
(KWS) website.

## Paying

The park is **cashless**. Pay through **KWSPay** on [[eCitizen]] before you
arrive, or at the gate by card or M-Pesa. Bring your passport, ID or resident
card - the rate you pay depends on it.

## Gates and hours

- The **Main Gate** is on Lang'ata Road, next to the KWS headquarters, about 20
  to 30 minutes from the CBD outside rush hour.
- The park opens around **6am** and closes around **6pm**. You must be out by
  closing.
- **Early morning** is best for predators, and cooler.

## How to visit

- **Self-drive.** You can drive your own or a hired car. Many tracks are fine
  in an ordinary car in the dry season; after rain, a high-clearance vehicle or
  4x4 is much better. Stay in your vehicle except at marked picnic and walking
  sites.
- **A half-day tour.** Many operators run morning and afternoon game drives
  with a guide and a pop-up-roof vehicle, often with hotel pickup. Compare what
  the price includes - park fees are often extra.
- **KWS vehicles** - KWS also runs its own shuttle tours; ask at the Main Gate.

## What you can see

Lions, cheetahs, black rhinos (the park is an important rhino sanctuary),
giraffes, zebras, buffalo, hippos, crocodiles, many antelope and over 400 bird
species. There are no elephants.

## Nearby

Combine a visit with the **Sheldrick Wildlife Trust** elephant orphanage
(pre-booked, late morning), the **Nairobi Safari Walk** and **animal
orphanage** at the Main Gate, or the **Giraffe Centre** in Karen. See
[Things to do in Nairobi](/guides/things-to-do-in-nairobi).

## Tips

- **Binoculars** and a long camera lens make a big difference.
- **Don't feed or approach animals**, even monkeys at the picnic sites.
- **Carry water and snacks**; there is little inside the park.
- **Ask other drivers** what they have seen - it is how many people find lions.
