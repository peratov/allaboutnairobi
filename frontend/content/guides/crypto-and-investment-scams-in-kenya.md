Every few years a scheme promising easy returns sweeps through Kenyan
WhatsApp groups, churches and offices, and then stops paying. The names
change; the pattern does not. This page is about recognising it before you
pay in.

## The warning signs

- **Guaranteed returns**, especially high ones paid daily or weekly.
  Real investments go down as well as up, and say so.
- **Pay for recruiting.** Bonuses for bringing in friends mean new members'
  money pays the old ones - a pyramid.
- **Urgency** - "the window closes Friday", "only 20 slots left".
- **Withdrawals that need a fee**, a "tax" or an upgrade first.
- **No licence you can check**, or a licence number that leads nowhere.
- **Contact only by WhatsApp or Telegram**, with an "account manager" who
  will not speak on the phone.

## Common forms in Kenya

- **Crypto "investment" and mining apps** that show a growing balance you can
  never fully withdraw.
- **Forex and crypto trading platforms** run by unlicensed "brokers", often
  with a mentor who trades "for" you.
- **Signal groups** on Telegram selling trading tips or "copy trading".
- **Romance and friendship scams** that move slowly to "an opportunity" -
  sometimes called pig-butchering.
- **Fake celebrity endorsements** on Facebook and YouTube.
- **Recovery scams** that contact victims offering, for a fee, to get the
  money back. They are the same people, or worse.

## How to check

- Search the **Capital Markets Authority**'s list of licensed firms, and its
  **investor alerts**, which name unlicensed schemes. Forex brokers and crypto
  exchanges must be licensed; see [Crypto in Kenya](/guides/crypto-in-kenya).
- For deposit-taking, check the **Central Bank of Kenya** (banks and
  microfinance) or **SASRA** (SACCOs).
- Ask: **where does the return come from?** If nobody can explain it, it is
  coming from the next investor.
- Compare with the boring options - [money market funds](/guides/money-market-funds-in-kenya)
  and [Treasury bills](/guides/buying-treasury-bills-and-bonds) - which
  show you what a real, regulated return looks like.

## If you have been caught

1. **Stop paying** - including "fees" to release your money.
2. **Save everything**: chats, numbers, M-Pesa messages, wallet addresses,
   screenshots.
3. **Report it** to the police and the DCI, and to the CMA if it posed as an
   investment. See [Reporting a crime](/guides/reporting-a-crime-and-lost-documents).
4. If you paid by M-Pesa or bank very recently, **call them at once** - a
   transfer can sometimes be stopped.
5. **Ignore anyone offering recovery for a fee.**
