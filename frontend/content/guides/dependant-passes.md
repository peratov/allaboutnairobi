A **dependant's pass** lets a foreign spouse, child or other dependant live in
Kenya with someone who has the right to be here - a work permit holder, a
permanent resident, an exempted person, or a Kenyan citizen.

## Who can get one

The dependant of:

- **A work permit holder** - for example a spouse or children coming with
  someone on a [Class D permit](/guides/class-d-work-permit).
- **A permanent resident** or **an exempted person** (such as some diplomats
  and international staff, whose arrangements are often handled separately by
  their organisation).
- **A Kenyan citizen** - a foreign spouse or child.

## Fees

- **Dependants of work permit and permanent residence holders:**
  KES {{ DEPENDANT_PASS_FEE|shillings }} issuance fee.
- **Dependants of Kenyan citizens:** KES
  {{ DEPENDANT_PASS_FEE_CITIZEN|shillings }}.
- **Citizens of East African Community states:** free.

Check the current fees on the Directorate of Immigration Services' site before
paying. Employers often pay for employees' families.

## Documents

Applications are made on the **eFNS** (Foreign Nationals Services) portal with
**Form 28**. You normally need:

- **A cover letter** to the Director General of Immigration.
- **Passports** (and ID for Kenyan citizens) of the applicant and the
  dependant, and both people's current immigration status.
- **Two passport photos** of the dependant.
- **Proof of relationship** - a certified **marriage certificate** for a spouse,
  **birth certificates** for children.
- **Proof of income** to support the dependant.
- **The principal's work permit**, with at least six months left.
- **An affidavit or evidence of dependency** where asked, for example for
  older children or dependent relatives.

## Timing

Apply as soon as the principal permit is approved, or together with it. Plan
for several weeks. A dependant's pass usually runs for the same period as the
principal permit and must be renewed with it.

## After approval

The pass is endorsed in the passport. Adults then **register as foreign
nationals** and get a **foreigner certificate** (alien ID), needed for a bank
account, SIM cards and more. See
[eCitizen, KRA PIN and IDs](/guides/ecitizen-kra-pin-and-ids).

## Can a dependant work?

**No - not on a dependant's pass alone.** A spouse who wants to work needs
their own work permit, or permission from Immigration. Volunteering can also
need the right status. See [Volunteering in Nairobi](/guides/volunteering-in-nairobi).

## Children

- Children need passes before they start school; international schools will
  ask. See [International schools](/guides/international-schools-in-nairobi).
- **Babies born in Kenya** to foreign parents need their own passport and
  pass. See [Giving birth in Nairobi](/guides/giving-birth-in-nairobi).
