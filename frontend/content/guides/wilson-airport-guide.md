**Wilson Airport**, off Langata Road about five kilometres from the CBD, is
Nairobi's busy second airport. It handles **light aircraft**, **safari
flights** to the parks and reserves, some **domestic** routes, charters and
flying schools. Most big domestic and international airlines use **JKIA**
instead - see [JKIA airport guide](/guides/jkia-airport-guide).

## Where you can fly

Scheduled airlines from Wilson fly to:

- **Safari destinations** - the **Maasai Mara** (well under an hour),
  Amboseli, Samburu, Laikipia and other airstrips. See
  [Maasai Mara from Nairobi](/guides/maasai-mara-from-nairobi).
- **The coast and islands** - including **Lamu** and Malindi. See
  [Lamu from Nairobi](/guides/lamu-from-nairobi).
- **Other towns** such as Nanyuki, depending on the airline.

Several airlines operate here, each with its **own terminal or office**.
Check which one your flight leaves from.

## Luggage

- Safari and light-aircraft flights usually allow about **15 kg per person,
  including hand luggage**.
- Bags must be **soft** - duffel bags, not hard suitcases - so they fit in
  small planes.
- Some airlines can **store extra luggage** at Wilson while you're on
  safari; ask when booking.
- Extra weight may be possible for an extra seat or fee, if booked ahead.

## Getting there

- **Ride-hailing or taxi** - the easiest way, about 20-40 minutes from the
  CBD or Westlands depending on traffic. Tell the driver which airline
  terminal you need.
- **Driving** - there's paid parking. See [Parking in Nairobi](/guides/parking-in-nairobi).
- **From JKIA** - allow plenty of time for the transfer by road.

## Check-in

- Arrive **about an hour before** your flight; small planes leave on time
  and may not wait.
- Bring your **ID or passport** - domestic flights still check ID.
- Security is simpler than at JKIA, but bags are screened.
- Facilities are basic - a café or two and seating at each terminal.

## Tips

- **Pack light** and bring layers - small planes can be cool, and the parks
  are cold at dawn.
- **Flights may stop** at several airstrips on the way.
- **Weather** can delay flights in the rainy season.
- Book **park fees and lodges** to match your flight. See
  [Visiting Kenya's national parks](/guides/visiting-kenyas-national-parks).
