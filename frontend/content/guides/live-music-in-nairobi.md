Nairobi is one of East Africa's music capitals. On any weekend you can find
a live band playing old-school rhumba, a club full of the newest Kenyan hits,
an outdoor festival, or jazz in a garden.

## Kenyan sounds to know

- **Benga** - fast, guitar-driven music from western Kenya, the sound of
  Kenyan pop for decades.
- **Rhumba** - Congolese-influenced dance music, hugely popular with
  older crowds and live bands.
- **Ohangla** - Luo music built on drums, with a strong live tradition.
- **Mugithi** - Kikuyu one-man-guitar music, played in bars late into the
  night.
- **Taarab** - Swahili coast music with strings and poetry.
- **Genge, gengetone and newer street genres** - Nairobi's own urban
  sounds, in Sheng, and the soundtrack of matatus and clubs.
- **Gospel** - one of Kenya's biggest-selling genres, with big concerts.
- Plus **afrobeats**, **amapiano**, **hip hop**, **reggae** and **jazz**,
  all with strong local scenes.

## Where to hear it

- **Bars and restaurants with live bands** - many have a band night each
  week, especially for rhumba, benga and covers.
- **Clubs** in Westlands, Kilimani and along the main roads, with DJs and
  live performances. See [Nightlife in Nairobi](/guides/nightlife-in-nairobi).
- **Garden and lounge venues** for jazz and acoustic sets, often on Sunday
  afternoons.
- **Theatres and cultural venues** - the **Kenya National Theatre** and the
  **Kenya Cultural Centre** next to it, and the **Alliance Française** and
  **Goethe-Institut**, for concerts, plays and festivals.
- **Churches and stadiums** for big gospel events.

## Festivals and big shows

Nairobi hosts outdoor music festivals and concerts through the year -
picnic-style afternoon festivals, international artists at the big venues,
and Kenyan stars' own shows, especially around December. Most sell tickets
online, often payable by M-Pesa. See the [events calendar](/events).

## Tips

- **Buy from official ticket sellers**; fake tickets are sold on social
  media.
- Concerts often **start late** - the headliner may not appear until
  after midnight.
- **Plan your ride home** with ride-hailing. See
  [Ride-hailing and delivery apps](/guides/ride-hailing-and-delivery-apps-in-nairobi).
- Outdoor festivals can be cold after dark: bring a jacket.
