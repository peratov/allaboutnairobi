Your **KCSE** certificate is asked for again and again - for university and
college, jobs, the police, the military and many government services. The
**Kenya National Examinations Council** (KNEC) runs the exams and issues the
certificates.

## Getting your results

- KCSE results are usually released early in the following year.
- Results can be checked by **SMS** and on **KNEC's online portal** using
  your index number; the details are announced with the results.
- Your **school** receives the official **result slips**.

Next steps after results are in
[Applying to university](/guides/applying-to-university-in-kenya) and
[TVET colleges and trades](/guides/tvet-colleges-and-learning-a-trade).

## Collecting your certificate

Certificates reach schools some months after the results. **Collect yours
from the school where you sat the exam**, with your ID. If the school is
holding it back, ask the county education office for help.

## Keeping it safe

- Keep the original somewhere dry and secure.
- Make **certified copies** for applications, and never hand over the
  original unless it is required to be seen.
- Scan it and store the scan safely.

## Verifying certificates

Employers and institutions can verify certificates with KNEC. Fake
certificates are a crime and are increasingly caught - many public bodies
now check them.

## Lost or damaged certificates

KNEC does not simply print a new certificate. If yours is lost or destroyed,
apply through KNEC's online system for the replacement or **certification
of results** it offers:

1. **Report the loss** to the police and get an abstract. See
   [Reporting a crime and lost documents](/guides/reporting-a-crime-and-lost-documents).
2. **Apply online** on KNEC's portal, with your index number, exam year,
   school and ID.
3. **Pay the fee** shown on the portal, through the official [[paybill]]
   or bank account only.
4. **Collect or download** the document when it is ready.

Rules and fees have changed recently, so follow KNEC's current instructions.
Use only KNEC's official site - not agents who promise faster results.

## KCPE and the new CBC assessments

The same applies to **KCPE** certificates. For the newer CBC assessments,
see [Senior school and Grade 10 placement](/guides/senior-school-and-grade-10-placement).
