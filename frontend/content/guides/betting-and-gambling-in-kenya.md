Sports betting is hugely popular in Kenya, especially among young people,
and advertised everywhere. Knowing the rules, the costs and the risks helps
you stay in control - or decide not to bet at all.

## The law

- Betting is legal only with **licensed** operators. The national gambling
  regulator licenses betting firms; the old Betting Control and Licensing
  Board is being replaced by a new Gambling Regulatory Authority.
- You must be **18 or older** to bet.
- **Unlicensed** sites and apps give you no protection if they refuse to
  pay.

## Taxes

The government taxes betting in two ways:

- **Excise duty** on the amount you stake or move into your betting
  account.
- **Withholding tax** on winnings, deducted before you are paid.

The rates have changed several times recently in the annual Finance Acts.
The effect is that you get back less than the advertised odds suggest.

## The real odds

- Betting firms set odds so that, **over time, they win** and most
  bettors lose.
- "Sure bets", tipsters and paid predictions don't change this - many are
  scams.
- Jackpots are designed to be very hard to win.

## Warning signs

- Betting more than you can afford, or **borrowing** to bet - including
  [mobile loans](/guides/mobile-loans-true-cost).
- **Chasing losses** - betting more to win back what you lost.
- Hiding betting from family, or missing rent, fees or food.
- Feeling restless or irritable when not betting.

## Staying in control

- **Set a limit** for the month and stop when it's gone.
- **Never borrow** to bet, and keep betting money separate from rent and
  bills.
- **Use account limits** and self-exclusion tools on betting apps.
- **Take breaks** and don't bet when upset or drunk.

## Getting help

- **Talk to someone** - a friend, family member or religious leader.
- **Counselling** - see [Therapy and mental health](/guides/therapy-and-mental-health-in-nairobi).
- **Debt** - see [Checking your CRB status](/guides/checking-your-crb-status)
  and speak to lenders early.
- **Block betting sites** and delete apps; ask your bank about blocking
  gambling payments.
