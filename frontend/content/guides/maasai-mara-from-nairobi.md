The Maasai Mara is Kenya's most famous reserve, and the most popular safari
from Nairobi. You can drive there in a day or fly in under an hour.

## Road or air?

**By road.** Around five to six hours from Nairobi, via the Rift Valley
escarpment and Narok. The final stretch to the reserve gates can be rough.
Most budget and mid-range safaris go by road in a 4x4 or safari minivan,
typically for three days and two nights.

**By air.** Scheduled light aircraft fly several times a day from **Wilson
Airport** in Nairobi to airstrips inside and around the reserve, in about 45
to 75 minutes. Lodges meet you at the airstrip. Flying costs more, but saves a
long day each way - worth it for short trips. Check the baggage limit, which is
usually small and requires soft bags.

## Reserve fees

The Maasai Mara National Reserve is run by Narok County. In 2026, the daily
fee for a non-resident adult is:

- **US$ {{ MARA_FEE_LOW_USD }}** from 1 January to 30 June.
- **US$ {{ MARA_FEE_PEAK_USD }}** from 1 July to 31 December.

Citizens, residents and children pay lower rates. A ticket covers one
12-hour day, so a three-day safari means three days of fees. Most packages
include fees, but always check.

**Conservancies** bordering the reserve, such as Olare Motorogi and Naboisho,
charge their own fees and limit vehicle numbers, so game drives are quieter.

## When to go

- **July to October** - the **Great Migration**, when huge herds of wildebeest
  and zebra arrive from Tanzania and cross the Mara River. The busiest and most
  expensive time.
- **January to March** - dry, good game viewing, fewer vehicles.
- **April and May** - the long rains. Some camps close; prices drop.

Resident predators, elephants and plains game are there all year.

## Choosing a package

- **Group joining safaris** - cheapest, shared minivan and budget camps.
- **Private road safaris** - your own vehicle and driver-guide, flexible.
- **Fly-in lodge stays** - lodge or camp with game drives included.

Ask every operator:

- Is the reserve fee included, and for how many days?
- What kind of vehicle - a pop-up-roof minivan or a 4x4 land cruiser?
- Is it a private vehicle or shared, and how many people per vehicle?
- What meals and drinks are included?

Choose a company that is a member of the **Kenya Association of Tour
Operators** (KATO), and pay by card or bank transfer to the company, not to an
individual.

## What to pack

Neutral-coloured clothes, a warm layer for early mornings, a hat and
sunscreen, binoculars, and a camera. Soft bags if you are flying.
