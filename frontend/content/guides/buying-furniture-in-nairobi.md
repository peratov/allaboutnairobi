Furnishing a home in Nairobi can be cheap or very expensive, depending on
where you buy. Much of the city's furniture is made locally, often to order,
and you can save a lot by going to the makers directly.

## Where to buy

- **Roadside workshops** - along Ngong Road, Thika Road, Waiyaki Way and
  in many estates, carpenters display sofas, beds, tables and wardrobes on
  the roadside and make to order. Good prices, and you can choose the size,
  fabric and finish.
- **Gikomba** - a huge area of furniture makers and timber yards, among the
  cheapest places in the city, with a wide range of quality. See
  [Markets in Nairobi](/guides/markets-in-nairobi).
- **Showrooms and furniture shops** - along the main roads and in malls,
  with ready-made, imported and higher-end local pieces, often with delivery.
- **Supermarkets and homeware chains** - mattresses, office chairs, plastic
  and flat-pack furniture.
- **Online** - marketplaces and makers' social media pages.
- **Second-hand** - people leaving Kenya, especially expatriates, sell
  whole households through online groups. See [Leaving Kenya](/guides/leaving-kenya).

## Ordering from a maker

1. **Show a picture** or drawing, and agree exact **measurements** - measure
   your rooms and doors first.
2. **Agree the materials**: the type of **wood** (hardwood such as mahogany,
   mvule or cypress, or cheaper softwood and board), the **foam density**
   for sofas, the fabric and the finish.
3. **Agree the price and the delivery date in writing**, including delivery
   to your door.
4. **Pay a deposit**, not the full amount, and the rest on delivery.
5. **Visit during making** if you can, to check the frame and materials
   before it is covered.

## Checking quality

- **Frames**: solid, dry wood, joined properly - not nailed board that will
  loosen. Wet wood can crack or warp.
- **Sofas**: sit on them; good foam springs back. Ask what is inside.
- **Finish**: even, smooth, no sharp edges, and drawers and doors that run
  smoothly.
- **Mattresses**: check the density and the brand's standard mark, and lie
  on it.
- **Second-hand**: check for termites, damp and bed bugs.

## Getting it home

Most sellers deliver for a fee by pickup or lorry. Check it fits through
your doors and up the stairs, and that the movers protect it. See
[Moving house in Nairobi](/guides/moving-house-in-nairobi).
