Nairobi is the hub for travel around Kenya. Almost every trip starts here, by
plane, train, bus or car.

## Flying

- **From JKIA** - Kenya Airways and its low-cost sister airline **Jambojet**
  fly to Mombasa, Kisumu, Eldoret, Malindi, Ukunda (Diani) and other towns.
- **From [Wilson Airport](/map#place/wilson-airport)** - smaller airlines such
  as Safarilink and AirKenya fly light aircraft to the safari airstrips, the
  Maasai Mara and the coast.

Luggage limits on light aircraft are strict and often soft bags only. Book
early for holidays.

## The SGR train

The **Madaraka Express** runs between the Nairobi Terminus near Syokimau and
Mombasa, in about five to six hours. It is comfortable and cheap, and sells
out at holidays. Book on Kenya Railways' website with the ID you will travel
with. See [Getting around Nairobi](/guides/getting-around-nairobi#trains).

## Long-distance buses and shuttles

Coach companies such as Easy Coach and Modern Coast run to towns across Kenya
and to Kampala. Shared shuttles run to towns in the highlands and Rift Valley.
Buses are the cheapest option; travel by day where you can.

## Driving

Driving gives the most freedom, especially for the parks. The main highways
are busy with lorries, so allow time and avoid driving at night. See
[Driving in Nairobi](/guides/driving-in-nairobi). Car hire, with or without a
driver, is widely available.

## National parks and reserves

- Parks run by the **Kenya Wildlife Service** take entry fees online and by
  card or M-Pesa, not cash.
- The **Maasai Mara** and some other reserves and conservancies are run
  separately and charge their own fees.
- Book lodges and camps well ahead for July to October, the busiest season.

## Health

Malaria is a risk at the coast, around Lake Victoria and in much of the
lowlands, unlike in Nairobi. See a travel clinic before you go. See
[Healthcare in Nairobi](/guides/healthcare-in-nairobi#staying-healthy).

## Popular trips

- **The coast** - Mombasa, Diani, Watamu, Malindi and Lamu
- **The Maasai Mara** - for the wildlife, and the migration from about July to
  October
- **Amboseli** - elephants beneath Kilimanjaro
- **Lake Naivasha and Hell's Gate** - see [Day trips](/guides/day-trips-from-nairobi)
- **Mount Kenya and Nanyuki** - hiking, and conservancies such as Ol Pejeta
