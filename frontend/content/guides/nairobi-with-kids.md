Nairobi is an easy city to raise children in, if you plan around the traffic.
There is wildlife on the doorstep, a great deal of green space, and a strong
culture of family weekends outdoors.

## Places children love

All on the [map](/map):

- **[Giraffe Centre](/map#place/giraffe-centre)** - feeding giraffes face to
  face.
- **[Sheldrick Wildlife Trust](/map#place/sheldrick-wildlife-trust)** - baby
  elephants at feeding time. Book ahead.
- **[Nairobi National Park](/map#place/nairobi-national-park)** - a game drive
  before breakfast, and back for lunch. The park also has an animal orphanage
  and the Nairobi Safari Walk near the main gate.
- **[Karura Forest](/map#place/karura-forest)** - trails, bikes, a waterfall and
  caves.
- **[Bomas of Kenya](/map#place/bomas-of-kenya)** - traditional homesteads and
  dancing.
- **[Nairobi National Museum](/map#place/nairobi-national-museum)** and the
  Snake Park.

Many malls have play areas, and weekend markets and garden restaurants often
have space for children to run around.

## Childcare

- **Nannies and house managers** are common. See
  [Hiring household staff](/guides/hiring-household-staff) - and remember you
  are an employer, with the legal duties that brings.
- **Daycares and kindergartens** range widely in price and quality. Visit,
  ask about staff ratios and safety, and talk to other parents.
- **Schools** - see [Schools in Nairobi](/guides/schools-in-nairobi).

## Health

- Register with a **paediatrician** early. Gertrude's Children's Hospital
  specialises in children, and the large private hospitals have paediatric
  departments. See [Healthcare in Nairobi](/guides/healthcare-in-nairobi).
- Keep **vaccinations** up to date, and ask a doctor about malaria prevention
  before trips outside Nairobi.
- **Sun and water.** Hats and sunscreen at this altitude; bottled or boiled
  water.

## Getting around

- **Car seats** are not standard in taxis or ride-hailing cars. Bring your own,
  or use a ride service that offers them.
- **Traffic** makes long journeys hard with small children. Choose schools and
  activities near home - see [Nairobi's neighbourhoods](/guides/neighbourhoods-of-nairobi).
- **Pavements** are uneven in many areas, so a sturdy pushchair or a carrier
  works better than a light stroller.
