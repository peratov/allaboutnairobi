Nairobi is a good base for weekends away: lakes, mountains, wildlife and the
Indian Ocean are all within a few hours. For trips you can do in a day, see
[Day trips from Nairobi](/guides/day-trips-from-nairobi).

## Lake Naivasha

**About 2 hours by road.** A freshwater lake in the Rift Valley with hippos,
birdlife and lakeside hotels and campsites. Combine a boat ride and
Crescent Island with cycling in **Hell's Gate National Park** or climbing
**Mount Longonot**. Busy on long weekends, so book ahead.

## Nanyuki and Mount Kenya

**About 3 to 4 hours by road**, or a short flight. A town on the equator at the
foot of Mount Kenya, with conservancies such as **Ol Pejeta** (rhinos and a
chimpanzee sanctuary), hiking, and cooler weather. Good for families and
walkers.

## Amboseli

**About 4 to 5 hours by road**, or a short flight. Big herds of elephants,
with **Kilimanjaro** as a backdrop on clear mornings. Best as two nights or
more. See [Choosing a safari company](/guides/choosing-a-safari-company).

## The Maasai Mara

**5 to 6 hours by road, or under an hour by air.** Worth at least two nights.
See [The Maasai Mara from Nairobi](/guides/maasai-mara-from-nairobi).

## Diani

**About an hour's flight to Ukunda**, or the train to Mombasa and a transfer.
White-sand beaches south of Mombasa, with resorts, kitesurfing, snorkelling and
colobus monkeys. See [Nairobi to Mombasa](/guides/nairobi-to-mombasa).

## Watamu and Kilifi

**Fly to Malindi**, or the train to Mombasa and a drive north. Quieter
beaches, a marine park with good snorkelling, and relaxed small hotels.

## Lamu

**A flight to Manda island**, then a boat. An old Swahili town with no cars,
dhows, and a slow pace. Best for three nights or more.

## Closer and quieter

- **Limuru and Tigoni** - tea estates, cottages and cool air, under an hour
  away.
- **Kajiado and the Magadi road** - dramatic landscapes and hot springs.
- **Machakos and Ol Donyo Sabuk** - hills and a national park, a short drive
  east.

## Tips

- **Leave early** on Friday, or late morning on Saturday, to avoid city
  traffic.
- **Book ahead** for long weekends and school holidays - see
  [Public holidays](/guides/public-holidays-in-kenya).
- **Residents get lower park and hotel rates** - carry proof of residence.
- **Malaria** is a risk at the coast and in some lowland parks; ask a travel
  clinic.
