Nairobi hosts AFCON 2027 matches at **three stadiums**: a brand-new 60,000-seat
arena off Ngong Road, the rebuilt national stadium at Kasarani, and the central
Nyayo National Stadium. All three are on the
[map](/map#place/raila-odinga-international-stadium), and the
[matchday planner](/tools/afcon-matchday-planner) works out when to leave for
each.

| Stadium | Seats | Where | Best reached from |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Raila Odinga International Stadium | 60,000 | Jamhuri, off Ngong Road | Kilimani, Lavington, Karen, Lang'ata |
| Moi International Sports Centre, Kasarani | 60,000 | Off Thika Road | Thika Road, Roysambu, Gigiri, Ruaka |
| Nyayo National Stadium | 30,000 | By the CBD, on Mombasa Road | CBD, South B and C, Madaraka, Upper Hill |

## Raila Odinga International Stadium

**The new national showpiece.** Known during construction as **Talanta
Stadium**, at Talanta Sports City, it was renamed after the late statesman
Raila Odinga. Building began in March 2024, carried out by China Road and
Bridge Corporation under the Kenya Defence Forces' engineers, at a reported cost
of about KES 45.85 billion. It was reported to be more than 90% complete in
August 2026.

- **Capacity:** 60,000 - Kenya's largest stadium.
- **Design:** a roof shaped like the shield on Kenya's flag, 52 executive
  suites, solar panels and rainwater harvesting.
- **Where:** at Jamhuri, off **Ngong Road**, near Jamhuri Park showground and
  Ngong Racecourse, about 10 km west of the CBD. See it
  [on the map](/map#place/raila-odinga-international-stadium).
- **Big matches:** it is one of the stadiums in contention for the opening
  match or the final. CAF has not decided yet.

**Getting there**

- **Ngong Road** is the main route, and it is slow at peak times - on matchdays,
  much slower.
- **From Kilimani, Kileleshwa and Lavington** it is a short ride. From **Karen
  and Lang'ata**, use the Southern Bypass or Ngong Road.
- **Matatus** run along Ngong Road from the CBD towards Karen and Ngong.
- **Expect organised drop-off zones** and road closures on matchdays.

## Moi International Sports Centre, Kasarani

**Kenya's home of big football since 1987.** Better known as **Kasarani**, the
stadium closed in February 2026 for major works: a hybrid grass pitch and new
drainage, fully replaced seating and a new canopy, 2,000-lux floodlights, VAR
facilities, big screens, new changing rooms and automated ticketing. The works
take it from about 48,000 seats to **60,000**.

- **Where:** off the **Thika Superhighway**, about 12 km north-east of the CBD,
  near Garden City and Thika Road Mall. See it [on the map](/map#place/kasarani-stadium).
- **History:** it hosted Kenya's matches at CHAN 2024, including the famous
  1-0 win over Morocco and the quarter-final against Madagascar.

**Getting there**

- **Thika Road** is fast off-peak but crawls at rush hour, and the exits jam
  after big matches.
- **Matatus and buses** run constantly along Thika Road from the CBD; many
  drop near the stadium.
- **Allow time for the walk** from drop-off points to the gates.
- **CHAN 2024 lesson:** crowds without tickets forced their way in for Kenya's
  matches, and CAF cut capacity as a penalty. Arrive early, have your ticket
  ready, and keep away from crushes.

## Nyayo National Stadium

**The central venue.** Nyayo sits where **Lang'ata Road, Mombasa Road and
Aerodrome Road** meet, just south of the CBD. It has been renovated with a new
canopy, lighting and track, and hosted CHAN 2024 matches and Kenya's AFCON
2027 qualifier against Eritrea in September 2026.

- **Capacity:** listed at about 30,000 for AFCON 2027.
- **Where:** see it [on the map](/map#place/nyayo-national-stadium).

**Getting there**

- **The easiest to reach** - many fans can walk from parts of the CBD, South B
  and Madaraka, or take a short ride.
- **The Expressway and Mombasa Road** pass right by; the junction is one of
  Nairobi's busiest.
- **Matatus** from the CBD towards Lang'ata Road and Mombasa Road pass the
  stadium.

## On the day

### Before you leave

- **Your ticket** - on your phone and, if you can, a screenshot in case the
  network is jammed. A **Fan ID** too if CAF requires one.
- **ID or passport.**
- **A charged phone** and a small power bank.
- **M-Pesa and some cash** for food and transport.
- **A warm layer** - June and July are Nairobi's coolest months, and evenings
  can drop to around 12°C. See [Weather in Nairobi](/guides/weather-in-nairobi).

### At the gate

- **Arrive early** - two to three hours for a sell-out. Queues and security
  checks take time.
- **Expect searches.** Leave behind anything you would not take through airport
  security; stadiums usually ban glass, bottles, weapons, fireworks, flares and
  large bags. Check the official list before you go.
- **Follow stewards and police**, and don't push at gates.

### Inside

- **Find your seat and exits** as soon as you arrive.
- **Agree a meeting point** with your group in case phones don't work.
- **Keep your phone and wallet** in a front pocket.

### Leaving

- **Wait a little** - the first half-hour after the whistle is the most
  crowded.
- **Walk to a pickup point** away from the stadium before calling a ride.
- **Share your trip** with someone, and check the car and driver match the app.

## Training grounds

Teams will train at nearby grounds - Kasarani's own annex pitches, and other
Nairobi venues. Open training sessions, if any, will be announced by the teams.
