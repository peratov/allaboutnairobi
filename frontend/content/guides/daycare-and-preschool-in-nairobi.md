Working parents in Nairobi use a mix of **nannies**, **daycares** and
**preschools**, often combined. Options are plentiful in the middle-class and
upmarket areas; the best places fill up, so start looking early.

## The options

- **A nanny at home** - the most common arrangement for babies. Flexible and
  often the cheapest for more than one child. See
  [Hiring household staff](/guides/hiring-household-staff).
- **Daycare** - full-day care for babies and toddlers, often from a few months
  old.
- **Playgroups** - a few hours a day for toddlers, focused on play and social
  skills.
- **Preschool (PP1 and PP2)** - for ages four and five under Kenya's
  Competency-Based Education, or nursery and reception at international schools.
  See [Schools in Nairobi](/guides/schools-in-nairobi).

## What it costs

Fees vary enormously - from modest community daycares to international-style
nurseries that charge as much as a school. Ask each for:

- **Monthly or termly fees**, and whether half-days cost less.
- **Registration and deposit** fees.
- **Meals, nappies and materials** - included or extra?
- **Transport**, if offered.
- **Holiday care** - many close during school holidays.

Compare the total with a nanny's pay and statutory deductions using the
[household staff calculator](/tools/household-staff-calculator).

## What to check

- **Registration** - daycares and preschools should be registered with
  **Nairobi City County**, which is responsible for early childhood education
  and childcare facilities.
- **Ratio of carers to children**, especially for babies.
- **Staff training and background checks.**
- **Safety** - secure gates, safe play areas, first aid and a sick-child
  policy.
- **Hygiene** - kitchens, nappy changing and toilets.
- **Daily routine** - naps, meals, outdoor play and reporting to parents.
- **Visit unannounced**, and speak to other parents.

## Tips

- **Choose near home or work** - Nairobi traffic makes long drop-offs hard.
- **Settle children in gradually** over the first week or two.
- **Keep emergency contacts** and your child's health details up to date with
  the provider.
