Kenya uses the same plugs and voltage as the UK. If you are visiting or
moving from elsewhere, check your devices before plugging them in.

## The basics

| | Kenya |
| --- | --- |
| Plug and socket | **Type G** - three rectangular pins, as in the UK |
| Voltage | **240 volts** |
| Frequency | **50 hertz** |

## Will my device work?

Look at the label on the charger or appliance:

- **"100-240V"** - most phone, laptop and camera chargers. These work in
  Kenya with just a **plug adapter**.
- **"110V" or "120V" only** - many appliances from North America and Japan,
  such as hair dryers, kettles and kitchen machines. These need a
  **voltage converter** (step-down transformer), or they will be damaged. It
  is often cheaper to buy a local one.
- **"220-240V"** - appliances from Europe, the UK, much of Africa and Asia.
  These work with a plug adapter.

Adapters are sold in supermarkets, electronics shops and at the airport.

## Power cuts and surges

The power in Nairobi is generally reliable but can go off, and comes back
with a **surge** that can damage electronics.

- Use a **surge protector** or extension with surge protection for
  computers, televisions and routers.
- A **voltage stabiliser** helps protect fridges, freezers and other
  appliances with motors.
- A **UPS** (uninterruptible power supply) keeps a computer or Wi-Fi
  router running through short cuts.

For longer cuts, see [Solar and backup power](/guides/solar-and-backup-power).

## Buying appliances in Nairobi

- Appliances sold locally are made for **240V and Type G** plugs.
- Look for the **KEBS** standardisation mark, which shows a product meets
  Kenyan standards. Cheap, unmarked extensions and adapters are a fire risk.
- Ask about the **warranty**, and keep the receipt.
- Large appliances are sold in supermarkets, electronics chains, brand
  shops in malls and online. See [Shopping in Nairobi](/guides/shopping-in-nairobi).

## Wiring and sockets at home

Have new sockets, wiring or appliances that need a dedicated circuit (such
as an electric shower or cooker) installed by a licensed electrician. See
[Finding a fundi in Nairobi](/guides/finding-a-fundi-in-nairobi).
