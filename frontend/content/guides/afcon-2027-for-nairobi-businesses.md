A month of international football brings tens of thousands of visitors and a
city in party mood. For Nairobi businesses, AFCON 2027 is a real opportunity -
if you prepare early and stay within the rules.

## Who stands to gain

- **Hotels, guesthouses and short-term rentals**, especially near the
  stadiums, in Westlands and near the airport.
- **Restaurants, bars and cafés** showing matches.
- **Transport** - ride-hailing drivers, car hire with drivers, airport
  transfers, and tour operators.
- **Merchandise** - jerseys, flags, scarves and souvenirs.
- **Food vendors** near stadiums and fan zones.
- **Tour and safari operators**, for visitors between matches.
- **Event organisers and venues** hosting watch parties.

## Plan early

- **Accommodation:** list and price early, with clear cancellation terms.
  Register short-term rentals properly. See
  [Airbnb and short-term rentals](/guides/airbnb-hosting-in-nairobi).
- **Staff:** you may need extra hands. See
  [Hiring your first employee](/guides/hiring-your-first-employee).
- **Stock:** order early, but don't overstock merchandise that depends on which
  teams qualify.
- **Payments:** make sure you can take M-Pesa, cards and, for visitors, card
  payments in foreign currencies.

## Licences and permits

- **The county business permit** for premises. See
  [Nairobi business permit](/guides/nairobi-business-permit).
- **Food businesses** need food hygiene licences and medical certificates for
  staff.
- **Liquor licences** for selling alcohol, and events need the right permits
  for noise, crowds and hours.
- **Street vending** is allowed only in designated areas; expect strict
  enforcement around stadiums and fan zones.
- **Transport** operators need the right NTSA licensing and insurance.

## Brand and trademark rules

AFCON's names, logos and trophy are **CAF's trademarks**, and CAF's sponsors
pay for exclusive rights. Tournaments usually have **commercial restriction
zones** around stadiums and official fan zones. Selling **counterfeit official
merchandise** is illegal, and the Anti-Counterfeit Authority seizes fakes.

You can support the team - "Go Harambee Stars!" - and show matches, but don't
present your business as an official AFCON partner or use CAF's logos without
permission.

## Showing matches

Businesses showing matches to customers may need a commercial viewing licence
from the official broadcaster. Check with the broadcaster that holds the rights
in Kenya once announced.

## Tax

Extra income is taxable in the usual way. Issue eTIMS invoices, and remember
the turnover tax or VAT that applies to you. See
[eTIMS explained](/guides/etims-for-small-businesses) and
[Turnover tax vs income tax](/guides/turnover-tax-vs-income-tax).
