Food is one of the most popular small businesses in Nairobi - a kibanda, a
cloud kitchen, home baking, juice, catering or a restaurant. It is also one
of the most regulated, because Nairobi County inspects food premises and can
close them down. Here is what you need.

## The licences and certificates

- **Single Business Permit** - from Nairobi County, renewed every year. See
  [Nairobi business permit](/guides/nairobi-business-permit).
- **Food hygiene licence** - the county's public health licence for the
  premises, after an inspection of the kitchen, storage, water, drainage and
  toilets.
- **Food handler's medical certificate** - every person who handles food
  needs one, renewed regularly. It is issued through the county's system
  after tests at an approved laboratory; buy only through official channels,
  as fake certificates are common.
- **Fire safety certificate** - from the county fire department, especially
  if you cook with gas.
- **KEBS** - packaged food sold in shops needs to meet Kenya Bureau of
  Standards requirements, and most supermarkets ask for its mark.
- **KRA** - a PIN, and the tax that fits your turnover. See
  [Turnover tax vs income tax](/guides/turnover-tax-vs-income-tax) and
  [eTIMS for small businesses](/guides/etims-for-small-businesses).

Check current requirements and fees with Nairobi County before you open -
they change with each Finance Act.

## The kitchen

Inspectors look for:

- clean running water and handwashing points
- a proper kitchen with ventilation and a chimney or hood
- food stored off the floor and covered, with raw and cooked food apart
- a fridge for anything perishable
- clean toilets for staff
- waste bins and drainage
- staff in clean aprons and head covers

## Home and online food

Selling food cooked at home - cakes, meals, snacks on Instagram or WhatsApp -
is common, but the county's rules still apply once you sell to the public.
Delivery apps usually ask for a business permit and hygiene licence before
listing you. See [Ride-hailing and delivery apps](/guides/ride-hailing-and-delivery-apps-in-nairobi).

## Money matters

- **Price for waste** - food spoils; count what you throw away.
- **Gas and power** are big costs. See [Kenya Power tokens](/guides/kenya-power-tokens).
- **Take payments by [[till number]]** to keep records. See
  [M-Pesa and mobile money](/guides/mpesa-and-mobile-money).
- **Buy wholesale** - see [Buying wholesale in Nairobi](/guides/buying-wholesale-in-nairobi)
