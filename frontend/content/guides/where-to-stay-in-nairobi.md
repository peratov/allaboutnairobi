Where you stay in Nairobi matters because of the traffic. A well-chosen area
can save hours each day. Choose by what you plan to do, then by budget.

## At a glance

| Area | Best for | Feel |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Westlands | Business trips, nightlife, first-timers | Busy, modern, lively |
| Karen | Wildlife, families, a quieter stay | Green, spacious, relaxed |
| Kilimani and Lavington | Longer stays, restaurants | Residential, central |
| Gigiri and Runda | UN and embassy visits | Quiet, leafy, upmarket |
| The CBD | Short business visits, budget | Busy by day, quiet at night |
| Near JKIA | Early flights and layovers | Practical |

## Westlands

The most popular choice. Hotels of every level, malls, restaurants, bars and
coworking spaces, and quick access to the Expressway for the airport. Good for
a first visit and business trips. Can be noisy at night. See
[Living in Westlands](/guides/living-in-westlands).

## Karen

On the south-western edge of the city, close to Nairobi National Park, the
Giraffe Centre, the Sheldrick elephant orphanage and the Karen Blixen Museum.
Boutique hotels, lodges and guesthouses in large gardens. Ideal for a wildlife
focus and families; you will need taxis to get anywhere.

## Kilimani and Lavington

Central and residential, with many serviced apartments, cafés and restaurants.
A good base for longer stays. See
[Serviced apartments in Nairobi](/guides/serviced-apartments-in-nairobi).

## Gigiri and Runda

The diplomatic area, home to the UN offices and many embassies, with Village
Market and Two Rivers malls nearby. Quiet and upmarket; a long way from
the south of the city.

## The CBD

Convenient for government offices, some businesses and budget hotels, and
close to the National Museum and KICC. Lively by day; avoid walking around
after dark. See [Is Nairobi safe for tourists?](/guides/is-nairobi-safe-for-tourists).

## Near JKIA

Hotels near the airport, including some with direct access, suit early
departures and layovers. There is little to do nearby. See the
[JKIA airport guide](/guides/jkia-airport-guide).

## Tips

- **Book somewhere with airport transfers** if you land late.
- **Check what "near" means** - distances are short on a map but long in
  traffic.
- **For a safari**, many operators pick up from hotels in all these areas.
- **For stays of a month or more**, a serviced apartment is usually better
  value.
