Billions of shillings in Kenya belong to people who have forgotten about
them or died without telling their families. Dormant bank accounts, unpaid
dividends, insurance payouts, pension benefits and even unclaimed M-Pesa
balances end up with the **Unclaimed Financial Assets Authority** (UFAA).
Searching and claiming is **free**.

## How money becomes unclaimed

When an account or payout has no activity and the owner cannot be traced for
a set period, the bank, insurer, company or fund must hand it to the UFAA.
Common examples:

- **Bank accounts** left dormant after a move, a job change or a death.
- **Shares and dividends** - see [Investing on the NSE](/guides/investing-on-the-nse).
- **Insurance** payouts and matured policies - see
  [Life insurance in Kenya](/guides/life-insurance-in-kenya).
- **Pension** benefits and **salary** left with former employers.
- **Mobile money** balances, refunds and deposits.

## Searching

- Search on the **UFAA website** using your **ID number** or name.
- Ask at a **Huduma Centre**, which offer UFAA services and can search for
  you - including by a deceased relative's ID number.
- Search for parents and grandparents too, if they have died.

## Claiming

1. **Find the asset** in the search.
2. **Fill in the claim form** - for your own assets, the owner's claim form
   with an indemnity; for a deceased person's, the beneficiary claim form.
3. **Attach documents** - your ID, proof of the asset (such as an old bank
   statement or share certificate), and for a deceased owner, the death
   certificate and succession papers. Forms may need to be commissioned -
   see [Affidavits and commissioners for oaths](/guides/affidavits-and-commissioners-for-oaths-in-kenya).
4. **Submit** online through [[eCitizen]] or at a UFAA office or Huduma
   Centre.
5. **Get paid** to your bank account or mobile money.

There is **no deadline** to claim. For a deceased person's assets, you
usually need a grant of representation first - see
[Death and succession in Kenya](/guides/death-and-succession-in-kenya).

## If the money is still with the bank

If an account went quiet only recently, it may still be with the bank as a
**dormant account**. Visit the branch with your ID and ask to reactivate it.

## Avoiding scams

- The UFAA **does not charge** to search or claim.
- Ignore calls or messages saying you have "unclaimed millions" and must pay
  a fee to release them. See [M-Pesa fraud and scams](/guides/mpesa-fraud-and-scams-in-kenya).
- Use only the official UFAA site, eCitizen and Huduma Centres.

## Keeping it from happening

- Keep **next of kin and nominees** updated with banks, insurers and pension
  schemes.
- Tell your family where your accounts and policies are. See
  [Making a will in Kenya](/guides/making-a-will-in-kenya).
- Use or close old accounts.
