Every salaried worker in Kenya sees the **[[Housing Levy]]** on their payslip.
It funds the government's **Affordable Housing Programme**, and **Boma
Yangu** is where you apply for one of its homes.

## The Housing Levy

- **Employees** pay {{ HOUSING_LEVY_RATE|percent }}% of gross pay, and the
  employer pays the same again. It is deducted through payroll.
- It is a **levy**, not a personal savings account. Paying it does not by
  itself entitle you to a house.
- Since late 2024 it is deductible when working out PAYE, so it reduces your
  tax slightly. See your net pay with the
  [salary calculator](/tools/salary-calculator).

## Boma Yangu

**Boma Yangu** ("my home") is the programme's portal, at bomayangu.go.ke.
Through it you register, save towards a deposit, choose a unit in a project,
and apply for allocation.

### Who can apply

- **Kenyan citizens** aged 18 or over, with a national ID.
- Generally **first-time homeowners** in the county where they are applying.

### Step by step

1. **Register** on bomayangu.go.ke with your ID number and phone number.
2. **Save towards a deposit** in your Boma Yangu account, by M-Pesa or bank.
   These are your own savings.
3. **Choose a project and unit type** when projects open for applications.
4. **Reach the deposit** required - commonly around 10% of the unit's price.
   Applicants who have saved the deposit are prioritised.
5. **Allocation** is by ballot and eligibility. Successful applicants are
   notified by SMS and email.
6. **Pay the balance** by the route you choose: a mortgage, a
   **tenant purchase scheme** (monthly payments to the National Housing
   Corporation until you own the unit), or cash.

## Nairobi projects

Nairobi has several large projects, such as those at Mukuru, Starehe, Park Road,
Pangani and Shauri Moyo, with more under construction. Unit types range from
bedsitters to three-bedroom flats, and prices depend on the project and whether
the unit is a "social", "affordable" or "market" unit. Check current projects
and prices on Boma Yangu itself.

## Is it right for you?

- **The wait can be long**, and projects may be delivered late.
- **Location matters** - check the commute from a project before committing.
  See [Nairobi's neighbourhoods](/guides/neighbourhoods-of-nairobi).
- **Compare the monthly cost** with renting and with an ordinary mortgage. See
  the [mortgage affordability calculator](/tools/mortgage-affordability-calculator).

## Scams

Only Boma Yangu allocates these homes. Nobody can sell you a place in the
queue or a guaranteed unit. Pay only into your own Boma Yangu account, never
to an individual or "agent".
