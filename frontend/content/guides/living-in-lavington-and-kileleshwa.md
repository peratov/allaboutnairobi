Lavington and Kileleshwa sit side by side west of the city centre, between
Kilimani and Westlands. Both are leafy and residential, popular with families
and professionals who want space without a long commute. They are on the
[map](/map#constituency/dagoretti-north).

## The difference

- **Kileleshwa** - mostly apartment blocks, many with pools, gyms and
  boreholes. Greener and a little quieter than Kilimani.
- **Lavington** - more houses, townhouses and low-rise compounds, with large
  gardens, plus newer apartment blocks. Quieter and more suburban.

## Rents

Rough asking rents in 2026 listings:

| What | Kileleshwa (KES a month) | Lavington (KES a month) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| One-bedroom flat | 45,000 - 80,000 | 50,000 - 90,000 |
| Two-bedroom flat | 70,000 - 110,000 | 80,000 - 130,000 |
| Three-bedroom flat | 100,000 - 160,000 | 110,000 - 180,000 |
| Townhouse or house | Few | 200,000 - 350,000 |

## Who they suit

- **Families** - space, gardens, and many schools nearby, including Braeburn's
  Gitanga Road campus and several Kenyan-curriculum private schools.
- **Professionals** working in Westlands, Kilimani, Upper Hill or the CBD.
- **Less so** people without a car in Lavington, where walking and matatus are
  limited.

## Getting around

- **To Westlands** - 10 to 30 minutes via James Gichuru Road or Waiyaki Way.
- **To Upper Hill and the CBD** - 15 to 45 minutes.
- **School runs** jam Gitanga, Othaya and James Gichuru roads at peak times.
- **Kileleshwa** has more matatu routes and is easier without a car.

## Safety

Both are generally considered safe residential areas. Most homes are in gated
compounds with [[askari]]s, and many use alarm and response companies. The
usual care with phones and walking at night applies. See
[Safety in Nairobi](/guides/safety-in-nairobi).

## Daily life

- **Shopping:** Lavington Mall and Valley Arcade, with supermarkets and
  cafés; the Kilimani and Westlands malls are close.
- **Health:** close to Nairobi Hospital and the Westlands hospitals.
- **Outdoors:** leafy streets for walking, and Karura and Ngong Road forests a
  short drive away.

## Water

Rationing affects parts of both areas. Ask about boreholes and storage tanks
before signing. See [Utilities in Nairobi](/guides/utilities-in-nairobi#water).
