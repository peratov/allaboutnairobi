Nairobi is not built for bicycles, but cycling is growing - on forest
trails, weekend road and gravel rides out of the city, and among commuters
beating the traffic. The altitude and the hills make it good training.

## Where to ride

- **Karura Forest** - well-kept trails for all levels, with bike hire. The
  easiest place to start. See [Parks and green spaces](/guides/parks-and-green-spaces-in-nairobi).
- **Ngong Road Forest Sanctuary** - forest trails and single track close to
  the city.
- **Weekend road rides** - early-morning group rides head out of the city
  on quieter roads, towards Limuru, Kiambu's tea and coffee farms, the Ngong
  Hills and Magadi Road.
- **Gravel and mountain biking** - around the Ngong Hills, the tea farms of
  Limuru, Kijabe and the Rift Valley escarpment. See
  [Day trips from Nairobi](/guides/day-trips-from-nairobi).

## Group rides and clubs

Cycling clubs and groups organise weekend rides for different speeds,
mountain bike rides, races and charity events. Most organise on social media
and messaging groups. Riding with a group is safer and the best way to learn
the routes.

## Commuting by bike

A few streets have **cycle lanes**, but most riding is on roads shared with
matatus, buses and bodas. If you commute:

- choose **quieter routes** through estates rather than main highways
- ride **early**, before traffic builds
- be **visible** - lights, reflective clothing and a bright helmet
- assume drivers have not seen you
- ask your office about **secure parking** and a shower

## Buying a bike

- **Bike shops** in Westlands, Karen, the CBD and malls sell new road,
  mountain and hybrid bikes, parts and repairs.
- **Second-hand** bikes are sold through cycling groups and online. Check
  the frame, and that it is not stolen.
- **Imported bikes and parts** can be expensive because of shipping and duty.
- A good **lock** and somewhere secure to keep it at home matter: bike theft
  is common.

## Staying safe

- Wear a **helmet** every time.
- Carry water, a phone, a spare tube and some cash or M-Pesa.
- Avoid riding alone on remote roads; tell someone your route.
- Watch for potholes, open drains and speed bumps.
- Nairobi's mornings are cool and the sun is strong by midday - take a light
  layer and sunscreen.
