From **19 June to 17 July 2027**, the **Africa Cup of Nations** - AFCON - comes
to East Africa. Kenya, Uganda and Tanzania co-host the tournament together
under the name **Pamoja**, Kiswahili for "together". It is the first AFCON with
three hosts, and the first in East and Central Africa since Ethiopia in 1976.

Nairobi is at the heart of it, with **three stadiums** and tens of thousands
of visiting fans. This guide pulls everything together; the
[AFCON 2027 hub](/afcon) has a live countdown, the key dates and the planning
tools.

## The tournament at a glance

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Dates | 19 June to 17 July 2027 |
| Teams | 24, including hosts Kenya, Uganda and Tanzania |
| Matches | 52 |
| Stadiums | 10, in 7 cities |
| In Nairobi | Raila Odinga International Stadium, Kasarani and Nyayo |
| Defending champions | Morocco |
| Official name | TotalEnergies CAF Africa Cup of Nations PAMOJA 2027 |

AFCON 2027 is also the last to be played in an odd-numbered year - from 2028,
CAF moves the tournament to a four-year cycle.

## How the tournament works

The 24 teams are drawn into **six groups of four**. The top two in each group
and the four best third-placed teams go through to the **round of 16**, then
quarter-finals, semi-finals, a third-place match and the final. That makes 52
matches in just under a month.

Kenya, Uganda and Tanzania qualify automatically as hosts. The other 21 places
are decided in qualifiers that run until March 2027.

## Nairobi's three stadiums

- **Raila Odinga International Stadium** - a new 60,000-seater at Talanta
  Sports City, off Ngong Road, built for the tournament and a contender for the
  opening match or the final.
- **Moi International Sports Centre, Kasarani** - Kenya's long-time national
  stadium off Thika Road, rebuilt to 60,000 seats.
- **Nyayo National Stadium** - the central venue beside the CBD, with about
  30,000 seats.

Each has its own section - how to get there, what to expect at the gate - in
[Nairobi's AFCON stadiums](/guides/afcon-2027-stadiums-in-nairobi). All three
are on the [map](/map#place/raila-odinga-international-stadium).

## The Harambee Stars

Kenya's national team, the **Harambee Stars**, are in the finals as co-hosts,
coached by South African legend **Benni McCarthy**. Kenya has played at six
AFCONs but has never got past the group stage. Home crowds - and the run to the
quarter-finals at CHAN 2024 - give real hope of changing that. See
[Harambee Stars and AFCON](/guides/harambee-stars-and-afcon).

## What is still to be decided

At the time of writing, CAF has **not** announced:

- which stadiums host the **opening match** and **the final**,
- the **draw** for the finals and the **match schedule**,
- **ticket prices** and when sales open,
- whether fans will need a **Fan ID**, and whether a **joint East African visa**
  will be introduced,
- official **fan zones** in Nairobi.

We update the [hub](/afcon) and these guides as announcements are made. Treat
anything else you read as speculation until CAF confirms it.

## Tickets

Tickets will be sold through **CAF's official channels**. Do not buy from
websites or social media accounts claiming to sell AFCON 2027 tickets before
sales officially open - they are scams. See
[Tickets and Fan ID](/guides/afcon-2027-tickets-and-fan-id).

## Coming from abroad

- **Visas:** most visitors need Kenya's [[eTA]]; most African nationals do not.
  Uganda has announced a visa-fee waiver for fans around the tournament, and
  the three hosts are discussing a single visa. See
  [Visiting Nairobi for AFCON](/guides/visiting-nairobi-for-afcon-2027).
- **Where to stay:** choose by stadium - Kilimani, Lavington and Karen for Raila
  Odinga Stadium, Thika Road and Kasarani for Kasarani, the CBD and South B for
  Nyayo. Book early and with free cancellation.
- **Budget:** the [AFCON trip budget](/tools/afcon-trip-budget) adds up rooms,
  tickets, transport and safari add-ons.

## Getting around on matchdays

Nairobi's traffic is the biggest challenge. Leave early, use ride-hailing or
matatus rather than driving, and expect road closures near the stadiums. The
[matchday planner](/tools/afcon-matchday-planner) tells you when to leave from
where you are staying. See also [Getting around Nairobi](/guides/getting-around-nairobi).

## Lessons from CHAN 2024

Kenya co-hosted the African Nations Championship (CHAN) with Uganda and
Tanzania in August 2025 - a dress rehearsal. On the pitch it went well: Kenya
topped their group, beating Morocco. Off it, crowds without tickets forced
their way into Kasarani, and CAF fined the Football Kenya Federation and cut
the stadium's capacity for Kenya's matches. For AFCON:

- **Only go with a valid ticket.**
- **Arrive early** - hours, not minutes, for big matches.
- **Follow stewards and police**, and keep away from crushes at the gates.
