Under Kenya's **Competency-Based Education (CBE)** system, learners move
from junior school into **senior school** (Grades 10 to 12). The first group
entered senior school in January 2026. Instead of the old KCSE subject mix,
learners now choose a **pathway** in Grade 9. Details are still being
refined, so check announcements from the Ministry of Education and KNEC each
year.

## The KJSEA

At the end of Grade 9, learners sit the **Kenya Junior School Education
Assessment (KJSEA)**, run by the Kenya National Examinations Council (KNEC).
Placement in senior school is based on the KJSEA together with school-based
assessment scores from earlier years (including Grades 6 and 7).

## The three pathways

Every learner takes the **core subjects**: English, Kiswahili (or Kenyan Sign
Language), Mathematics, and Community Service Learning. They then choose
subjects from one pathway:

- **STEM** - sciences, technical subjects and applied sciences.
- **Social Sciences** - languages, humanities and business studies.
- **Arts and Sports Science** - performing and visual arts, and sports.

Each pathway has several **subject combinations**, and not every school
offers every combination.

## How choosing works

In Grade 9, learners - with their parents - choose a pathway, subject
combinations and a list of senior schools through the Ministry's selection
system. Selection opens for a few weeks each year (in 2026 it ran from late
August to mid-September).

Placement then depends on:

- the **pathway and subject combination** chosen,
- the learner's **KJSEA and school-based scores**, and
- **places available** at schools offering that combination.

## Tips for parents

- **Start with your child's strengths and interests**, not a school's
  reputation. The pathway shapes what they can study later.
- **Check the combination** - make sure the schools you choose actually offer
  it.
- **Choose a spread of schools**, including day schools near home.
- **Look at university and college requirements** for courses your child is
  considering.
- **Keep track of deadlines** and use only official Ministry systems; do not
  pay anyone who promises a place.

## Changing pathway

The Ministry has allowed some learners to request a change of pathway or
school after placement, within set windows. Ask the school's head, and follow
official guidance.
