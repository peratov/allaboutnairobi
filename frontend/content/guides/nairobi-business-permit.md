Every business trading from premises in Nairobi needs the county's **Unified
Business Permit (UBP)**, renewed every year. It replaced several separate
county licences with one permit, and the fee depends on the kind and size of
business.

## Who needs it

Shops, offices, restaurants, salons, workshops, clinics, schools and most
other businesses operating from premises in Nairobi City County. Home-based
and online businesses should check with the county whether they need one.

Registration with the Business Registration Service does **not** replace the
permit. See [Registering a business](/guides/registering-a-business-in-kenya).

## What it costs

The fee is set by the county's Finance Act schedule, by activity and size -
for example the number of staff and the floor area. A small shop might pay a
few thousand shillings a year; large businesses pay tens of thousands or more.
There is also a small application fee.

In 2026 the county announced a **waiver of permit fees for new start-ups**.
Check whether you qualify before paying.

## Step by step

1. **Apply online** through the Nairobi City County services portal (linked
   from nairobi.go.ke), where you create an account.
2. **Enter the business details** - name, registration number, KRA PIN,
   activity, the premises (plot number and location), and size.
3. **Get an invoice** calculated for your activity and size.
4. **Pay** by M-Pesa or bank, using the invoice number.
5. **Download and print** the permit. Display it at the premises.

## Renewing

Permits run for the calendar year and must be renewed early in the year. Late
renewal attracts penalties. Enforcement officers carry out checks, and
businesses without a valid permit can be fined or closed.

## Other licences you may need

- **Fire safety certificate** from the county.
- **Food handling and health certificates** for food businesses.
- **Liquor licence** to sell alcohol.
- **Signage and advertising** permits for outdoor signs.
- **Sector licences** from national regulators, for example in health,
  finance or education.

## Tips

- **Keep receipts** and the permit copy with your business records.
- **Pay only through official county channels.** Ask officers for
  identification, and never pay cash on the spot without an official
  receipt.
