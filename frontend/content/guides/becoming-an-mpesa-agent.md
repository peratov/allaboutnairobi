The green-and-white [[M-Pesa]] agent kiosk is one of Nairobi's most common
small businesses. Agents let customers deposit and withdraw cash, and earn a
**commission** on each transaction. It is steady work, but margins are thin
and it needs capital and care.

## Two routes

- **Through a dealer (sub-agent)** - the usual route for one shop. An
  established M-Pesa dealer (a company that runs many outlets) registers you
  under them, provides the agent line and support, and shares the
  commission.
- **Directly with Safaricom** - for registered companies with several
  outlets ready to operate. Safaricom's requirements are stricter, and
  approval takes months.

## What you need

- A **shop or kiosk** in a busy spot - near a [[stage]], market or estate
  shops - and permission to trade there.
- **Documents** - usually your ID, [[KRA PIN]], a
  [Nairobi business permit](/guides/nairobi-business-permit), a
  [certificate of good conduct](/guides/certificate-of-good-conduct), and
  business registration. See [Registering a business](/guides/registering-a-business-in-kenya).
- **Float** - money in two forms: **e-float** on the agent line (to give
  customers when they deposit) and **cash** in the till (for withdrawals).
  You need enough of both to serve customers all day.
- **Security** - a secure kiosk, a safe or lockable drawer, and ideally CCTV.

## Earnings

- Agents earn a **commission** on deposits and withdrawals, set by the
  network and shared with your dealer if you have one.
- Income depends on **volume** - a busy location matters more than anything.
- Many agents add other services - airtime, bill payments, phone accessories
  or a small shop - to make the kiosk pay.

## Running it

- **Check the customer's ID** for withdrawals, as the rules require, and
  keep records in your logbook.
- **Rebalance float** through the bank or your dealer as you run low.
- **Bank cash** regularly rather than holding large sums.

## Fraud and safety

- Never reverse a transaction because a caller asks - **fake reversal
  requests** are a common scam. Confirm on your statement first.
- Beware **fake notes** - check them.
- Don't share your agent PIN, and change it if staff leave.
- Vary when you bank cash, and close before late night if your area is
  quiet.

See [M-Pesa fraud and scams](/guides/mpesa-fraud-and-scams-in-kenya).
