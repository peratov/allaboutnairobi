Your business name, logo, songs, photos, writing and software are
**intellectual property**. In Kenya, trademarks are registered with the
**Kenya Industrial Property Institute** (KIPI), and copyright is
administered by the **Kenya Copyright Board** (KECOBO).

## Business name vs trademark

Registering a **business name or company** with the Business Registration
Service does **not** give you a trademark. Someone else could register your
brand as a trademark. See
[Registering a business in Kenya](/guides/registering-a-business-in-kenya).

## Registering a trademark

A trademark protects a **name, logo or slogan** used for particular goods or
services.

1. **Search** KIPI's records to check nobody has registered something
   similar for the same goods or services.
2. **Apply** online on KIPI's filing system, choosing the right **classes**
   of goods or services, and pay the fees.
3. **Examination** - KIPI checks the application and that the mark is
   distinctive.
4. **Publication** - accepted marks are published in KIPI's journal so
   others can oppose.
5. **Registration** - if no one opposes, the mark is registered. Renew it
   when due to keep it.

Many people use an IP agent or advocate, especially for logos and several
classes. See [Finding a lawyer](/guides/finding-a-lawyer-in-kenya).

## Copyright

- **Copyright is automatic** - it protects original music, writing, art,
  photos, films and software as soon as they are created in a fixed form.
  You don't have to register.
- **Registering with KECOBO** online gives you official evidence of
  ownership and the date, which helps in disputes. It is quick and
  inexpensive.
- Keep **drafts, files and dates** as proof of when you created your work.

## Music and performers

Musicians and performers earn royalties through **collective management
organisations** licensed by KECOBO, which collect fees from broadcasters,
venues and businesses that play music. Register with the right one for your
work.

## If someone copies you

- **Collect evidence** - screenshots, samples, receipts, dates.
- **Ask them to stop** in writing, ideally through an advocate.
- **Report to KECOBO** for copyright piracy, or to KIPI and the courts for
  trademark infringement. The **Anti-Counterfeit Authority** handles
  counterfeit goods.
- Online platforms have **takedown** processes for copied content.

## Using others' work

Don't use others' music, photos or logos without permission or a licence -
including in adverts and social media.
