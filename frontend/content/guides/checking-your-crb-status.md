Kenya has three licensed **credit reference bureaus (CRBs)**: **Metropol**,
**TransUnion** and **Creditinfo**. Banks, SACCOs, mobile lenders and
digital loan apps report to them, and look at your record before lending.
Employers also ask for CRB clearance for some jobs.

## Your credit report

You are entitled to **one free credit report a year from each bureau**. Request
it through the bureau's website, app or USSD code, with your ID number.
Further reports cost a small fee.

The report shows your loans, whether you pay on time, and any **negative
listing** - a default reported by a lender. A **positive** record helps you
borrow more cheaply.

## Why people are listed

- **Unpaid mobile loans**, often small or forgotten - Fuliza, M-Shwari and
  loan apps report defaults.
- **Unpaid bank loans or credit cards.**
- **Guaranteeing** someone else's loan that was not repaid.
- **Errors** - someone else's loan, or a debt already paid.

## Clearing a negative listing

1. **Get your report** and find which lender listed you, and for how much.
2. **Pay the lender**, or agree a settlement in writing, and keep proof.
3. **Ask the lender to update the bureau.** Lenders should update records once
   a debt is paid.
4. **Check the report again** after a few weeks.

Paid-up negative information stays on the record for a period, marked as
settled, but it counts much less against you.

## Disputing an error

If something is wrong, dispute it with the bureau with any evidence, such as
receipts or a letter from the lender. The bureau must investigate with the
lender. If that fails, complain to the Central Bank of Kenya, which licenses
the bureaus.

## CRB clearance certificate

Some employers and public bodies ask for a **clearance certificate**. Apply
on the bureau's website or app with your ID. The fee is about
**KES {{ CRB_CLEARANCE_FEE|shillings }}**. It is issued once there is no
unresolved negative listing. See
[Clearance certificates for jobs](/guides/clearance-certificates-for-jobs).

## Scams

Nobody can "remove" a genuine listing for a fee. Deal only with the bureaus and
the lender.
