Nairobi has Kenya's largest concentration of universities and colleges, public
and private, and students from across East Africa and beyond.

## Universities

Among the best known in and around Nairobi:

- **University of Nairobi** - Kenya's oldest university, beside the CBD
- **Kenyatta University** - on Thika Road
- **Strathmore University** - private, in Madaraka
- **USIU-Africa** - private, off Thika Road
- **Aga Khan University** - health sciences and media
- **Jomo Kenyatta University of Agriculture and Technology (JKUAT)** - in
  Juja, with a Nairobi campus

Many are on the [map](/map#landmarks-education).

## Check accreditation

Universities are accredited by the **Commission for University Education
(CUE)**, which publishes the list of accredited universities and programmes.
Technical and vocational colleges (TVETs) are overseen by the **TVET
Authority**. Check a course is accredited before paying, especially with
smaller private colleges.

## Kenyan students

- **Placement.** Government-sponsored places at public universities and
  colleges are allocated through the **Kenya Universities and Colleges Central
  Placement Service (KUCCPS)**, based on exam results and choices.
- **Funding.** The student-centred funding model combines government
  scholarships and loans, administered with the **Higher Education Loans Board
  (HELB)**, according to need. Apply on time each year.

## Foreign students

International students need a **student pass** from the Directorate of
Immigration Services, applied for with an admission letter from an accredited
institution. The university's international office usually guides you
through it. A student pass does not allow you to work.

## Living as a student

- **Housing.** Halls of residence, private hostels near campuses, and shared
  flats. Areas near the main campuses fill up at the start of the year.
- **Getting around.** Matatus serve every campus, and many universities run
  shuttle buses.
- **Money.** See the [budget planner](/tools/budget-planner) and
  [Cost of living in Nairobi](/guides/cost-of-living-in-nairobi).
