Nairobi is a good city for dogs and cats, with plenty of vets, green space in
the suburbs, and active rescue groups. Bringing a pet into the country takes
planning, so start early.

## Bringing a pet into Kenya

You need an **import permit** from the Directorate of Veterinary Services,
arranged **before** you travel. Applications are made through the Directorate
or a Kenyan embassy, and permits are valid for a limited time for one journey.
Expect to need:

- **A rabies vaccination**, given well before travel and still valid. Follow
  the timing in the permit conditions exactly.
- **A microchip**, recommended and often expected.
- **A veterinary health certificate** from your home country, issued shortly
  before the flight and endorsed by its government vet service.
- Any **parasite treatment** the permit asks for.

Check your airline's rules too: many carry pets only as cargo, and rules on
breeds and crates vary. A pet relocation agent can handle the paperwork.

## Vets

Nairobi has many private veterinary clinics, especially in Westlands,
Kilimani, Karen and Lang'ata, and several run emergency services. Register with
one when you arrive and save its number.

## Keeping pets healthy

- **Rabies vaccination** every year for dogs and cats. Rabies is present in
  Kenya, so keep it up to date.
- **Ticks** are common, especially in the rains. Use tick prevention and check
  dogs after walks.
- **The heat and the altitude** are mild, but give shade and water.

## Adopting

The **Kenya Society for the Protection and Care of Animals (KSPCA)** in Karen,
and several smaller rescue groups, rehome dogs and cats. Adoption usually
includes vaccination and a home check.

## Practicalities

- **Rentals.** Ask before you sign; many landlords and apartment blocks limit
  pets. Get permission in writing.
- **Walking dogs.** Some forests and parks allow dogs on leads - check each
  one's rules before you go. National parks do not allow pets.
- **Security dogs.** Many homes keep dogs as guards. Make sure staff and
  visitors know how to be safe around them.

## Leaving Kenya with a pet

You will need an export health certificate and whatever your destination
requires. See [Leaving Kenya](/guides/leaving-kenya).
