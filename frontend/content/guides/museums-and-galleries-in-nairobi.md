Nairobi's museums tell the story of Kenya - from the earliest humans to
independence - and a lively contemporary art scene shows where it is going.
Most are inexpensive, and many have discounts for citizens and residents.

## Museums

- **[Nairobi National Museum](/map#place/nairobi-national-museum)**, on
  Museum Hill - Kenya's main museum: human origins, wildlife, birds,
  cultures and history, with botanic gardens, a **snake park** next door,
  and temporary exhibitions. Run by the **National Museums of Kenya**.
- **[Nairobi Gallery](/map#place/nairobi-gallery)**, in the old PC's office
  at the edge of the CBD - Kenyan and African art in a historic building.
- **[Kenya National Archives](/map#place/kenya-national-archives)**, on Moi
  Avenue - historical documents, photographs and art, in a former bank
  building.
- **[Nairobi Railway Museum](/map#place/nairobi-railway-museum)** - steam
  locomotives and carriages from the railway that founded the city. See
  [History of Nairobi](/guides/history-of-nairobi).
- **[Karen Blixen Museum](/map#place/karen-blixen-museum)**, in Karen - the
  farmhouse of the Danish writer, with its gardens and views of the Ngong
  Hills.
- **[Bomas of Kenya](/map#place/bomas-of-kenya)**, in Lang'ata - traditional
  homesteads of Kenya's communities and daily **dance performances**.

## Contemporary art

Nairobi has a busy art scene of painters, sculptors and photographers.

- **Commercial galleries** in Westlands, Lavington, Karen and Muthaiga show
  and sell work by Kenyan and East African artists, with regular openings.
- **Artist studios and collectives** open their doors for events and open
  studio days.
- **Arts centres** host exhibitions, performances and workshops, and the
  **cultural centres** - the Alliance Française and the Goethe-Institut -
  run exhibitions too.
- **Art fairs and auctions** of East African art are held through the year.

Gallery openings are usually free and a good way into the scene. Follow
galleries on social media for dates.

## Tips

- **Bring ID**: resident and citizen rates usually need proof.
- **Check opening hours** - some close on certain days or public holidays.
- **Combine visits**: the National Museum and the snake park; the Railway
  Museum, Nairobi Gallery and the Archives in the CBD; the Karen Blixen
  Museum with the Giraffe Centre and the Sheldrick elephant orphanage.
- Many museums have **school and children's** programmes. See
  [Nairobi with kids](/guides/nairobi-with-kids).
