**Ngara** and **Pangani** sit just north of the CBD, between the city centre,
Parklands and Eastleigh. Older streets of shops and flats are being
replaced by new apartment blocks, offering central living at lower rents
than Westlands or Kilimani.

## The areas

- **Ngara** - right next to the CBD, busy and commercial, with markets,
  fabric and hardware shops, old flats and many new apartment towers.
- **Pangani** - a little further north-east, with older estates, new
  government and private housing developments, and quieter streets.

## Who it suits

- **People working in the CBD** who want to walk or take a short ride to
  work.
- **Students** at the city's universities and colleges.
- **People on a middle budget** who want to be central.
- **Less so** those who want quiet, leafy streets.

## Rents

Rough asking rents for unfurnished flats in 2026 listings:

| What | Rough rent (KES a month) |
| --- | --- |
| One bedroom | 20,000 - 40,000 |
| Two bedrooms | 35,000 - 60,000 |

Newer "executive" blocks with lifts, parking and a pool can ask much more.
See the [move-in cost calculator](/tools/move-in-cost-calculator) and the
[neighbourhood comparer](/tools/neighbourhood-comparer).

## Getting around

- **Walking** to the CBD is possible from much of Ngara.
- **Matatus** run along Murang'a Road, Thika Road and Juja Road.
- **Thika Road** and the Globe roundabout area get very congested at rush
  hour.
- **Westlands and Parklands** are a short ride.

## Things to check

- **Security** - varies; prefer a gated building with a guard, and be
  careful walking at night around busy roads and markets. See
  [Safety in Nairobi](/guides/safety-in-nairobi).
- **Noise and crowds** near markets, bus stages and main roads. See
  [Noise and neighbours](/guides/noise-and-neighbours-in-nairobi).
- **Water and parking** in older blocks.
- **New buildings** - check the developer and that the building is approved
  and finished.

## Daily life

- **Markets** - Kariokor Market, Ngara's own market, and Gikomba and
  Eastleigh close by. See [Markets in Nairobi](/guides/markets-in-nairobi).
- **Shopping** - supermarkets locally and everything in the CBD.
- **Food** - local restaurants and street food; more choice in the CBD and
  Parklands.
- **Health** - hospitals in Parklands and the CBD are close.
