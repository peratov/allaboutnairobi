At **18**, every Kenyan citizen should register for a national ID. First-time
applicants now receive the digital **Maisha Card**, with a lifelong
[[Maisha Namba]]. You need it for almost everything - a bank account,
[[M-Pesa]] in your own name, a job, voting, a [[KRA PIN]], a passport and
university.

## What to take

Usually:

- your **original birth certificate** - see
  [Getting a birth certificate](/guides/getting-a-birth-certificate)
- **copies of your parents' IDs** (or death certificates, or an affidavit
  if a parent's ID isn't available)
- a **letter from the chief or assistant chief** of your area, confirming
  who you are
- school documents can help - such as a school ID or leaving certificate

Requirements vary between offices, especially where citizenship needs extra
checks, so ask your local registration office before you go.

## How to apply

1. **Visit the chief's office** in your area for the introduction letter.
2. **Go to a registration office** - the National Registration Bureau's
   offices at sub-county level, or a Huduma Centre that offers ID services.
3. **Fill in the application** with the registration officer.
4. **Biometrics** - your fingerprints and photo are taken.
5. **Keep the waiting card** - it's your proof of application.

The first ID is **free**. Don't pay anyone who offers to "speed it up".

## Collecting your ID

You'll get an SMS when your card is ready. Collect it from the office where
you applied, with your waiting card. Waiting times have improved but can
still be several weeks.

## After you get it

- **Register your SIM** in your own name - see
  [Phones and SIM cards](/guides/phones-and-sim-cards).
- **Get a [[KRA PIN]]** and set up [[eCitizen]] - see
  [eCitizen, KRA PIN and IDs](/guides/ecitizen-kra-pin-and-ids).
- **Register to vote** - see [Registering to vote](/guides/registering-to-vote-in-kenya).
- **Join SHA** - see [Registering for SHA](/guides/registering-for-sha).
- **Keep it safe** - never leave it as security with anyone, and report a
  lost ID at once. See [Reporting a crime and lost documents](/guides/reporting-a-crime-and-lost-documents).
