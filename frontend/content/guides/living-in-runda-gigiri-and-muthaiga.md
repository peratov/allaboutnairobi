North of the city centre, **Runda, Gigiri, Muthaiga and Rosslyn** are
Nairobi's diplomatic suburbs: large houses, embassies, the United Nations
Office at Nairobi, and several international schools. They are quiet, green
and among the most expensive places to live. They are on the
[map](/map#constituency/westlands).

## The areas

- **Gigiri** - home to the UN complex and many embassies and residences.
- **Runda** - a large, planned estate of big houses on half-acre plots, with an
  active residents' association.
- **Muthaiga** - Nairobi's oldest wealthy suburb, with large colonial-era
  homes and the Muthaiga Club.
- **Rosslyn** - near the International School of Kenya and Rosslyn Academy,
  with townhouses and newer compounds.

## Rents

Rough asking rents in 2026 listings:

| What | Rough rent (KES a month) |
| --- | --- |
| Two- or three-bedroom apartment | 120,000 - 250,000 |
| Townhouse in a compound | 200,000 - 400,000 |
| Four-bedroom house with garden | 220,000 - 400,000 and up |

Many homes are rented through agents, and leases for diplomatic and UN staff
are often quoted in US dollars.

## Who they suit

- **UN, embassy and NGO staff** working at Gigiri.
- **Families** at ISK, Rosslyn Academy and nearby schools.
- **Less so** people working in the south of the city, or anyone on a budget.

## Getting around

- **A car is needed.** Walking is limited and matatus are few.
- **To Westlands** - 15 to 40 minutes on Limuru Road or the Northern Bypass.
- **To the CBD** - 30 to 60 minutes at peak times.
- **To JKIA** - via the Northern and Eastern bypasses or the Expressway.

## Security

Security is taken seriously: gated compounds, guards, alarms and response
companies, and some areas with extra police presence around diplomatic sites.
UN and embassy staff usually follow their organisation's security rules on
housing.

## Daily life

- **Shopping:** Village Market, Two Rivers, Rosslyn Riviera and Ridgeways
  malls.
- **Health:** clinics in the malls, with the Aga Khan and MP Shah hospitals in
  Parklands.
- **Outdoors:** Karura Forest is close by.
