Salons and barbershops are everywhere in Nairobi, from a single chair in an
estate to large salons in the malls. Prices vary enormously; the trick is
finding someone you trust with your hair.

## Barbers

- **Estate barbershops** are cheap and quick, and usually walk-in.
- **Upmarket barbers** in malls and the main suburbs offer appointments,
  beard work and treatments, at higher prices.
- Many barbers do **house calls**, especially for children.

## Salons

- **Braiding** - box braids, cornrows, twists and more. Braiding takes
  hours; prices depend on the style, length and whether the extensions are
  included.
- **Natural hair and locs** - specialist salons and stylists for natural
  hair care, starting and maintaining locs.
- **Relaxing, colouring and weaves** - widely offered; ask what products
  they use.
- **Nails, lashes and make-up** - most salons offer these too.
- **Other hair types** - salons in the malls and expatriate areas cut and
  colour straight and wavy hair. Ask for recommendations from people with
  similar hair.

## Finding a good one

- **Ask** people whose hair you like where they go.
- **Look at their social media**; most stylists post their work.
- **Book ahead** for popular stylists, and before Christmas, Easter and
  weddings.
- Many stylists offer **home visits**, often at a similar price.

## Prices

Prices range from very cheap in the estates to similar to European cities in
upmarket salons. Ask the **full price before you start** - including
extensions, products and washing. Paying by M-Pesa is normal. A small tip
is appreciated; see [Tipping in Kenya](/guides/tipping-in-kenya).

## Hygiene

- Check that **combs, clippers and tools** are cleaned between clients,
  and that blades are new or sterilised.
- Watch for burns from chemicals and hot tools, and say if something hurts.
- Braiding very tightly can damage your hairline - ask for it looser.

## Running a salon or barbershop

Salons and barbershops need a **county business permit** and must meet
health and safety rules. See [Nairobi business permit](/guides/nairobi-business-permit)
and [Small business ideas in Nairobi](/guides/small-business-ideas-in-nairobi).
