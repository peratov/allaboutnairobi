Two days is enough to see why Nairobi is unlike any other capital: wildlife
in the morning, and good food and a night out in the evening. This plan
assumes you stay in Westlands, Kilimani or Karen and use ride-hailing between
stops.

## Day one - wildlife

**6am - Nairobi National Park.** Start early for the best chance of lions and
rhinos. A half-day tour with pickup is the easiest option. See
[Nairobi National Park](/guides/nairobi-national-park).

**11am - Sheldrick Wildlife Trust.** Watch orphaned baby elephants being fed
during the late-morning visiting hour. **Book in advance** online.

**1pm - Lunch in Karen.** Karen has many garden restaurants.

**2.30pm - Giraffe Centre.** Feed endangered Rothschild's giraffes from a
raised platform.

**4pm - Karen Blixen Museum.** The farmhouse from *Out of Africa*, with
gardens looking towards the Ngong Hills.

**Evening - Dinner.** Try [[nyama choma]] (roast meat) - many visitors go to
the famous Carnivore restaurant near Lang'ata. See
[Kenyan food and eating out](/guides/kenyan-food-and-eating-out).

## Day two - the city

**8am - Karura Forest.** Walk or cycle past a waterfall and caves in a large
forest inside the city. Bring your passport or ID for the entry fee.

**11am - Nairobi National Museum.** Kenya's history, culture and wildlife,
with a snake park next door.

**1pm - Lunch in the city.**

**2.30pm - The city centre.** See the KICC (with a rooftop view on a clear
day), the Nairobi Gallery and City Market. Go with a guide or keep valuables
out of sight.

**5pm - Sunset drink.** A rooftop bar in Westlands or Kilimani.

**Evening - A night out.** Westlands is the liveliest area. See
[Nightlife in Nairobi](/guides/nightlife-in-nairobi).

## Getting around

Traffic is the biggest risk to any plan. Travel early, avoid rush hours
(roughly 7 to 9am and 5 to 8pm), and use Uber, Bolt or Little rather than
driving yourself. See [Getting around Nairobi](/guides/getting-around-nairobi).

## If you have a third day

- **Hell's Gate and Lake Naivasha** - cycling among zebras, and a boat on the
  lake. See [Day trips from Nairobi](/guides/day-trips-from-nairobi).
- **The Maasai Mara** - best as an overnight trip. See
  [Maasai Mara from Nairobi](/guides/maasai-mara-from-nairobi).

## Tips

- **Book Sheldrick ahead**, and check opening times for everything.
- **Carry a card and some cash**; most places take M-Pesa and cards.
- **Dress in layers** - mornings can be cool. See
  [Weather in Nairobi](/guides/weather-in-nairobi).
- **Read [Safety in Nairobi](/guides/safety-in-nairobi)** before you go.
