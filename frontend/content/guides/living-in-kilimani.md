Kilimani is where many people moving to Nairobi start. It is central,
full of apartment blocks, and has everything within a short ride: malls,
cafés, hospitals, gyms and offices in Upper Hill and Westlands. It is on the
[map](/map#constituency/dagoretti-north), in Dagoretti North constituency.

## Who it suits

- **Young professionals and couples** who want to be close to work and
  nightlife.
- **Newcomers** who want a furnished flat, short leases and plenty of choice.
- **Less so** families wanting a garden and quiet, who often look at
  Lavington, Kileleshwa or further out.

## Rents

Rough asking rents for unfurnished flats in 2026 listings:

| What | Rough rent (KES a month) |
| --- | --- |
| Studio or bedsitter | 25,000 - 45,000 |
| One bedroom | 40,000 - 80,000 |
| Two bedrooms | 60,000 - 130,000 |
| Three bedrooms | 90,000 - 180,000 |

Newer towers with a pool, gym and backup power sit at the top of each range.
Furnished and serviced flats cost noticeably more. Add a service charge in some
buildings, and work out the full move-in cost with the
[move-in cost calculator](/tools/move-in-cost-calculator).

## The main streets

- **Argwings Kodhek Road** - the spine, with Yaya Centre, cafés and
  restaurants.
- **Ngong Road** edge - close to The Junction and Prestige Plaza, and the
  route to Karen.
- **Lenana, Ring Road Kilimani, Kindaruma and Wood Avenue** - quieter roads of
  apartment blocks.
- **Near Kenyatta Hospital and Upper Hill** - handy for hospital and office
  workers.

## Getting around

- **To Upper Hill and the CBD** - 10 to 30 minutes by car depending on
  traffic.
- **To Westlands** - 15 to 40 minutes via Waiyaki Way or Ring Road.
- **Ride-hailing** is cheap and quick at off-peak times.
- **Matatus** run along Ngong Road and Argwings Kodhek.

See [Getting around Nairobi](/guides/getting-around-nairobi) and the
[commute cost calculator](/tools/commute-cost-calculator).

## Safety

Kilimani is generally considered one of the safer central areas, but phone
snatching and car break-ins happen. Most blocks have gates and [[askari]]s.
Take a taxi rather than walking late at night, and choose a building with
controlled access. See [Safety in Nairobi](/guides/safety-in-nairobi).

## Water and power

Rapid building has put pressure on water in some parts, and supply can be
rationed. Ask whether the building has a **borehole** or storage tanks, and a
**backup generator**, before signing. See
[Utilities in Nairobi](/guides/utilities-in-nairobi).

## Daily life

- **Shopping:** Yaya Centre, Prestige Plaza and The Junction, with
  supermarkets and pharmacies.
- **Health:** Nairobi Hospital and Kenyatta National Hospital are close.
- **Eating out:** one of the densest areas for cafés and restaurants.
- **Nightlife:** livelier than Lavington, calmer than Westlands.

## Checklist before signing

- Visit at **rush hour and at night**.
- Ask about **water, power backup and parking**.
- Check noise from bars and construction nearby.
- Read [Finding housing](/guides/finding-housing) for deposits and scams.
