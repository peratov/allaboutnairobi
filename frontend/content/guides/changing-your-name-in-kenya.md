People change their names for many reasons: marriage, divorce, religion, or
simply preference. A full legal change in Kenya is made by **deed poll**. A
spelling mistake on a document is a correction, which is simpler.

## Taking a spouse's name

Many people use their spouse's name after marriage with their **marriage
certificate** as proof, without a deed poll. Institutions differ in what they
accept - for an ID or passport change, ask the Registration of Persons or
Immigration what they need. See [Getting married in Kenya](/guides/getting-married-in-kenya).

## A deed poll, step by step

1. **Prepare the deed poll** - a legal document declaring that you give up your
   old name and adopt the new one. An advocate usually prepares it, and it is
   signed before a commissioner for oaths or advocate, with witnesses.
2. **Register it** with the **Registrar of Documents** (in Nairobi, or at the
   coast registry in Mombasa), with copies of your ID and birth certificate,
   and pay the registration fee.
3. **Publish it in the Kenya Gazette.** The change becomes public, allowing
   anyone to object.
4. **Keep the registered deed poll and the Gazette notice** - you will need
   them for every institution.

Children's name changes need their parents' or guardians' consent, and may
need a court's involvement.

## Updating your documents

After the Gazette notice:

- **National ID or Maisha Card** - apply to the **Registration of Persons**
  with the deed poll and Gazette notice.
- **Passport** - apply on [[eCitizen]] for a new passport in the new name. See
  [Getting a passport](/guides/applying-for-a-kenyan-passport).
- **KRA PIN** - update your details on [[iTax]] or at a KRA office.
- **Banks, SHA, NSSF, HELB, insurers, employer and schools.**
- **Title deeds and vehicle logbooks** held in your old name.
- **Academic certificates** - institutions may add a note or reissue them.

Update your ID first: most other institutions will ask to see it.

## How long it takes

Registration and gazettement typically take weeks to a few months. Updating
every document takes longer - keep copies of the deed poll and Gazette notice
handy.
