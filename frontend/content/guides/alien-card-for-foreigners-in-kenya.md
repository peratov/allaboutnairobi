Foreign nationals who live in Kenya get a **foreigner certificate** - widely
called the **alien card**, or alien ID. It is your identity document in
Kenya, used to open bank accounts, register a SIM card, get a KRA PIN and
deal with government offices.

## Who must register

Every foreigner aged **18 or over** who lives in Kenya for more than
**{{ ALIEN_REGISTRATION_DAYS }} days** must register, under the **Kenya
Citizenship and Immigration Act 2011**. You need a valid immigration status
first, such as:

- a **work permit** - see [Class D work permit](/guides/class-d-work-permit)
- a **dependant's pass** - see [Dependant passes](/guides/dependant-passes)
- a **student pass**
- a **digital nomad permit** - see [Class N permit](/guides/class-n-digital-nomad-permit)
- **permanent residence** - see [Permanent residence and citizenship](/guides/permanent-residence-and-citizenship)

Visitors on an eTA do not get an alien card. Refugees receive refugee
identity documents through a separate process.

## How to apply

1. **Apply online** on the Department of Immigration's **eFNS** (Foreign
   Nationals Service) portal, logging in with your **eCitizen** account.
2. **Pay the fee** online, and print the application form (Form 50) and
   payment receipt.
3. **Attend in person** at the Department of Immigration in Nairobi (Nyayo
   House) or the office you are directed to, for **fingerprints** and a
   photograph.
4. **Collect** the card when you are notified that it is ready.

## Documents

- your **passport**, with a copy of the bio-data page
- a copy of your **permit or pass**
- **proof of residence** in Kenya - for example a tenancy agreement or
  utility bill
- **two colour passport photos** with a white background
- the printed **application form** and **payment receipt**
- your previous card, if you are renewing

Check the current list and fee on the eFNS portal before you go.

## Validity and renewal

The card is valid for as long as the permit or pass it is based on. When you
**renew your permit**, renew the alien card too.

## Lost cards and leaving

- If you lose it, **report it to the police**, get an abstract, and apply
  for a replacement on eFNS.
- When you leave Kenya for good, you may be asked to surrender it. See
  [Leaving Kenya](/guides/leaving-kenya).

## What you use it for

Banks, mobile networks, KRA, NSSF, SHA, schools, landlords and employers
ask for it. Until it arrives, most will accept your passport and permit.
See [eCitizen, KRA PIN and IDs](/guides/ecitizen-kra-pin-and-ids).
