South of the city centre, **South B**, **South C**, **Nairobi West** and
**Madaraka** are long-established middle-class estates. They offer good value,
a central location, and easy access to Mombasa Road and the airport. South B
is in [Makadara](/map#constituency/makadara) and South C in
[Lang'ata](/map#constituency/langata) on the map.

## The areas

- **South B** - busy, with estates, apartment blocks, shops and a lively local
  scene, close to the Industrial Area.
- **South C** - quieter and more residential, with maisonettes and newer
  apartments.
- **Nairobi West and Madaraka** - near Strathmore University and Nyayo
  Stadium, popular with students and young professionals.

## Rents

Rough asking rents in 2026 listings:

| What | Rough rent (KES a month) |
| --- | --- |
| Bedsitter or studio | 15,000 - 25,000 |
| One bedroom | 20,000 - 40,000 |
| Two bedrooms | 35,000 - 65,000 |
| Maisonette or townhouse | 60,000 - 120,000 |

## Who they suit

- **Workers in the CBD, Upper Hill or the Industrial Area.**
- **Frequent flyers** - JKIA is close along Mombasa Road or the Expressway.
- **Families and first-time renters** looking for value.

## Getting around

- **To the CBD** - 15 to 40 minutes; matatus run often.
- **To JKIA** - often 20 to 30 minutes via the Expressway.
- **Mombasa Road** can be slow at peak times, though the Expressway above it
  helps.

## Safety

Busy estates with the usual city risks. Choose gated buildings with guards,
and take care with phones on the main roads. See
[Safety in Nairobi](/guides/safety-in-nairobi).

## Daily life

- **Shopping:** Capital Centre and other malls along Mombasa Road, plus
  supermarkets and markets in the estates.
- **Health:** Mater Hospital in South B, and Nairobi West Hospital.
- **Eating out:** plenty of local restaurants and [[nyama choma]] spots.
