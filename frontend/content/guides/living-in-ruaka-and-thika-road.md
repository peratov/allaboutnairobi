North of the city, **Ruaka** and the towns along the **Thika Superhighway**
offer lower rents and newer buildings than central Nairobi. The trade-off is
the commute.

## The areas

- **Ruaka** - just outside the county border in Kiambu, near Two Rivers mall
  and Gigiri. Rapidly built up with apartment blocks.
- **Roysambu and Kasarani** - along Thika Road inside Nairobi, near Garden
  City, Kasarani stadium and USIU. See the [map](/map#constituency/kasarani).
- **Kahawa, Githurai, Ruiru and Juja** - further out, cheaper, popular with
  students and young families near Kenyatta University and JKUAT.

## Rents

Rough asking rents in 2026 listings:

| What | Ruaka (KES a month) | Thika Road towns (KES a month) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bedsitter or studio | 12,000 - 20,000 | 8,000 - 15,000 |
| One bedroom | 20,000 - 35,000 | 12,000 - 25,000 |
| Two bedrooms | 35,000 - 60,000 | 20,000 - 45,000 |

Newer blocks with lifts, gyms and backup power cost more.

## Who they suit

- **Workers in Gigiri, Runda or along Thika Road** (Ruaka suits people working
  in the north of the city).
- **Students and staff** at USIU, Kenyatta University and JKUAT.
- **People on a budget** who can work around peak traffic.

## Getting around

- **Thika Road** is fast off-peak but jams near Muthaiga, Pangani and
  Roysambu at rush hour. Matatus and buses run constantly.
- **From Ruaka**, Limuru Road and the Northern Bypass lead to Westlands and
  Gigiri. Ruaka's own roads are congested at peak times.
- **Commuter trains** run on some routes into the CBD.

See [Getting around Nairobi](/guides/getting-around-nairobi).

## Things to check

- **Water** - fast building has outpaced supply in parts of Ruaka and Thika
  Road towns. Ask about boreholes and tanks.
- **Drainage** - some roads flood in heavy rain.
- **Building quality** - visit several flats and check approvals for new
  blocks.

## Daily life

- **Shopping:** Two Rivers near Ruaka, and Garden City, Thika Road Mall and
  others along Thika Road.
- **Health:** clinics and hospitals along Thika Road, and larger hospitals in
  the city.
