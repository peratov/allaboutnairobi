Kenya's crypto tax rules changed in 2025. The headline tax most people heard
about is gone, but that does not make crypto tax-free. This is general
information, not tax advice; for anything large, ask a tax adviser.

## The Digital Asset Tax is gone

From 2023 a **Digital Asset Tax** took a slice of the value of every crypto
transfer through a platform. The **Finance Act 2025 repealed it from 1 July
2025**. If you see older articles quoting it, they are out of date.

## Excise duty on fees

In its place, **excise duty at {{ DIGITAL_ASSET_EXCISE_RATE|percent }}%**
is charged on the **fees** that virtual asset service providers charge -
trading, withdrawal and similar fees - not on the value you trade. The
platform pays it and usually builds it into the fee you see. You do not file
anything for it.

## Income tax still applies

- **Trading as a business.** If you buy and sell crypto regularly, as a
  livelihood or with a business's organisation, the profit is **business
  income**, taxed in your annual return like any other.
- **Being paid in crypto.** A salary, freelance fee or business sale paid in
  crypto is income in shillings, valued when you receive it. An employer must
  run [PAYE](/guides/paye-and-taxes) on it; a freelancer declares it.
- **Occasional investing.** How a one-off gain by an individual is treated is
  less settled, and KRA has not published simple guidance for it. Keep
  records, and take advice before a large sale.

## Records to keep

For every purchase, sale, swap and payment:

- the **date**, the **amount of crypto**, and its **value in shillings** then
- **fees** paid
- the **platform** or wallet, and the **transaction ID**
- what the money was for, if it was income

Exchanges' downloadable statements help, but they disappear when an account
closes - save them yearly.

## Filing

Declare crypto income in your annual return on iTax by {{ TAX_RETURN_DEADLINE }}, with your
other income. See [Filing your KRA return](/guides/filing-your-kra-return-with-a-p9)
and, if crypto is your business, [Turnover tax vs income tax](/guides/turnover-tax-vs-income-tax).
