Demand for good, affordable houses in Nairobi is high, and fraudsters use
that pressure. Most rental scams follow a few patterns, and nearly all of
them end the same way: you pay before you have the keys.

## The common scams

- **The fake listing.** Photos of a real house - often copied from another
  site - at a rent well below the area's usual price. The "agent" is
  happy to take a deposit but cannot show you the house today.
- **The booking or viewing fee.** You are asked to send money to "hold" the
  house or before a viewing. After paying, the agent stops answering.
- **The landlord abroad.** The "owner" is overseas and will send the keys
  once you pay the deposit and first month's rent.
- **The fake caretaker.** Someone shows you a real, empty house - but they do
  not work for the landlord, and the money goes to them.
- **Double letting.** The same house is let to several people, each of whom
  pays a deposit.
- **The disappearing agent.** A real agent takes your deposit and rent in
  cash or to a personal number, and the landlord never receives it.

## Checks before you pay

1. **See the house in person**, or send someone you trust. Do not pay for a
   house you have only seen in photos.
2. **Know the market.** A rent far below what similar houses in the
   neighbourhood cost is a warning. Compare in the
   [neighbourhood comparer](/tools/neighbourhood-comparer).
3. **Meet the caretaker and neighbours.** Ask who the landlord is and how
   rent is usually paid.
4. **Ask who you are dealing with.** An agent should give a company name,
   office address and registration. Estate agents must be registered with
   the **Estate Agents Registration Board**.
5. **Pay into an account in the landlord's or company's name** - a paybill or
   bank account - not a personal M-Pesa number. Check the name on the
   confirmation before entering your PIN.
6. **Get a written tenancy agreement** before paying the deposit, and a
   receipt for everything. See [Tenancy agreements in Kenya](/guides/tenancy-agreements-in-kenya).
7. **Be wary of urgency.** "Three other people want it today" is a sales
   line.

## If you are buying, not renting

Land and property fraud uses the same tricks on a larger scale. Always do a
search before paying anything. See [Land search on Ardhisasa](/guides/land-search-on-ardhisasa)
and [Buying property in Kenya](/guides/buying-property-in-kenya).

## If you have been scammed

- Report it to the **police** and get an **OB number**.
- Ask Safaricom or your bank to stop or reverse the payment straight away.
  See [M-Pesa fraud and scams](/guides/mpesa-fraud-and-scams-in-kenya).
- Report the listing to the website or social media platform it was on.
- Warn others in local housing groups.
