Westlands is Nairobi's second city centre: office towers, malls, hotels,
restaurants and most of the city's nightlife, with apartment blocks packed
around them. Living here means a short walk or ride to a great deal, and
paying for it. It is on the [map](/map#constituency/westlands).

## Who it suits

- **People who work in Westlands** or along Waiyaki Way, who can skip the
  worst of the traffic.
- **Those who want city life** - restaurants, bars and malls on the doorstep.
- **Less so** anyone who values quiet evenings, or families wanting space.

## Rents

Rough asking rents for unfurnished flats in 2026 listings:

| What | Rough rent (KES a month) |
| --- | --- |
| Studio | 35,000 - 70,000 |
| One bedroom | 45,000 - 90,000 |
| Two bedrooms | 80,000 - 200,000 |
| Three bedrooms | 130,000 - 300,000 |

Westlands has many new towers with gyms, pools and rooftop lounges, which
push the averages up. Furnished and serviced flats are common, and cost more.
Use the [move-in cost calculator](/tools/move-in-cost-calculator) to budget.

## The main areas

- **Central Westlands** - around Sarit, Westgate, The Mall and Woodvale Grove.
  Busy and noisy, walkable.
- **Parklands** - next door, more residential, with the Aga Khan hospital and
  City Park.
- **Riverside and General Mathenge** - quieter, leafier, and pricier.
- **Spring Valley and Loresho** - further out, with houses and gardens.

## Getting around

- **To the CBD** - 15 to 45 minutes; the
  [Expressway](/guides/getting-around-nairobi#the-expressway) starts nearby.
- **To the airport** - often around 30 to 40 minutes on the Expressway.
- **To Kilimani and Upper Hill** - 15 to 40 minutes.
- **Walking** works in daylight within central Westlands.

Waiyaki Way and the roundabouts around Westlands clog at rush hour, so live
near where you work if you can.

## Safety

Central Westlands is busy late into the night, which helps, but crowds bring
phone theft and pickpocketing, especially around bars. Use ride-hailing at
night, keep your phone out of sight on the street, and choose a building with
controlled access. See [Safety in Nairobi](/guides/safety-in-nairobi).

## Daily life

- **Malls:** Sarit, Westgate, The Mall and others, with supermarkets, cinemas
  and banks.
- **Health:** Aga Khan University Hospital and many clinics.
- **Nightlife:** the busiest in Nairobi. See
  [Nightlife in Nairobi](/guides/nightlife-in-nairobi).
- **Coworking:** many spaces for remote workers. See
  [Working remotely](/guides/working-remotely-from-nairobi).

## Checklist before signing

- **Noise.** Ask whether there are bars or clubs nearby, and visit on a Friday
  night.
- **Parking and traffic** getting in and out of the building at rush hour.
- **Water and backup power.**
- Read [Finding housing](/guides/finding-housing) for deposits and scams.
