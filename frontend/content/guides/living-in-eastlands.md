**Eastlands** is the name Nairobians give to the large, busy estates east of
the city centre, along Jogoo Road, Outering Road and towards the airport.
It is where a large share of the city lives: rents are far lower than in the
west, and community life is strong.

## The areas

- **Buruburu** - one of the oldest planned middle-class estates, with
  maisonettes, schools and a busy shopping centre off Jogoo Road.
- **Donholm**, **Savannah** and **Tassia** - apartments and older
  houses around Outering Road, with good matatu links.
- **Umoja**, **Kayole** and **Komarock** - dense estates of flats and
  rooms, among the cheapest in the city.
- **Embakasi** - a huge area around the airport, from older estates to new
  apartment blocks, with the Embakasi and Pipeline areas very crowded.
- **Utawala** and **Ruai** - further out along the Eastern Bypass, newer and
  more spacious, popular with families building or buying their first home.

## Who it suits

- **People on a budget** who want to live inside Nairobi.
- **Families** who want more space for the money, especially in Buruburu and
  Utawala.
- **People working** in the CBD, Industrial Area or near JKIA.
- **Less so** people working in Westlands, Gigiri or Karen, who face long
  cross-town commutes.

## Getting around

- **Matatus** run constantly along Jogoo Road, Outering Road, Mombasa Road
  and the Eastern Bypass to the CBD. See [Matatus in Nairobi](/guides/matatus-in-nairobi).
- **Commuter trains** from Embakasi Village and stations along the line to
  the CBD can beat the traffic.
- **Traffic** on Jogoo Road, Outering Road and the Eastern Bypass is heavy at
  rush hour.
- **JKIA** is close - a big advantage for frequent flyers and airport
  workers.

## Things to check

- **Water** - shortages are common in parts of Eastlands. Ask how often water
  comes, and whether the building has a tank or borehole. See
  [Water tanks and boreholes](/guides/water-tanks-and-boreholes).
- **Security** - varies a lot from street to street. Look for a gated
  compound with a guard, and ask neighbours.
- **Flooding** - some low-lying parts flood in the long rains.
- **Noise** - bars, churches and matatu stages can be loud. See
  [Noise and neighbours](/guides/noise-and-neighbours-in-nairobi).
- **The building** - very cheap new blocks are sometimes poorly built. Check
  for cracks and damp, and that the building is approved.

## Daily life

- **Shopping** - supermarkets, local markets, and shopping centres in
  Buruburu, Donholm and along the bypass, plus Gikomba and Eastleigh nearby.
  See [Markets in Nairobi](/guides/markets-in-nairobi).
- **Food** - lively nyama choma joints, street food and estate restaurants.
- **Schools** - many public and private schools, including long-established
  ones in Buruburu.
- **Health** - hospitals and clinics along Jogoo Road, Outering Road and in
  Embakasi. See [Hospitals in Nairobi](/guides/hospitals-in-nairobi).
