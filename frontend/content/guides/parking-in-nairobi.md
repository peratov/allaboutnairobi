Street parking in the CBD and other busy parts of Nairobi is run by
**Nairobi City County**, and you must pay for it. Unpaid parking ends in
clamping or towing, which costs far more than the fee.

## Where you pay

You pay county parking in the **CBD** and in other zones the county
charges, usually marked by signs and painted bays. Rates are lower outside
the most congested areas. The county sets the fees in its annual Finance
Act, so check the current rate before you park.

## How to pay

- Pay through the county's **Nairobi Pay** system - by **USSD on *647#**
  or online - using M-Pesa.
- You need your **number plate** and the **zone**.
- Keep the confirmation message until you leave.
- **Do not pay cash to "attendants"** or anyone on the street. In 2026 the
  county impounded hundreds of vehicles whose drivers had paid informal
  attendants instead of the official system.

## When you pay

- Parking is charged on **weekdays** during working hours.
- Street parking is generally **free on weekends** and public holidays;
  check the signs.
- One payment usually covers the **whole day**, so you can move between bays.

## Seasonal parking

If you drive into town every day, a **seasonal parking** ticket - monthly,
quarterly or yearly - costs less than paying daily. Buy it through the same
county system. The county has offered early-bird discounts on yearly tickets.

## Clamping and towing

If you do not pay, or park where you should not:

- your car may be **clamped**, or **towed** to the county's yard
- you pay the parking fee plus **clamping or towing charges** and any
  penalties before it is released
- pay only through the county's official channels and get a receipt

Never park on yellow lines, in bus or matatu stops, on pedestrian
crossings, or blocking gates - these can be towed straight away.

## Private parking

- **Malls** charge by the hour or day, paid at a machine, the exit or by
  M-Pesa. Some give free time if you shop.
- **Office buildings** rent parking to tenants and visitors.
- **Apartment blocks** usually include one bay per flat; ask before you
  rent. See [Finding housing](/guides/finding-housing).
- **Hospitals, hotels and events** have their own paid parking.

## Security

- Do not leave anything visible in the car - bags, laptops or phones.
- Use guarded or well-lit parking at night.
- Watch for people trying to distract you while you park.
