Drones are popular for photos at weddings, events and safaris - but Kenya
regulates them closely. The **Kenya Civil Aviation Authority** (KCAA)
controls who can fly, where and how. Flying without the right approvals
can lead to the drone being seized, fines or arrest.

## The basic rules

- **Every drone must be registered** with the KCAA before it is flown, for
  recreation or business.
- **Commercial use** - weddings, filming, surveying, agriculture - needs
  further **operator approval** and a qualified remote pilot.
- **Importing a drone** needs a **KCAA import permit**; customs will hold a
  drone that arrives without one. See
  [Importing goods into Kenya](/guides/importing-goods-into-kenya).

Apply and check current requirements on the KCAA's official website. The
regulations have been revised, so don't rely on old blog posts.

## Visitors

Bringing your own drone as a tourist is **difficult**. You may need KCAA
approval before you travel, and some sources say only Kenyan citizens and
companies can register drones. Many visitors either leave the drone at
home or hire a **licensed local drone operator** instead.

## Where you can't fly

- **Near airports** and airstrips, including JKIA and Wilson Airport.
- **Over government buildings**, military sites, State House and other
  security areas.
- **Over crowds**, roads and events without permission.
- **In national parks and reserves** - flying is restricted and needs
  separate approval from the wildlife authorities. Drones disturb animals.
  See [Visiting Kenya's national parks](/guides/visiting-kenyas-national-parks).
- **Over private property** without the owner's permission.

## Flying safely

- Keep the drone **in sight** and below the height limits set in your
  approval.
- Don't fly at **night** or in bad weather unless approved.
- **Respect privacy** - don't film people or homes without consent. See
  [Your data protection rights](/guides/your-data-protection-rights-in-kenya).

## Hiring a drone operator

For weddings, events and property photos, hire an operator who can show
KCAA approval and insurance. See [Planning a wedding in Nairobi](/guides/planning-a-wedding-in-nairobi).
