Tens of thousands of fans are expected in Nairobi for AFCON 2027. This guide is
for anyone coming from abroad - or from elsewhere in Kenya - to follow their
team. For the tournament itself, see [AFCON 2027 in Nairobi](/guides/afcon-2027-in-nairobi).

## Before you travel

### Visas and entry

- **Kenya:** most visitors apply online for an [[eTA]] before flying, for
  US$ {{ ETA_FEE_USD }}. **Most African nationals do not need one**, and East
  African Community citizens travel freely. See
  [Applying for an eTA](/guides/kenya-eta-step-by-step).
- **Uganda** has announced a **visa-fee waiver** for visitors from about a
  month before kickoff to a month after the final.
- **A single visa** for Kenya, Uganda and Tanzania during the tournament has
  been discussed by the three governments but not confirmed.
- **Tanzania** has its own e-visa for most nationalities.

Check the official sites for your passport, and keep an eye on the
[AFCON hub](/afcon) for any AFCON-specific arrangements or Fan ID.

### Health

- **Yellow fever** vaccination may be required, depending on where you travel
  from.
- **Malaria** is not a significant risk in Nairobi, which is high and cool,
  but is present at the coast, around Lake Victoria and in many other areas -
  including parts of the other host countries. Ask a travel clinic.
- **Travel insurance** with medical cover is essential.

### Book early

Rooms will be in demand, especially for Kenya matches and the knockout rounds.
Book **refundable** accommodation now and firm up once the draw sets the
fixtures.

## Where to stay

Choose by stadium - Nairobi traffic makes cross-town trips slow.

- **For Raila Odinga International Stadium** (Ngong Road): Kilimani, Kileleshwa,
  Lavington, Karen and Lang'ata. See [Living in Kilimani](/guides/living-in-kilimani).
- **For Kasarani** (Thika Road): Roysambu, Kasarani and along Thika Road, or
  Gigiri and Runda for more comfort. See
  [Ruaka and Thika Road](/guides/living-in-ruaka-and-thika-road).
- **For Nyayo** (by the CBD): the CBD, Upper Hill, South B, South C and
  Madaraka. See [South B and South C](/guides/living-in-south-b-and-south-c).
- **Westlands** is the most popular all-round base, with the most hotels, bars
  and restaurants, and the Expressway towards Nyayo and the airport.

See [Where to stay in Nairobi](/guides/where-to-stay-in-nairobi) and
[Serviced apartments](/guides/serviced-apartments-in-nairobi).

## Arriving

- **JKIA** is Nairobi's main airport. See the [JKIA airport guide](/guides/jkia-airport-guide).
- **Get a SIM or eSIM** on arrival - you will need data for tickets, maps and
  ride-hailing. See [eSIMs for visitors](/guides/esims-and-sim-cards-for-visitors).
- **Set up M-Pesa** if you are staying a while. See
  [M-Pesa and mobile money](/guides/mpesa-and-mobile-money).

## Money

The currency is the Kenyan shilling. Cards work in malls, hotels and many
restaurants; everything else is M-Pesa or cash. See
[Changing money in Nairobi](/guides/changing-money-in-nairobi). Budget the trip
with the [AFCON trip budget](/tools/afcon-trip-budget).

## Getting around

- **Ride-hailing** (Uber, Bolt, Little) is the easiest way to get around.
- **Matatus** are cheap and go everywhere.
- **Avoid driving yourself** on matchdays.

Plan stadium trips with the [matchday planner](/tools/afcon-matchday-planner).
See [Getting around Nairobi](/guides/getting-around-nairobi).

## Weather

June and July are **Nairobi's coolest, driest months**: mild, often cloudy days
and cold evenings, sometimes down to around 12°C. Bring a warm layer for
evening matches. See [Weather in Nairobi](/guides/weather-in-nairobi).

## Staying safe

Nairobi welcomes visitors, and most trips are trouble-free. Big events attract
pickpockets and scammers, so:

- **Keep your phone out of sight** in crowds and near car windows.
- **Use ride-hailing at night.**
- **Buy tickets only from official channels.**
- **Save the emergency numbers:** {{ EMERGENCY_NUMBER }} or 112.

See [Is Nairobi safe for tourists?](/guides/is-nairobi-safe-for-tourists).

## Between matches

- **Nairobi National Park** - lions and rhinos with the skyline behind. See
  [Nairobi National Park](/guides/nairobi-national-park).
- **Baby elephants and giraffes** - the Sheldrick Wildlife Trust and the
  Giraffe Centre. See [Nairobi in 48 hours](/guides/nairobi-in-48-hours).
- **The Maasai Mara** - the Great Migration usually begins arriving in July.
  Reserve fees rise on 1 July. See [The Maasai Mara from Nairobi](/guides/maasai-mara-from-nairobi).
- **Lake Naivasha and Hell's Gate** - an easy day or overnight trip. See
  [Weekend getaways](/guides/weekend-getaways-from-nairobi).
- **The coast** - by SGR train or a short flight. See
  [Nairobi to Mombasa](/guides/nairobi-to-mombasa).
