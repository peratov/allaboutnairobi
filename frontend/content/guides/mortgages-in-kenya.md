Only a small share of Kenyan homes are bought with a mortgage, mainly because
rates are high compared with many countries. But rates have fallen, and
cheaper government-backed loans exist for lower- and middle-income buyers.

## Interest rates

- **Standard bank mortgages** have mostly charged around **12% to 16%** a year
  in 2026, usually on a variable rate that moves with the Central Bank rate.
- **KMRC-backed affordable home loans** - banks and SACCOs refinanced by the
  Kenya Mortgage Refinance Company - start from around
  **{{ MORTGAGE_RATE_KMRC|percent }}%**, with fixed rates, for eligible buyers.
  They have limits on income (around KES 150,000 a month) and on the home's
  price.
- **SACCO mortgages** can be cheaper for members.

## Deposits and terms

- **Deposit:** usually **10% to 20%** of the price, sometimes less for
  affordable housing loans.
- **Term:** up to 20 or 25 years, often ending by retirement age.
- **Affordability:** lenders usually limit repayments to about a third to a
  half of your net income.

Check what you could borrow with the
[mortgage affordability calculator](/tools/mortgage-affordability-calculator).

## Costs on top

- **Stamp duty** - {{ STAMP_DUTY_URBAN|percent }}% of the value in Nairobi.
  See the [stamp duty calculator](/tools/stamp-duty-calculator).
- **Valuation, legal fees** (for both your advocate and the bank's), and the
  bank's **arrangement fee**.
- **Insurance** - mortgage protection (life) and building insurance.

These often add 6 to 8% of the price, and are usually paid from savings.

## Applying

1. **Get pre-approval** to know your budget.
2. **Find the property**, and have your advocate run the checks. See
   [Buying property in Kenya](/guides/buying-property-in-kenya).
3. **The bank values the property**, issues an offer letter and registers a
   **charge** on the title.
4. **The loan pays the seller** at completion.

You will need ID, KRA PIN, payslips and bank statements (six to twelve
months), and for the self-employed, audited accounts or business records.

## Kenyans abroad

Several Kenyan banks offer **diaspora mortgages**, with income assessed in
foreign currency. Expect to show a foreign payslip, bank statements and proof
of residence abroad. See [Kenyans abroad](/guides/kenyans-abroad-managing-things-from-afar).

## Other ways to buy

- **Tenant purchase** through the Affordable Housing Programme. See
  [Housing Levy and Boma Yangu](/guides/housing-levy-and-boma-yangu).
- **Developer payment plans** for off-plan property - check the developer
  carefully.
