Kenya has hundreds of safari companies, from one-vehicle operators to large
companies with their own lodges. Most are honest, but quality varies widely
and scams exist. A few checks help you choose well.

## Check the company

- **Tourism licence.** Tour operators must be licensed by the **Tourism
  Regulatory Authority (TRA)**. Ask for the licence number.
- **Membership of KATO.** The **Kenya Association of Tour Operators** has a
  code of conduct and a complaints process, and you can check membership on its
  website.
- **A physical office** and a company registered in Kenya.
- **Reviews** on several independent sites, including recent ones - not only
  testimonials on the company's own site.

## Comparing quotes

Quotes are hard to compare until you break them down. Ask each company:

- **Park and reserve fees** - included, and for how many days? These are a
  large part of the cost. See [Maasai Mara from Nairobi](/guides/maasai-mara-from-nairobi).
- **Vehicle** - a pop-up-roof minivan or a 4x4 Land Cruiser? 4x4s handle rough
  roads and rain far better.
- **Private or shared** - your own vehicle and guide, or joining a group?
- **Window seats** - a guaranteed window seat for everyone?
- **Accommodation** - the names of the camps or lodges, so you can look them up.
- **Meals and drinks** - full board? Are drinks included?
- **Game drives** - how many a day, and are full-day drives possible?
- **Transfers** - pickup from your hotel or the airport?

The cheapest quote often uses a shared minivan, budget camps outside the
reserve and fewer game drives. That can still be a good trip, if you know
what you are getting.

## The guide

Your driver-guide makes the safari. Ask about their experience and
qualifications, and whether they are certified by the Kenya Professional Safari
Guides Association.

## Paying safely

- **Pay the company**, by card or bank transfer to a business account - never
  cash to an individual or to a personal M-Pesa number.
- **Get a written itinerary and invoice** listing what is included.
- **Check the deposit and cancellation terms.**
- **Take travel insurance.**

## Red flags

- **Prices far below everyone else's** for the same itinerary.
- **Pressure to pay immediately**, especially in cash.
- **Touts** approaching you on the street or at the airport.
- **Vague answers** about accommodation, vehicles or fees.
- **No licence number** or KATO membership.
