A growing number of families in Nairobi teach their children at home,
whether for religious reasons, a child's special needs, frequent moves, or
dissatisfaction with schools. But homeschooling sits in a **legal grey
area** in Kenya, and you should understand the risks before you start.

## What the law says

- The **Basic Education Act 2013** makes basic education free and
  compulsory, and makes it a parent's duty to take a child to school.
- **No law expressly allows homeschooling.** Parents have challenged the
  school-attendance duty in court, so far without changing it, and some
  homeschooling parents have been arrested and charged.
- **Change may be coming.** Homeschooling families have pushed for it to be
  recognised in new education law. Check the current position before you
  decide, and consider legal advice. See
  [Finding a lawyer in Kenya](/guides/finding-a-lawyer-in-kenya).

## How families homeschool anyway

- **International curricula** - many follow the British (IGCSE and A
  levels), American or other programmes, with an online school or a
  provider that sets and marks work.
- **Online schools** - accredited online schools give structure, records
  and recognised certificates.
- **Learning pods and co-ops** - a few families share a tutor or take turns
  teaching.
- **Alternative providers** - some small community and private learning
  centres operate as **Alternative Providers of Basic Education**.

## Exams and certificates

- **Kenyan exams** - check with KNEC whether and how your child can sit
  national exams as a private candidate. See
  [KCSE results and certificates](/guides/kcse-results-and-certificates).
- **International exams** - IGCSEs and A levels can be taken as a private
  candidate through approved exam centres in Nairobi.
- **Keep records** - work samples, reports and certificates, in case you
  need to show what your child has learned or move them into a school later.

## Things to weigh

- **Socialising** - sport, clubs, church and co-ops matter more when your
  child isn't in school every day.
- **Cost** - online schools and exam fees add up.
- **Moving back** - schools may assess a homeschooled child before placing
  them. See [Schools in Nairobi](/guides/schools-in-nairobi) and
  [International schools in Nairobi](/guides/international-schools-in-nairobi).
