Government jobs are sought after in Kenya for their security, pension and
benefits. Recruitment is spread across several bodies, each with its own
process, and competition is stiff.

## Who recruits

- **The Public Service Commission** (PSC) recruits for national government
  ministries and departments, and advertises vacancies on its online jobs
  portal.
- **County Public Service Boards** recruit for county governments - in
  Nairobi, the **Nairobi City County Public Service Board**.
- **The Teachers Service Commission** (TSC) recruits and employs teachers in
  public schools.
- **The National Police Service**, the **Kenya Defence Forces** and other
  security services hold their own recruitment drives, usually announced
  nationally with set dates and venues.
- **State corporations** - parastatals and agencies such as KRA, Kenya Power
  and the regulators - recruit directly, on their own websites.
- **Parliament** and the **Judiciary** have their own service commissions.

Vacancies are also advertised in the national newspapers.

## Internships and graduate programmes

The PSC runs a **public service internship programme** for recent
graduates, with a monthly stipend, and many state corporations offer
internships and attachments. They are a common route into a permanent
post.

## Applying

1. **Read the advert closely** - the requirements, the grade, and the
   closing date. Late or incomplete applications are usually rejected.
2. **Apply only through the official channel** named in the advert -
   usually an online portal.
3. **Prepare your documents**: national ID, academic and professional
   certificates, and a CV.
4. **Shortlisted candidates** are invited to interviews and sometimes
   tests. Shortlists are often published.

## Clearance certificates

Senior and many other public jobs require proof you meet the integrity
rules in **Chapter Six of the Constitution**. Expect to provide:

- a **KRA tax compliance certificate**
- a **HELB clearance certificate**, if you had a student loan
- **EACC** clearance
- a **CRB** clearance report
- a **certificate of good conduct** from the DCI

See [Clearance certificates for jobs](/guides/clearance-certificates-for-jobs).
Start early: some take days or weeks.

## Recruitment scams

- **Government jobs never require payment** to apply, be shortlisted or
  be hired.
- Ignore anyone offering a job, or a place in police or KDF recruitment, in
  exchange for money - it is a scam and a crime.
- Check adverts on the recruiting body's **official website**.

See [M-Pesa fraud and scams](/guides/mpesa-fraud-and-scams-in-kenya).
