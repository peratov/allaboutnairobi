Nairobi is the only capital with a national park inside its boundary, and much
of the best of the city is outdoors. Every place below is on the [map](/map).

## Wildlife

- **[Nairobi National Park](/map#place/nairobi-national-park)** - lions,
  rhino, giraffe, buffalo and hundreds of bird species, with the skyline behind
  them. Go early in the morning in your own car, with a guide, or on a tour.
  Park fees are paid online through the Kenya Wildlife Service; the gates do
  not take cash.
- **[Sheldrick Wildlife Trust](/map#place/sheldrick-wildlife-trust)** - the
  orphaned elephant nursery. Visits are once a day and need to be booked in
  advance. You can also foster an elephant.
- **[Giraffe Centre](/map#place/giraffe-centre)** - feed Rothschild's giraffes
  from a raised platform in Lang'ata.

## Forests and parks

- **[Karura Forest](/map#place/karura-forest)** - trails for walking, running
  and cycling, a waterfall and caves, a short drive from Westlands.
- **[Nairobi Arboretum](/map#place/nairobi-arboretum)** - a tree park good for
  a picnic or an early run.
- **[Ngong Road Forest](/map#place/ngong-road-forest)** - quieter trails
  between Ngong Road and Karen.
- **[Uhuru Park](/map#place/uhuru-park)** - boats on the lake and the city's
  favourite weekend lawn.

## Museums and culture

- **[Nairobi National Museum](/map#place/nairobi-national-museum)** - Kenyan
  history, culture and natural history, with the Snake Park next door.
- **[Karen Blixen Museum](/map#place/karen-blixen-museum)** - the farmhouse
  from Out of Africa.
- **[Bomas of Kenya](/map#place/bomas-of-kenya)** - traditional homesteads
  and dance performances.
- **[Nairobi Railway Museum](/map#place/nairobi-railway-museum)** - steam
  locomotives and the story of the railway the city grew around.
- **[Nairobi Gallery](/map#place/nairobi-gallery)** and the
  **[Kenya National Archives](/map#place/kenya-national-archives)** - art and
  history in the CBD.
- **[KICC](/map#place/kicc)** - go up to the rooftop for the best view of the
  city.

## Markets

The travelling Maasai Market for crafts, City Market in the CBD, and the huge
second-hand markets of Gikomba and Toi. See
[Shopping in Nairobi](/guides/shopping-in-nairobi).

## Food and nights out

See [Kenyan food and eating out](/guides/kenyan-food-and-eating-out) and [Nightlife in Nairobi](/guides/nightlife-in-nairobi).

- **Nyama choma** on a weekend afternoon is the Nairobi institution. Carnivore,
  on Lang'ata Road, is the famous one for visitors.
- **Westlands** has the densest nightlife, from rooftop bars to clubs.
  **Kilimani** and **Karen** have quieter bars and restaurants.
- Go out by ride-hailing and come back the same way. See
  [Safety in Nairobi](/guides/safety-in-nairobi#at-night).

## Sport

Football in the Kenyan Premier League, rugby on weekends, horse racing at
[Ngong Racecourse](/map#place/ngong-racecourse), and athletics at the
[Kasarani](/map#place/kasarani-stadium) and
[Nyayo](/map#place/nyayo-national-stadium) stadiums. See [Events](/events).
