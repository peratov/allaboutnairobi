Every year thousands of Kenyans go abroad to study - for undergraduate
degrees, master's or PhDs. The process takes many months, so start at least
a year ahead.

## Choosing where and what

- **Popular destinations** include the UK, US, Canada, Australia, Germany
  and other European countries, and increasingly Asia and other African
  countries.
- Compare **costs** - fees plus living costs - **work rights** for students,
  and whether the qualification is recognised in Kenya.
- **Check the institution is accredited** in its own country. If you plan
  to work in Kenya, check how qualifications are recognised here.

## Applying

- Apply directly to the institution, or through a country's central
  application system where it has one.
- You will usually need **certified transcripts and certificates**, an
  **English test** for some countries, **references** and a **personal
  statement**. See [KCSE results and certificates](/guides/kcse-results-and-certificates).
- **Deadlines** are often 6-12 months before the course starts.

## Paying for it

- **Scholarships** - offered by foreign governments, universities and
  foundations. Many open a year ahead and are very competitive. Apply only
  through official websites, and never pay for a "scholarship".
- **Family savings and loans** - plan the full cost for every year.
- **Part-time work** - many countries limit student work hours; don't
  count on it to pay fees.

## Student visas

- Apply for the correct **student visa** on the country's official
  immigration website, after receiving your offer.
- Expect to show **proof of funds** - bank statements showing fees and
  living costs - plus your offer letter, passport and sometimes a medical
  check.
- Funds usually need to be held for a set period before you apply.

See [Applying for visas from Nairobi](/guides/applying-for-visas-from-nairobi)
and [Applying for a Kenyan passport](/guides/applying-for-a-kenyan-passport).

## Education agents

Many universities work with agents in Nairobi. A good agent can help, but:

- Check the agent is **listed by the university** they represent.
- Don't pay large fees to agents, and **pay tuition directly** to the
  university.
- Be wary of promises of **guaranteed visas** or jobs.

## Before you go

- Arrange **health insurance** - often required. See
  [Travel insurance from Kenya](/guides/travel-insurance-from-kenya).
- Sort out **money transfers** for fees and living costs. See
  [Sending money abroad](/guides/sending-money-abroad-from-kenya).
- If you have a **HELB loan**, keep up with it. See
  [HELB loans](/guides/helb-loans-repayment-and-clearance).
