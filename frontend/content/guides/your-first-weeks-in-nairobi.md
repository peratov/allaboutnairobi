Do not sign a lease from abroad. Stay somewhere temporary for the first few
weeks, learn the city and its traffic, and then choose where to live.

## Where to stay at first

- **Serviced apartments** - a kitchen, cleaning, security and often a
  generator, with weekly or monthly rates. The best option for a month or more.
  Common in Kilimani, Westlands and Lavington.
- **Short-term rentals** through booking apps - flexible, but check reviews,
  security, water supply and backup power before paying.
- **Hotels** - easiest for the first few nights, especially if you arrive late.

Choose somewhere central to where you will work or look for housing, so you
can view flats at different times of day.
