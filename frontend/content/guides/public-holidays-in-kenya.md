Kenya's public holidays are set by the Public Holidays Act. Government
offices, banks and most businesses close. Supermarkets, malls and restaurants
usually stay open.

## The rules

- **A holiday on a Sunday** moves to the following Monday. A holiday on a
  Saturday does not move.
- **Idd-ul-Azha** is a public holiday in years when the Interior Cabinet
  Secretary gazettes it, so it is not listed above until then.
- **Extra holidays** are sometimes declared at short notice by gazette notice,
  for example for a national event or a period of mourning.

## Working on a public holiday

If you work on a public holiday you are owed double pay for the hours worked.
See [Working in Kenya](/guides/working-in-kenya).

## The national days

- **Madaraka Day, 1 June** - self-government in 1963.
- **Mazingira Day, 10 October** - environment day, with tree planting. It has
  had several names: Moi Day, then Huduma Day, then Utamaduni Day.
- **Mashujaa Day, 20 October** - Heroes' Day.
- **Jamhuri Day, 12 December** - independence in 1963 and the republic in
  1964.
- **Utamaduni Day, 26 December** - culture day, formerly Boxing Day.

## Around Christmas

Nairobi empties at Christmas as people travel [[upcountry]]. Traffic out of the
city is heavy in the days before, bus and SGR tickets sell out, and many small
businesses close until early January.
