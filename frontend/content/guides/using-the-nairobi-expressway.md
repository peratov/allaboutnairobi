The **Nairobi Expressway** is an elevated toll road that runs above Mombasa
Road, Uhuru Highway and Waiyaki Way, from **Mlolongo** and **JKIA** to
**Westlands**. It turns a journey that can take hours at rush hour into one
of about 20 minutes - for a fee.

## The route

- It runs for about 27 km, from Mlolongo, past JKIA, along Mombasa Road and
  Uhuru Highway, to the James Gichuru Road junction on Waiyaki Way.
- There are **entry and exit points** along the way, including at JKIA,
  the Eastern and Southern Bypasses, Mombasa Road, the CBD and Westlands.
- It opened in 2022 and is run by a private operator under a concession
  with the government.

## Tolls

- The toll depends on **where you enter and exit** and on your **vehicle
  class** - cars pay least, lorries most.
- Charges are shown on the operator's website and at the toll stations.
  Check before you go: they are revised from time to time.
- Use the [commute cost calculator](/tools/commute-cost-calculator) to see
  what a daily trip adds up to.

## How to pay

You pay at the **toll station** when you exit, or when you enter at some
points. A barrier stays down until you pay.

- **Electronic toll collection (ETC)** - a tag in the car that is charged
  automatically, through dedicated fast lanes. The quickest option for
  regular users.
- **Manual toll card (MTC)** - a prepaid card you top up and tap at the
  booth.
- **Mobile money** - M-Pesa and Airtel Money.
- **Cash** in shillings at some lanes.

Tags and cards are registered at the operator's service centre, where you
show the vehicle's ownership documents, and topped up by app, USSD or mobile
money. **Never pay a toll attendant's personal M-Pesa number**; use the
official payment options only.

## The rules

- **Motorcycles and tuk-tuks are not allowed.**
- Speed limits are posted and enforced with cameras.
- Do not stop on the expressway except in an emergency. If you break down,
  stay with the vehicle, switch on hazard lights, and call the operator's
  emergency number shown on the signs.
- Some matatus and buses use it on set routes, including to JKIA.

## Is it worth it?

For the airport, Westlands-to-Mombasa Road trips and rush hour, usually yes.
Off-peak, the roads below may be nearly as fast. See
[JKIA airport guide](/guides/jkia-airport-guide) and
[Driving in Nairobi](/guides/driving-in-nairobi).
