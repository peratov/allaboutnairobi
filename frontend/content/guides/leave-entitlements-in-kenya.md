The **Employment Act 2007** sets the minimum leave for every employee in
Kenya, whether you work in an office, a shop or a home. A contract can give
you more, but never less. Work out your own balance in the
[leave calculator](/tools/leave-calculator).

## Annual leave

- **{{ ANNUAL_LEAVE_DAYS }} working days** of paid leave for every twelve
  months of continuous work.
- Weekends and public holidays inside your leave do not count against it.
- You are entitled to take at least **two weeks in a row**. The rest can be
  taken later by agreement, but should be used within 18 months.
- Your employer should not pay you instead of letting you take leave while
  you are still employed. When you **leave a job**, unused leave must be paid
  out in your final dues.

## Sick leave

After **two months** of continuous work, you are entitled each year to:

- **7 days on full pay**, then
- **7 days on half pay**

You must produce a **medical certificate** from a qualified medical
practitioner, and tell your employer as soon as you can. Many employers give
more than this minimum: check your contract.

## Maternity leave

- **{{ MATERNITY_LEAVE_MONTHS }} months on full pay**, on top of your annual
  leave.
- Give your employer at least **seven days' written notice** before you
  start, with a medical certificate.
- You have the right to return to the **same job**, or a reasonably similar
  one, on the same terms.

See [Giving birth in Nairobi](/guides/giving-birth-in-nairobi).

## Paternity leave

**{{ PATERNITY_LEAVE_WEEKS }} weeks on full pay**. Your employer can ask for
notice and proof of the birth.

## Pre-adoptive leave

When a child is placed in your care for adoption, you are entitled to
**{{ PRE_ADOPTIVE_LEAVE_MONTHS }} month on full pay**. Give written notice
before the placement, with the placement documents. It does not reduce your
annual leave.

## Public holidays

Public holidays are paid days off. If you work on one, you are owed extra -
usually double pay. See [Public holidays in Kenya](/guides/public-holidays-in-kenya)
and the [overtime calculator](/tools/overtime-calculator).

## Compassionate and study leave

The law does not set compassionate (bereavement) or study leave. Most
employers give a few days for a close family death; check your contract or
staff handbook.

## Household staff

House managers, nannies, gardeners and askaris are employees with exactly the
same leave rights. See [Hiring household staff](/guides/hiring-household-staff).

## If leave is refused

Raise it in writing and keep copies. If it is not resolved, the county
**labour office** can call a conciliation meeting. See
[Working in Kenya](/guides/working-in-kenya#when-things-go-wrong).
