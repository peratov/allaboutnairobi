House fires in Nairobi are often caused by **cooking gas**, **faulty
wiring** and **candles** during power cuts. Most are preventable, and a
little preparation saves lives.

## Cooking gas

- **Buy cylinders from reputable dealers** and check the seal. Illegally
  refilled cylinders are a real danger. See
  [Utilities in Nairobi](/guides/utilities-in-nairobi).
- **Keep the cylinder upright**, outside or in a ventilated spot, away from
  heat.
- **Check hoses and regulators** for cracks; replace old ones.
- **Turn off the cylinder** at night and when you go out.
- **If you smell gas** - don't switch lights or appliances on or off, open
  windows and doors, turn off the cylinder, and get everyone out. Call for
  help from outside.

## Electrical safety

- **Use a qualified electrician** for wiring. See
  [Finding a fundi](/guides/finding-a-fundi-in-nairobi).
- **Don't overload sockets** or chain extension leads.
- **Use surge protectors** - Nairobi's power can surge when it comes back
  after a cut.
- **Unplug** heaters, irons and chargers when not in use.
- See [Plugs, voltage and appliances](/guides/plugs-voltage-and-appliances-in-kenya)
  and [Solar and backup power](/guides/solar-and-backup-power).

## Candles, jikos and kerosene

- **Never leave candles burning** unattended, and keep them away from
  curtains. Rechargeable lights are safer during power cuts.
- **Charcoal jikos** - use them outside or with plenty of ventilation.
  Carbon monoxide from a jiko in a closed room can kill.
- **Store kerosene** and other fuels away from heat and children.

## Be prepared

- **Smoke alarm** - fit one near bedrooms and test it monthly.
- **Fire extinguisher and fire blanket** in the kitchen; learn how to use
  them. Check the extinguisher is serviced.
- **Escape plan** - know two ways out, and where keys to grilles and gates
  are kept. Burglar bars can trap people in a fire.
- **Children** - keep matches and lighters out of reach.

## If there's a fire

1. **Get everyone out** - don't stop for belongings.
2. **Call {{ EMERGENCY_NUMBER }}** for the fire brigade once you are safe.
3. **Stay low** under smoke, and close doors behind you.
4. **Don't go back in.**
5. **Never throw water on a gas or oil fire** - turn off the gas if safe,
   and use a fire blanket or extinguisher.
