Warm weather, rain and close-packed buildings mean most Nairobi homes see
pests at some point. Most can be kept down with habits and cheap products;
bedbugs and termites usually need a professional.

## The usual suspects

- **Cockroaches** - kitchens and drains. Keep food sealed, wipe crumbs,
  fix leaks, and use gel baits rather than sprays.
- **Bedbugs** - in mattresses, bed frames and sofas, often brought in on
  second-hand furniture or luggage. Wash bedding hot, vacuum, and call a
  professional - they are very hard to clear alone.
- **Mosquitoes** - worst in the rainy seasons. Nets, screens on windows,
  repellent, and no standing water in buckets, tyres or flower pots.
  Nairobi itself has little malaria, but other parts of Kenya do. See
  [Vaccinations and malaria in Kenya](/guides/vaccinations-and-malaria-in-kenya).
- **Rats and mice** - seal gaps around pipes and doors, keep rubbish in
  closed bins, and use traps. See [Waste and recycling in Nairobi](/guides/waste-and-recycling-in-nairobi).
- **Termites** - they damage wooden doors, frames and furniture, and
  houses under construction. Treatment of the soil when building, and
  professional treatment once they are in.
- **Safari ants** (siafu) - columns that march through gardens and
  sometimes houses after rain. They move on by themselves; keep children and
  pets away and block the line with ash or a barrier.
- **Fleas and ticks** - on pets. See [Pets in Nairobi](/guides/pets-in-nairobi).

## Hiring a fumigator

- **Check they are licensed** - pest control operators are licensed by the
  **Pest Control Products Board** (PCPB). Ask to see the licence.
- **Ask what they will use** - registered products only, and whether it is
  safe for children, pets and food areas.
- **Ask how long to stay out** of the house, and what to do before they come.
- **Get a written quote and a guarantee** - a good firm returns for a
  follow-up treatment, especially for bedbugs.
- **Neighbours** - in a flat, pests travel between units. Ask your
  [[caretaker]] or landlord to treat the whole block.

## Renting

If a flat has pests when you move in, put it in writing to the landlord
straight away. Check for droppings, bedbug spots and cockroaches at the
viewing. See [Tenant rights in Kenya](/guides/tenant-rights-in-kenya) and
[Finding housing](/guides/finding-housing).

## Using products safely

Buy registered products from agrovets and supermarkets, follow the label,
keep them away from children and food, and never decant poison into drink
bottles. In an emergency, call **{{ EMERGENCY_NUMBER }}**.
