Football is Kenya's most popular sport. Nairobi has the country's biggest
clubs and stadiums, and on weekends bars across the city fill up for
English and European matches.

## Kenyan Premier League

- **Gor Mahia** and **AFC Leopards** are the two most famous clubs, with
  huge followings. Their meeting, the **Mashemeji derby**, is the biggest
  match in Kenyan football.
- Other top-flight clubs include **Tusker**, **KCB**, **Kenya Police** and
  others from Nairobi and around the country.
- The season usually starts around **August or September** and runs into
  the middle of the next year.

## Stadiums

- **Nyayo National Stadium** - near the city centre on Langata Road.
- **Moi International Sports Centre, Kasarani** - the national stadium off
  Thika Road.
- **Kenyatta Stadium** in Machakos and smaller grounds such as
  **Dandora** host league matches.

Venues change with renovations and availability, so check where your match
is. See the map for [Nyayo](/map#place/nyayo-national-stadium) and
[Kasarani](/map#place/kasarani-stadium).

## Tickets

- League tickets are **cheap**. Buy them through the clubs' and league's
  official channels - often online with [[M-Pesa]] - or at the gate.
- For big matches and internationals, **buy ahead** from official sellers;
  fakes circulate.

## Harambee Stars

Kenya's national team plays at Nyayo and Kasarani. See
[Harambee Stars and AFCON](/guides/harambee-stars-and-afcon) and

## Matchday tips

- **Arrive early** - queues and security checks take time, and big games
  fill up.
- **Wear club colours** in the home or away sections only - keep it
  friendly.
- **Carry little** - ID, phone and M-Pesa. Bags may be checked.
- **Getting home** - plan your ride before the final whistle; roads around
  stadiums jam. See [Safety in Nairobi](/guides/safety-in-nairobi).

## Watching European football

Sports bars and pubs across Nairobi show the **Premier League**, Champions
