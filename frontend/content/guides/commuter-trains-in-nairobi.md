Kenya Railways runs **commuter trains** from **Nairobi Central Station** to
the suburbs. They are cheap and avoid the traffic, but run mostly at rush
hour - so they suit regular commuters who live near a station.

## The lines

- **Eastern line** - towards **Makadara**, **Donholm** and **Embakasi
  Village**.
- **Northern line** - through **Dandora**, **Mwiki**, **Githurai** and
  **Kahawa** to **Ruiru**.
- **Western line** - through **Kibera** and **Dagoretti** to **Kikuyu**
  and **Limuru**.
- **Syokimau line** - towards **Imara Daima** and **Syokimau**, near the
  SGR terminus. This service has been suspended at times, so check before
  you travel.

Services, stops and days change - Kenya Railways has added new stations and
sometimes reduces services during engineering works - so check the current
timetable on its official channels before you rely on a train.

## Timetables

- Most trains run in the **morning and evening rush hours**, into town in
  the morning and out in the evening.
- There are few or no trains in the middle of the day and on Sundays on
  some lines.
- Trains can be delayed; leave a margin for important appointments.

## Tickets

- Fares are **low**, set by distance, and among the cheapest ways to travel.
- Buy tickets at the station or with cashless payment where offered. Keep
  your ticket for inspection.
- Fares are reviewed from time to time; check at the station.

## Tips

- **Trains get very full** at peak times. Keep bags in front and phones
  secure.
- **Stations** - many are in busy areas; walk with others in the dark.
- **Getting to the station** - a [[boda boda]] or short matatu ride is
  common. See [Boda bodas in Nairobi](/guides/boda-bodas-in-nairobi).
- **Nairobi Central Station** is on the edge of the CBD; walk or take a
  short ride to offices.

## The SGR and longer trains

- The **Madaraka Express** to Mombasa leaves from the **Nairobi Terminus**
  at Syokimau. See [Nairobi to Mombasa](/guides/nairobi-to-mombasa).
- Kenya Railways also runs some **longer-distance services** on the old
  metre-gauge line, such as to Nyahururu.
