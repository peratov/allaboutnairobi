Many Kenyans support ageing parents - often from Nairobi while the parents
live [[upcountry]], or from abroad. Planning early for health, money and
daily care makes it easier for everyone.

## Health

- **SHA** - make sure your parents are registered and contributions are up
  to date. See [Registering for SHA](/guides/registering-for-sha).
- **Private cover** - some insurers offer plans for older people, but
  cover is limited and expensive after a certain age. See
  [Health insurance in Kenya](/guides/health-insurance-in-kenya).
- **Regular checks** - blood pressure, blood sugar, eyes and hearing. See
  [Diabetes and high blood pressure](/guides/diabetes-and-high-blood-pressure-in-nairobi)
  and [Health checks and screening](/guides/health-checks-and-screening-in-nairobi).
- **Medicines** - set up a routine for refills and doses; a weekly pill box
  helps.

## Help at home

- **A carer or house help** - for many families, the main option. Agree
  duties, pay and time off in writing. See
  [Hiring household staff](/guides/hiring-household-staff).
- **Home-based care services** - agencies send trained caregivers or
  nurses, by the day or living in.
- **Adapting the home** - grab rails, good lighting, non-slip floors and a
  ground-floor bedroom prevent falls.
- **Phones** - a simple phone with key numbers saved, and a regular call.

## Care homes

There are a small number of **homes for the elderly** in and around
Nairobi, run by churches, charities and private operators. Visit before
choosing, and ask about staff, medical care, food and fees.

## Money

- **Inua Jamii** - the government's **Older Persons Cash Transfer** pays
  **KES {{ OLDER_PERSONS_CASH_TRANSFER|shillings }} a month** to eligible people
  aged **{{ OLDER_PERSONS_CASH_TRANSFER_AGE }} and over** who are not on a
  pension. Ask at the local social development office or chief's office
  about registering.
- **Pensions** - help them claim NSSF or other pensions. See
  [Checking your NSSF statement](/guides/checking-your-nssf-statement) and
  [Pensions in Kenya](/guides/pensions-in-kenya).
- **Sending money** - [[M-Pesa]] makes regular support simple. From abroad,
  see [Sending money to Kenya](/guides/sending-money-to-kenya).
- **Scams** - older people are often targeted by phone scams. Warn them
  never to share PINs or codes. See [M-Pesa fraud and scams](/guides/mpesa-fraud-and-scams-in-kenya).

## Planning ahead

- Encourage a **will**, and know where documents are - title deeds, ID,
  bank and insurance details. See [Making a will in Kenya](/guides/making-a-will-in-kenya).
- A **power of attorney** lets a trusted person act for them if needed.
  See [Power of attorney in Kenya](/guides/power-of-attorney-in-kenya).
- Talk as a family about who does what - visits, money, decisions - before
  a crisis.
