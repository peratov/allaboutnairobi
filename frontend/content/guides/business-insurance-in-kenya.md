A shop, salon, restaurant or small office in Nairobi faces the same risks as
a large company - fire, theft, an injured customer, a hurt employee - with far
less cushion. Two covers are required by law; the rest depend on what you do.
General information, not advice.

## What the law requires

- **Work injury cover (WIBA)** for anyone you employ. See
  [Work injury insurance](/guides/work-injury-insurance-wiba).
- **Third-party motor cover** on every vehicle the business uses on the road.
  See [Car insurance](/guides/car-insurance-in-kenya). Matatus, boda bodas and
  other vehicles carrying passengers or goods for hire need **commercial**
  cover, not private.

Some sectors need more - a licence or a lease may require fire cover or
public liability, and banks require cover on anything they finance.

## The covers most businesses buy

- **Fire and perils** for the premises (if you own them), fittings and stock.
- **Burglary** for stock and equipment, usually needing proof of forced entry.
- **Money cover** for cash on the premises and in transit to the bank.
- **Public liability**, if a customer or passer-by is hurt or their property
  is damaged because of your business.
- **Goods in transit**, for stock moving between supplier, shop and customer.
- **Professional indemnity** for consultants, accountants, engineers,
  architects and others whose advice can cost a client money.
- **Business interruption**, which pays lost profit while you cannot trade
  after an insured event like a fire.

Many insurers sell an **SME package** that combines several of these at a
better price than buying each alone.

## Buying it well

- **Use a licensed broker** for anything beyond the basics - they compare
  insurers and handle claims. Check their licence with the
  **Insurance Regulatory Authority**.
- **Value stock and equipment properly**. Under-insuring triggers
  **average**, which cuts every claim in proportion.
- **Keep stock records and receipts** - after a fire or burglary, the claim is
  only as good as your books. [eTIMS invoices](/guides/etims-for-small-businesses)
  help here.
- **Check the security conditions**. Burglary cover often requires grilles,
  locks or a night guard; break them and the claim fails.
