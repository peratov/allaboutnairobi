**Kisumu** is Kenya's third city, on the shore of **Lake Victoria** in the
west. It is warmer and slower than Nairobi, famous for fried tilapia by the
lake, and an easy weekend away - by plane in about an hour, or overland
through the Rift Valley.

## Getting there

- **Fly** - Kenya Airways and Jambojet fly from JKIA, and there are smaller
  airlines too. The flight takes about an hour. See
  [JKIA airport guide](/guides/jkia-airport-guide).
- **Bus** - several coach companies run day and overnight buses from
  central Nairobi, taking roughly six to seven hours. Book ahead at
  Christmas and Easter.
- **Drive** - about five to six hours via Nakuru, on a busy road. Leave
  early, and watch for lorries on the climbs. See
  [Driving in Nairobi](/guides/driving-in-nairobi).
- **The train** - Kenya Railways runs the **Kisumu Safari Train**, an
  overnight metre-gauge service from Nairobi Central Station, usually weekly
  with extra trips at holiday times. Check the current days and fares with
  Kenya Railways before you plan around it. See
  [Commuter trains in Nairobi](/guides/commuter-trains-in-nairobi).

## What to do

- **Eat fish by the lake** - whole fried or stewed tilapia with
  [[ugali]] and greens at **Dunga beach** or along the shore.
- **Watch the sunset** over the water from **Hippo Point** and the Dunga
  area; boat trips go out to look for hippos.
- **Kisumu Impala Sanctuary** - a small Kenya Wildlife Service sanctuary on
  the lakeshore with impala, giraffe and a walking trail.
- **Kit Mikayi** - a striking rock formation outside the city, tied to Luo
  legend.
- **Ndere Island** - a national park island in the lake, reached by boat.
- **The markets and the old town** - Kibuye market on Sundays is one of the
  biggest in the region.

## Practical tips

- **It is hotter and more humid** than Nairobi - bring light clothes.
- **Malaria** is present around the lake. Use repellent and a net, and ask
  a doctor about prevention. See
  [Vaccinations and malaria in Kenya](/guides/vaccinations-and-malaria-in-kenya).
- **Don't swim in the lake** - bilharzia is a risk, and so are hippos.
- **Learn a few words of Dholuo**, the local language - a greeting goes a
  long way.
