Most of Kenyan bureaucracy now runs through one website, and most private
transactions ask for one number. Get these sorted first and everything else
gets easier.

## eCitizen

[[eCitizen]] (ecitizen.go.ke) is the government's services portal. Passports,
driving licences, good conduct certificates, business names, land searches and
many county and national fees go through it. Payment is by M-Pesa or card.

- **Citizens** sign up with their national ID or [[Maisha Namba]].
- **Foreign residents** sign up with a passport or foreigner certificate.

Keep the email address and phone number on the account current: one-time codes
go to them.

## KRA PIN

A [[KRA PIN]] is your tax number. You will be asked for it by your employer,
your bank, a landlord's agent, an insurer, a car seller and the land registry.

Register free on [[iTax]] (itax.kra.go.ke). Citizens need their ID. Foreigners
generally need a work or residence permit or a foreigner certificate first.
Registration gives you a PIN certificate as a PDF: save it, because people will
ask for "a copy of your PIN".

Having a PIN means you must **file a return every year by
{{ TAX_RETURN_DEADLINE }}**, even if you owe nothing. See
[PAYE and taxes](/guides/paye-and-taxes#filing-your-return).

## Your ID

- **Kenyan citizens** are moving from the old national ID card to the
  **Maisha Card**, issued with a Maisha Namba.
- **Foreign nationals** on a permit or pass register with the Directorate of
  Immigration Services and receive a **foreigner certificate**. It is the ID a
  bank, employer or landlord expects from a resident foreigner. Carry a copy of
  your passport and permit too.

## Certificate of good conduct

A [[good conduct certificate]] is a police clearance from the Directorate of
Criminal Investigations. Employers, some landlords and immigration ask for one.

1. Apply and pay on eCitizen.
2. Have your fingerprints taken at DCI headquarters on Kiambu Road, or at a
   [[Huduma Centre]] that offers the service. Bring your ID or passport and
   the payment confirmation.
3. Download the certificate from eCitizen when it is ready. It can take a
   couple of weeks.

## Huduma Centres

A [[Huduma Centre]] is a one-stop government office. The one in the GPO
building in the CBD is the busiest in Nairobi. Staff can help you with an
eCitizen application if you are stuck. Go early in the day.

## Tips

- **Pay only through eCitizen or official paybills.** Anyone offering to
  "speed up" a document for cash is asking for [[kitu kidogo]].
- **Keep PDFs of everything** - PIN certificate, permit, passport page and
  payment receipts - on your phone and in the cloud.
