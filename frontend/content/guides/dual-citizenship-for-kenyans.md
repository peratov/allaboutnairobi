Since the 2010 Constitution, Kenya **allows dual citizenship**. A Kenyan by
birth who becomes a citizen of another country does not lose Kenyan
citizenship - but must **declare** the other citizenship to the Department of
Immigration.

## Who this is for

- **Kenyans who took another citizenship after 2010** - you are still Kenyan,
  and need to file a declaration.
- **Kenyans who lost citizenship before 2010** - under the old Constitution,
  taking another citizenship ended your Kenyan one. You can **apply to regain**
  it, and then declare the other citizenship.
- **Children born abroad to a Kenyan parent** - they are Kenyan by birth.
  Register the birth with the Kenyan mission and apply for their documents.

Before acting, check what your **other country** allows. Some countries end
your citizenship there if you take or keep another.

## Declaring dual citizenship

The law requires you to declare another citizenship within
**{{ DUAL_CITIZENSHIP_DISCLOSURE_MONTHS }} months** of acquiring it. Not
declaring is an offence, so if you are late, file now rather than not at all.

1. Fill in the **declaration of dual citizenship** form (Form 3) online on the
   immigration portal, reached through [[eCitizen]].
2. Pay the application fee shown on the portal.
3. Submit the printed form and supporting documents at **Nyayo House** in
   Nairobi or at a **Kenyan embassy or high commission** abroad.

Expect to need copies of both passports, your Kenyan ID or birth
certificate, your foreign naturalisation certificate, and passport photos.
Some missions ask for notarised copies - check with yours.

## Regaining Kenyan citizenship

If you lost Kenyan citizenship before 2010, apply to regain it with the
**regaining citizenship** form (Form 5), together with the dual citizenship
declaration. Embassies use your date of birth to decide which forms you need,
so check your mission's instructions before you apply. Processing can take
months.

## After approval

- Apply for a **Kenyan passport** - see
  [Applying for a Kenyan passport](/guides/applying-for-a-kenyan-passport).
- Enter and leave Kenya on your **Kenyan passport** - you then need no visa
  or [[ETA]] for Kenya.
- Keep your **ID**, [[KRA PIN]] and other records up to date. See
  [eCitizen, KRA PIN and IDs](/guides/ecitizen-kra-pin-and-ids).

## Things to know

- Dual citizens can own **freehold land** in Kenya like any other citizen.
- Some **state and public offices** are closed to dual citizens unless they
  give up the other citizenship.
- Use only official immigration sites and Kenyan missions. Agents charging
  to "fast-track" citizenship are not needed.
