Kenya sets minimum wages by **occupation** and by **area**. Nairobi falls in
the highest zone, with Mombasa, Kisumu, Nakuru and Eldoret. The rates rose by
12% from **1 May 2026** under the Regulation of Wages (General) (Amendment)
Order, 2026.

## Nairobi rates from 1 May 2026

Monthly basic minimum wages in the cities zone, before housing allowance:

- **General labourer** (including house workers, cleaners, gardeners, day
  guards and messengers): KES {{ NAIROBI_MIN_WAGE_GENERAL_LABOURER|shillings }}
- **Waiters, cooks, turnboys and similar:** KES
  {{ MIN_WAGE_WAITER_COOK|shillings }}
- **Night watchman:** KES {{ MIN_WAGE_NIGHT_WATCHMAN|shillings }}
- **Telephone operator, receptionist, storekeeper:** KES
  {{ MIN_WAGE_RECEPTIONIST|shillings }}
- **Tailor, driver of a medium vehicle:** KES
  {{ MIN_WAGE_DRIVER_MEDIUM|shillings }}
- **Cashier, heavy commercial vehicle driver, Grade I artisan:** KES
  {{ MIN_WAGE_CASHIER|shillings }}

These are rounded to the shilling. The order lists many more occupations -
check the full Legal Notice on Kenya Law for any job not shown here.

## Housing allowance

The minimum is **basic pay**. If the employer does not provide housing, it
must add a **housing allowance** of {{ HOUSING_ALLOWANCE_RATE|percent }}% of
basic pay on top.

## Who is covered

- **Most private-sector workers** paid under the general order, including
  domestic workers. See [Hiring household staff](/guides/hiring-household-staff).
- **Agricultural workers** have a separate order with their own rates.
- **Some sectors** have their own agreements (collective bargaining
  agreements) that set higher pay.

Minimum wage is before deductions. PAYE is usually nil at this level, but NSSF,
SHIF and the Housing Levy are still deducted. See the
[household staff calculator](/tools/household-staff-calculator) and the
[salary calculator](/tools/salary-calculator).

## Arrears

The 2026 order was published after 1 May but applies from that date, so
workers paid the old rate from May are owed the difference.

## If you are paid less

1. **Raise it with your employer** in writing, showing the rate.
2. **Contact the Labour Office** - the county labour officer can investigate
   and help resolve it.
3. **The Employment and Labour Relations Court** handles unresolved claims.

Paying below the minimum wage is an offence, and underpayments can be
claimed back.
