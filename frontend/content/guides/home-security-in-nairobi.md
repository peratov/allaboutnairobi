Most homes in Nairobi's middle- and upper-income areas have layers of
security: a gate and guard, an alarm linked to a response company, and often
an electric fence. How much you need depends on the type of home and the area.

## Apartments

Most apartment blocks already have a **gate, [[askari]]s, CCTV and controlled
access**, paid for through the service charge. Before renting, check:

- Guards are on duty **day and night**.
- **Visitors are checked** and signed in.
- There is **secure parking**.
- Your own door has a **good lock**, and ground-floor windows have grilles.

## Houses

Houses usually need their own arrangements:

- **A guard** at the gate, day and night, or at night only.
- **An alarm** with panic buttons, linked to a **response company** that sends
  a car when it goes off.
- **Electric fencing** on the perimeter wall.
- **Security lights** and **CCTV**.
- **Burglar bars and a secure inner door** to the bedrooms (a "safe haven").

In many areas, residents' associations organise shared guards and street
patrols.

## Choosing a security company

- **Licensing** - private security companies must be registered with the
  **Private Security Regulatory Authority (PSRA)**.
- **Response time** - ask how many cars cover your area and how fast they
  arrive. Ask neighbours.
- **Guards' pay and conditions** - guards must be paid at least the minimum
  wage. Companies paying below it tend to have tired, unmotivated staff. See
  [Minimum wage](/guides/minimum-wage-in-kenya).
- **Contract terms** - notice period, what is included, and who pays for
  false alarms.

Large national companies and many smaller local firms operate in Nairobi.

## Everyday habits

- **Know your guards** by name, and tell them who you are expecting.
- **Don't let anyone in** who claims to be from Kenya Power, the landlord or the
  county without checking first.
- **Test the alarm and panic buttons** every month.
- **Keep valuables out of sight** from the gate and windows.
- **Vary routines** if you are concerned, and keep emergency numbers handy.
