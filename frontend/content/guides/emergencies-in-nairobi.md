Emergencies are easier to handle if you prepare before one happens. Save the
numbers below, know where your nearest emergency department is, and keep a
few documents to hand.

## The numbers

- **Police, fire and ambulance:** **{{ EMERGENCY_NUMBER }}**, or **112** from
  any mobile.
- **Kenya Red Cross emergency line:** **{{ RED_CROSS_EMERGENCY }}**, toll free.
- **Gender-based violence helpline:** **1195**, toll free, 24 hours.
- **Childline Kenya:** **116**, for children at risk.
- **Kenya Power** (fallen lines, power faults): **97771**.

Also save:

- **A private ambulance service** - many private hospitals and companies run
  their own, and some insurers include ambulance cover.
- **Your building's security** or response company.
- **Your embassy**, if you are a foreign national.

## Medical emergencies

1. **Call an ambulance**, or if it is faster and safe, drive or take a taxi to
   the nearest emergency department. In Nairobi traffic, the closest capable
   hospital is often the best choice.
2. **Take your ID, insurance or SHA details**, and a list of medicines and
   allergies.
3. **Private hospitals** stabilise emergencies, but may ask for a deposit or
   insurance approval to admit you.

See [Nairobi's main hospitals](/guides/hospitals-in-nairobi).

## Fire

Get out and call **{{ EMERGENCY_NUMBER }}**. Know the fire exits in your
building, keep a fire extinguisher and blanket in the kitchen, and never block
the exits with padlocked grilles.

## Crime

- **Do not resist** armed robbers.
- **Report to the nearest police station** and get an **OB number** - needed
  for insurance and replacing documents.
- See [Reporting a crime and lost documents](/guides/reporting-a-crime-and-lost-documents).

## Gender-based violence

Call **1195** for confidential support, or go to a hospital. Survivors of
sexual violence should seek medical care within **72 hours** for emergency
treatment. Police stations have gender desks.

## Road accidents

Move to safety if you can, call for help, and don't move injured people unless
they are in danger. Take photos and details, and report to the police. See
[Car insurance](/guides/car-insurance-in-kenya#making-a-claim).

## Be ready

- **Save the numbers now.**
- **Know your nearest emergency department** from home and work.
- **Keep copies** of your ID, passport and insurance on your phone and in the
  cloud.
- **Tell someone** your plans when travelling outside the city.
