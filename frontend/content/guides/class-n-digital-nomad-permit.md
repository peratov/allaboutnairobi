Kenya's **Class N permit** lets foreign remote workers live in Kenya while
working for employers or clients **outside** Kenya. It is the right status for
digital nomads staying longer than a visit - working remotely on a visitor's
entry is not allowed.

For internet, power and coworking, see
[Working remotely from Nairobi](/guides/working-remotely-from-nairobi).

## Who qualifies

- You work remotely for an **employer outside Kenya**, or run your own
  business with **clients outside Kenya**.
- You earn at least about **US$ {{ DIGITAL_NOMAD_MIN_INCOME_USD|count }} a
  year** from outside Kenya, as reported in 2026.
- You do **not** plan to work for Kenyan employers or clients.

## Documents

Usually:

- **Passport** valid for at least six months, and a passport photo.
- **Proof of remote work** - an employment contract or letter from a foreign
  employer, or contracts with foreign clients and business registration.
- **Proof of income** - bank statements for recent months.
- **Police clearance** from your home country or country of residence.
- **Proof of accommodation** in Kenya.
- **Health insurance** covering your stay.

## Fees

The permit has a processing fee and an annual issuance fee, charged in US
dollars, as shown on the eFNS portal when you apply. Reported fees in 2026 are
around US$200 to process and US$1,000 a year. Check the portal, which is
what counts.

## Applying

1. Create an account on **eFNS**, the Directorate of Immigration Services'
   foreign nationals portal on [[eCitizen]].
2. Choose the **Class N** permit and complete the form.
3. Upload the documents and pay the processing fee.
4. Wait for the decision, then pay the issuance fee.
5. Have the permit endorsed and register as a foreign national for a
   **foreigner certificate**. See
   [eCitizen, KRA PIN and IDs](/guides/ecitizen-kra-pin-and-ids).

Apply from outside Kenya, or before your visitor's entry runs out, and allow
several weeks.

## What you cannot do

- **Work for Kenyan employers or clients**, or earn from the Kenyan market.
  That needs a different permit, such as a Class D or Class G.
- **Bring family on the same permit** - spouses and children need dependant
  passes. See [Dependant passes](/guides/dependant-passes).

## Tax

Living in Kenya for 183 days or more in a year can make you tax resident here.
Get advice from someone who knows both Kenyan tax and your home country's
rules before you arrive.
