Kenyan hospitals regularly run short of blood - for mothers in childbirth,
accident victims, children with severe anaemia and people with sickle cell
disease or cancer. All of it comes from **voluntary donors**. Giving takes
less than an hour.

## Who can give

You can usually donate if you:

- are aged **{{ BLOOD_DONOR_MIN_AGE }} to {{ BLOOD_DONOR_MAX_AGE }}**
- weigh at least **{{ BLOOD_DONOR_MIN_WEIGHT_KG }} kg**
- are in **good general health** on the day

You may be asked to wait if you have recently been ill, had a tattoo or
piercing, had surgery, been pregnant, or taken certain medicines. Staff will
check your health before you donate, and everything you tell them is
confidential.

## Where to donate in Nairobi

- **The Kenya National Blood Transfusion Service** (KNBTS) runs a regional
  blood transfusion centre in Nairobi, in the grounds of **Kenyatta
  National Hospital**, and satellite centres at public hospitals.
- **Blood drives** are held regularly at malls, universities, churches,
  offices and events, often announced on social media.
- Some **private hospitals** collect blood too.

Check opening times before you go; some centres let you book a slot.

## What happens

1. **Registration** - your details and ID.
2. **A health check** - a short questionnaire, and checks of your blood
   pressure and haemoglobin (iron level) with a finger-prick.
3. **The donation** - about a pint of blood, which takes around ten minutes.
4. **Rest and a snack** - sit for a few minutes, and drink plenty
   afterwards.

Every donation is tested, and you may be told if a test finds a health
problem.

## Before and after

- **Eat a meal** and drink water beforehand.
- Avoid heavy exercise and lifting with that arm for the rest of the day.
- If you feel faint, lie down with your feet raised and tell the staff.

## How often

Your body replaces the blood within weeks. Men can usually give about
every **three months** and women about every **four months** - the centre
will tell you when you can return.

## Other ways to help

- **Organise a drive** at your workplace, church or school with KNBTS.
- If a friend or relative needs blood in hospital, the hospital may ask
  family and friends to **replace** what was used by donating.
