South-east of Nairobi, along **Mombasa Road** and the **Namanga Road**,
Syokimau, Mlolongo, Athi River and Kitengela have grown fast. They offer lower
rents, more space and new houses, close to JKIA and the SGR - with the
commute as the trade-off.

## The areas

- **Syokimau** - planned estates of maisonettes and apartments, with an SGR
  and commuter-rail station. Close to JKIA.
- **Mlolongo** - busy and cheaper, right on Mombasa Road.
- **Athi River** - industrial and residential, around the Export Processing
  Zone.
- **Kitengela** - a large, fast-growing town on the Namanga Road, with
  affordable houses and plots.

## Rents

Rough asking rents in 2026 listings:

| What | Syokimau (KES a month) | Kitengela and Mlolongo (KES a month) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| One bedroom | 15,000 - 30,000 | 10,000 - 18,000 |
| Two bedrooms | 25,000 - 50,000 | 15,000 - 30,000 |
| Maisonette or house | 45,000 - 90,000 | 25,000 - 60,000 |

## Who they suit

- **People who work at or near JKIA**, the Industrial Area or Mombasa Road.
- **Families wanting a house and garden** on a mid-range budget.
- **People buying land to build.** See
  [Buying land around Nairobi](/guides/buying-land-around-nairobi).

## Getting around

- **The Expressway** runs from Mlolongo and Syokimau to Westlands, and makes
  the trip much faster for those who can pay the toll.
- **Mombasa Road** below it is slow at peak times, especially at Mlolongo.
- **Commuter trains** run from Syokimau to the CBD.
- **Kitengela** adds the Namanga Road, which jams near Athi River.

See [Getting around Nairobi](/guides/getting-around-nairobi) and the
[commute cost calculator](/tools/commute-cost-calculator).

## Things to check

- **Water** - many homes rely on boreholes, tanks and water vendors.
- **Roads** inside estates - some are murram (dirt) and hard going in the
  rains.
- **Security** - ask about guards and the residents' association.

## Daily life

- **Shopping:** Gateway Mall and malls along Mombasa Road, and Kitengela's
  own supermarkets and markets.
- **Travel:** JKIA and the SGR's Nairobi terminus are close by.
