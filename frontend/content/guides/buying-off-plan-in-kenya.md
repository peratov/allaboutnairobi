Buying **off-plan** - before or during construction - can be cheaper than
buying a finished flat, and developers offer payment plans. But buyers carry
the risk of delays, changes and, at worst, projects that stall. These checks
reduce that risk.

## Check the developer

- **Track record** - visit projects they have **completed**, and talk to the
  people who bought there. Did they deliver on time and to the specification?
- **Company details** - search the company on the Business Registration
  Service, and check for court cases against it.
- **Who is building it** - the main contractor should be registered with the
  **National Construction Authority (NCA)** in the right category.

## Check the project

Ask for, and have your advocate review:

- **The title** to the land, with an official search showing who owns it and
  any charges. See [Land search on Ardhisasa](/guides/land-search-on-ardhisasa).
- **County building approval** - the approval letter itself, not just proof it
  was submitted.
- **NEMA** approval and **NCA** project registration.
- **Any bank financing** - if the land is charged to a bank, how buyers' units
  will be released.
- **The proposed sectional plan** and management arrangements, including the
  estimated **service charge**.

## The sale agreement

Use **your own advocate**, not the developer's. The agreement should cover:

- **The exact unit**, specification and finishes.
- **The completion date**, and what happens if it is missed - compensation,
  or the right to cancel and get your money back.
- **Payment stages** tied to construction milestones.
- **A defects liability period**, commonly twelve months, during which the
  developer fixes defects at no cost.
- **How you will get your title** - a sectional title for the unit under the
  Sectional Properties Act.

## Protecting your money

- **Pay into an escrow or stakeholder account** held by an advocate, with money
  released as milestones are certified - never into a personal account.
- **Link payments to progress** rather than to dates.
- **Visit the site**, or have someone independent check progress, before each
  payment.
- **Keep every receipt.**

## Warning signs

- **Pressure to pay a deposit today** for a "last unit" or discount.
- **No approvals** yet, or the developer will not share documents.
- **A price well below similar finished flats** nearby.
- **Construction that has stopped** or slowed for months.
