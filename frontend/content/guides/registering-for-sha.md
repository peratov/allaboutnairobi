The [[SHA]] (Social Health Authority) replaced NHIF in October 2024. Every
Kenyan resident is expected to register, and hospitals check your SHA status
before treating you under the scheme. Registration is free.

## Before you start

You need:

- **Your national ID, Maisha Card or foreigner certificate** number.
- **The phone number registered in your name**, because the one-time codes go
  to it.
- **Your spouse's and children's details** if you want to add them - ID
  numbers for adults, birth certificate numbers for children.

## Register by phone (*147#)

This works on any phone, without data.

1. Dial **\*147#** and choose **SHA registration**.
2. Enter your ID number and your first name exactly as it appears on the ID.
3. Accept the terms and set a four-digit PIN.
4. Answer the questions on marital status and employment.

You should get a confirmation SMS. If the name does not match, it is usually a
spelling difference with the registration records - try the name exactly as it
is printed on the card.

## Register online

1. Go to **afyayangu.go.ke** (or sha.go.ke, which links to it).
2. Choose **Register individual** and your ID type.
3. Enter your ID number and phone number, then the code sent by SMS.
4. Complete your profile, including your household.

The Afya Yangu site is also where you add dependants later, update your
details and see your contribution history.

## What you pay

- **Employed:** {{ SHIF_RATE|percent }}% of gross pay, deducted by your
  employer.
- **Self-employed or informal:** {{ SHIF_RATE|percent }}% of household income
  as assessed by SHA's means test, and at least KES
  {{ SHIF_MINIMUM|shillings }} a month.
- **Unable to pay:** assessed through the same means test; the government may
  pay for the poorest households.

**Employees** do not need to pay separately: the deduction on your payslip is
your contribution. Check it appears on your statement. Work out your net pay
with the [salary calculator](/tools/salary-calculator).

**Self-employed people** complete a short **means-testing** questionnaire on
Afya Yangu (under your profile, set income to self-employed). SHA uses the
answers to set your contribution. Contributions are due in advance, and
cover only starts once you are paid up, so do not wait until you need a
hospital.

## Paying

Pay through SHA's M-Pesa paybill as shown on the Afya Yangu site or by
dialling \*147#. Use your ID number as the account number, and keep the
confirmation messages.

## Checking your status

- **By phone:** dial \*147# and choose the option to check status.
- **Online:** log in to Afya Yangu and look at your membership and
  contribution record.

A hospital will usually check your status at the front desk. If you are shown
as inactive, the most common reasons are unpaid contributions, an employer
that has not remitted deductions, or dependants who were never added.

## Adding your family

Your spouse and children are covered under your contribution once they are
added as dependants. Add them on Afya Yangu, or at a [[Huduma Centre]] if the
online form will not accept their details.

## What SHA covers

SHA pays for primary care at registered facilities, a set of hospital
services, and emergency and chronic-illness funds. It does not cover
everything, and not every private hospital in Nairobi accepts it for every
service. Ask the hospital what SHA will pay before a planned procedure. See
[Healthcare in Nairobi](/guides/healthcare-in-nairobi).
