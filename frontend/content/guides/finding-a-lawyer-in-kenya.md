For land deals, contracts, court cases, wills and many official papers you
will need an **advocate** - a lawyer admitted to the High Court and
licensed to practise. Fake lawyers exist, so always check before you hand
over documents or money.

## Checking an advocate

- Search the advocate's name on the **Law Society of Kenya** (LSK) online
  portal. It shows whether they hold a **current practising certificate**
  ("active") for this year - or are inactive, suspended or struck off.
- Practising certificates are **renewed every year**, so check the current
  year.
- **Meet at their office** and check it matches the details given.

## Choosing the right one

- **Pick a specialist** - conveyancing (land and property), family,
  employment, criminal, commercial or immigration law.
- **Ask people you trust** for recommendations.
- **Ask about experience** with cases like yours and who will actually do
  the work.

## Fees

- Advocates' minimum fees for many kinds of work are set by the
  **Advocates Remuneration Order**. For land transactions, fees are often a
  percentage of the property value.
- **Ask for a written fee quote** and an **engagement letter** setting out
  the work, fees and expenses before work starts.
- **Get receipts** for every payment.

## Client money

- For property and other deals, money is often paid through the advocate's
  **client account**. Pay only into an account in the **firm's name**, and
  confirm the details by phone at a number you already know - fraudsters
  fake emails with new bank details.
- Ask for statements of client money held.

## If you can't afford a lawyer

- **Free legal aid** - through the National Legal Aid Service, clinics and
  NGOs. See [Free legal aid in Kenya](/guides/free-legal-aid-in-kenya).
- **Small claims court** - you can represent yourself. See
  [Small claims court in Kenya](/guides/small-claims-court-in-kenya).

## Complaints

If an advocate is negligent, overcharges or withholds your money or
documents, complain to the **Advocates Complaints Commission**, or for
serious misconduct the **Advocates Disciplinary Tribunal**. Keep copies of
everything.
