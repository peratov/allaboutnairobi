Car hire is widely available in Nairobi, from international brands at JKIA
to local companies that deliver to your door. Many visitors hire a car **with
a driver**, which costs little more and removes the stress of Nairobi traffic.

## Self-drive or with a driver?

**With a driver** makes sense for:

- **City driving** - Nairobi's traffic, roundabouts and matatus are hard work
  for newcomers.
- **Safaris** - driver-guides know the roads and parks.
- **Business trips** - a driver waits while you are in meetings.

**Self-drive** suits people who are confident on busy roads, staying longer,
or travelling outside the city on their own schedule.

## What you need

- **A valid driving licence.** Visitors can drive on a foreign licence for a
  limited period; an **international driving permit** is recommended. Residents
  need a Kenyan licence. See [Getting a Kenyan driving licence](/guides/getting-a-kenyan-driving-licence).
- **A passport or ID.**
- **A credit or debit card** for the deposit.
- **A minimum age**, often 23 or 25, and a minimum time holding a licence.

## Choosing a car

- **Saloon or small SUV** - fine for the city and main roads.
- **4x4 (such as a Land Cruiser or Prado)** - for national parks, rough roads
  and the rainy season. Some parks are hard going without one.
- **Safari vehicles with pop-up roofs** - usually hired with a driver-guide.

## What it costs

Prices depend on the car, the season and the length of hire. Daily rates for
small cars are lowest; 4x4s cost several times more. A driver adds a daily fee,
plus meals and accommodation on overnight trips. Ask for a **weekly or
monthly rate** for long hires.

## Insurance and deposits

- **Check what cover is included** - comprehensive insurance, the **excess**
  you pay in an accident, and whether theft and windscreen damage are covered.
- **Ask about excess reduction** if the excess is high.
- **Deposits** are usually held on a card and released after the car is
  returned.

## Before you drive away

- **Inspect the car** with the agent and photograph every scratch.
- **Check the spare tyre, jack and tools**, the lights and the air
  conditioning.
- **Confirm the fuel policy** - usually full to full.
- **Save the company's emergency number.**
- **Check where you may drive** - some companies restrict certain roads or
  crossing borders.

## On the road

Drive defensively, avoid driving at night outside the city, and keep doors
locked and valuables out of sight in traffic. Park in secure car parks. See
[Driving in Nairobi](/guides/driving-in-nairobi).
