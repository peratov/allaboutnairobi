Kenya has more mobile borrowing than almost anywhere. Loans arrive in seconds
on your phone - which is why they are useful in an emergency, and why they
become expensive when used every month.

## The main options

- **[[Fuliza]]** - an overdraft on M-Pesa. When your balance is too low to pay,
  Fuliza covers the gap. There is an access fee, then a **daily fee** for every
  day the overdraft is unpaid.
- **M-Shwari** - a loan from NCBA through M-Pesa, with a **facilitation fee of
  {{ MSHWARI_FEE|percent }}%** for a 30-day loan.
- **KCB M-Pesa** - loans from KCB Bank through M-Pesa, over one to a few
  months.
- **Digital loan apps** - many lenders offer loans through smartphone apps.
  They must be licensed by the **Central Bank of Kenya** as digital credit
  providers.

## The true cost

Fees look small because they are quoted per loan, not per year. A
{{ MSHWARI_FEE|percent }}% fee for one month is very roughly **90% a year** if you
borrow like that every month. Fuliza's daily fee on a small overdraft left for
weeks can be even more.

Compare with a bank personal loan at a fraction of that, or a SACCO loan. Work
out any loan's cost with the
[loan repayment calculator](/tools/loan-repayment-calculator).

## Your credit record

Mobile lenders report to the credit reference bureaus. An unpaid Fuliza or app
loan can lead to a **negative listing** that makes bank loans harder and can
block some jobs. See [Checking your CRB status](/guides/checking-your-crb-status).

Repaying on time builds a record that can raise your limits and help you
borrow elsewhere.

## Using them well

- **For emergencies only**, and repaid within days.
- **Do not borrow from one app to repay another.**
- **Check the lender is on the CBK list** of licensed digital credit
  providers.
- **Opt out of Fuliza** if it tempts you, through the M-Pesa menu.

## Unlicensed lenders and harassment

Licensed lenders may not shame borrowers, contact people in your phone book, or
threaten you. If a lender does, report it to the Central Bank of Kenya and the
Office of the Data Protection Commissioner.

## Cheaper alternatives

- **A SACCO** - loans as a multiple of your savings, at lower rates. See
  [SACCOs in Nairobi](/guides/saccos-in-nairobi).
- **A salary advance** from your employer.
- **An emergency fund** in a money market fund. See
  [Money market funds](/guides/money-market-funds-in-kenya).
