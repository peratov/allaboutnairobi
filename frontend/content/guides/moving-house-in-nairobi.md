Most people in Nairobi move every year or two. A local move is simple if you
book early, agree the price in writing, and deal with both landlords before
moving day.

## Your options

- **A moving company** - packing, loading, transport, unpacking and often
  insurance. Best for families and fragile or valuable items.
- **A truck and loaders** - you pack; they load, drive and unload. Much
  cheaper, and common for smaller moves.
- **A pickup or small lorry** - for a bedsitter or a few items. Available
  through transport apps and from truck stages.

## What affects the cost

- **How much you have** - the number of rooms and bulky items.
- **Distance and traffic** - moving across town at rush hour takes longer.
- **Floors and lifts** - stairs with no lift take more people and time.
- **Packing** - full packing costs more than transport only.
- **The day** - month ends and weekends are busiest.

Get quotes from two or three movers after they have seen your home, in person
or by video, and confirm the final price, what is included, and the payment
terms **in writing**.

## Before moving day

- **Give notice** to your landlord as your lease requires, in writing.
- **Book the mover** early, especially for the last days of the month.
- **Tell the caretakers** at both buildings, and book the lift if needed.
  Some buildings only allow moves at set times.
- **Arrange utilities** - buy [[tokens]] for the new meter, and arrange
  internet. See [Home internet](/guides/home-internet-in-nairobi).
- **Pack valuables and documents** yourself, and carry them with you.

## Leaving the old home

- **Clean and fix** small damage.
- **Do the exit inspection** with the landlord or caretaker, and take dated
  photos.
- **Pay the final bills** and hand back keys and access cards.
- **Ask for the deposit** in writing, less any itemised deductions. See
  [Tenant rights](/guides/tenant-rights-in-kenya).

## On moving day

- **Be there** at both ends.
- **Check items off** as they are loaded and unloaded.
- **Report damage** straight away, with photos.
- **Pay once the job is done**, by M-Pesa or bank, and get a receipt.

## Settling in

Change your address with your bank, employer, insurer and KRA, and check the
new neighbourhood's security arrangements. See
[Setting up your home](/guides/setting-up-your-home).
