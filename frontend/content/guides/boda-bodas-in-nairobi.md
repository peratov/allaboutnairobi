A [[boda boda]] - a motorbike taxi - is the fastest way through Nairobi's
traffic for short trips, and in many estates it is how people get from the
[[stage]] to their door. It is also the riskiest way to travel, so it pays
to ride smart.

## Getting a boda

- **At a stage** - most estates, malls and matatu stages have a boda stage.
  Riders at an organised stage are usually known to each other and their
  group.
- **On an app** - Uber, Bolt and Little offer boda rides in many areas, with
  the price set upfront and the rider's details recorded. See
  [Ride-hailing and delivery apps](/guides/ride-hailing-and-delivery-apps-in-nairobi).
- **Regular rider** - many people keep the number of a rider they trust.

## Fares

- **Agree the fare before you get on**. Ask locals what a trip should cost.
- Fares rise at night, in rain, and for longer or hillier trips.
- Pay in cash or by [[M-Pesa]].

## Staying safe

- **Wear a helmet** - ask for one, and fasten it. Head injuries are the main
  cause of boda deaths.
- **One passenger**, sitting astride, holding on.
- Ask the rider to **slow down** if needed; get off if they ride dangerously
  or have been drinking.
- **Avoid bodas at night** in unfamiliar areas, and in heavy rain.
- Keep **bags in front** or on your back, and phones away - snatching from
  passengers happens.
- Wear **closed shoes**, and keep legs away from the hot exhaust.

## The rules

- Riders need a **driving licence** for motorcycles, an **insurance** cover
  that includes passengers, and a **reflective jacket** and helmets for
  themselves and the passenger.
- Nairobi County requires riders to register with a recognised SACCO or
  company and hold a county permit. Enforcement varies.
- Boda bodas have been **barred from parts of the CBD** at different times.
  Expect to be dropped at the edge of the centre and walk the rest.

## After an accident

- Get medical help first: call **{{ EMERGENCY_NUMBER }}**. See
  [Emergencies in Nairobi](/guides/emergencies-in-nairobi).
- Note the rider's name, phone, number plate and group.
- **Report to the police** and get an abstract - you need it for any
  insurance claim. See [Reporting a crime](/guides/reporting-a-crime-and-lost-documents)
  and [Insurance claims and complaints](/guides/insurance-claims-and-complaints-in-kenya).

## Working as a rider

Many young people earn a living on a boda. You will need the right licence,
insurance and county permit - see
[Getting a Kenyan driving licence](/guides/getting-a-kenyan-driving-licence)
and [Small business ideas in Nairobi](/guides/small-business-ideas-in-nairobi).
