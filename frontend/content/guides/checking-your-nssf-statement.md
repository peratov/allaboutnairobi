The [[NSSF]] (National Social Security Fund) is Kenya's national pension
scheme. Employees pay {{ NSSF_RATE|percent }}% of pensionable pay, and the
employer matches it. Checking your statement now and then catches employers
who deduct contributions but do not pay them in.

## Get your NSSF number

Your NSSF number is on your payslip, or your employer can give it to you. If
you have never registered, you can register with your ID at an NSSF office or
[[Huduma Centre]], or online through NSSF's self-service system.

## Checking your statement

- **Online** - register on the **NSSF self-service portal** (linked from
  nssf.or.ke) or the NSSF mobile app, with your ID and NSSF number, then view or
  download your statement.
- **At an NSSF office or Huduma Centre** - take your ID.

Compare the statement with your payslips. Each month you were employed should
show both your contribution and your employer's.

## Missing contributions

1. **Ask your employer** - HR or payroll - for proof of payment for the missing
   months.
2. **Report it to NSSF** if the employer does not fix it. NSSF can pursue
   employers who deducted but did not pay, with penalties.

Do this early. Chasing an employer years later, or after it has closed, is much
harder.

## Self-employed members

People without an employer can contribute voluntarily, by M-Pesa to NSSF's
paybill using their NSSF or ID number as instructed on the NSSF website. Keep
the confirmation messages.

## Benefits

- **Retirement** - at 60, or early retirement from 50.
- **Invalidity** - if you cannot work because of illness or disability.
- **Survivors** - paid to dependants when a member dies.
- **Emigration** - for members leaving Kenya permanently.
- **Funeral grant** - a contribution towards funeral costs.

## Claiming

1. Check your statement is complete.
2. Gather your **ID**, **NSSF number**, **bank details** and supporting
   documents - for retirement, a letter or certificate of service; for
   survivors, the death certificate and proof of relationship.
3. Submit the claim at an NSSF branch or Huduma Centre, or online where NSSF
   offers it.

Use only official NSSF channels. Nobody needs to be paid to "process" a claim.
