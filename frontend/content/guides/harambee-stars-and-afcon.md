Kenya's men's national football team is the **Harambee Stars** - from
*harambee*, "let's all pull together", the national motto. In June 2027 they
play an Africa Cup of Nations at home for the first time, as co-hosts with
Uganda and Tanzania.

## Kenya at the AFCON

Kenya has qualified for the Africa Cup of Nations **six times**: **1972, 1988,
1990, 1992, 2004 and 2019**. The team has never got past the **group stage**.

- **2004, Tunisia** - Kenya's first AFCON win, 3-0 against Burkina Faso.
- **2019, Egypt** - after 15 years away, Kenya beat neighbours Tanzania 3-2 in
  a thrilling group match, but went out at the group stage.

AFCON 2027 is the chance to go further, with home crowds behind them.

## CHAN 2024 - the dress rehearsal

In **August 2025**, Kenya co-hosted the **African Nations Championship (CHAN)**
- a tournament for players based in their home leagues - with Uganda and
Tanzania. The Harambee Stars:

- **topped their group**, including a famous **1-0 win over Morocco** at
  Kasarani,
- then lost the **quarter-final to Madagascar on penalties** after a 1-1 draw.

It showed what a home crowd can do - and, with crowd trouble at Kasarani and
fines from CAF, what has to be better organised for AFCON.

## The coach

**Benni McCarthy**, the South African former striker who won the Champions
League with Porto and later coached at Manchester United, was appointed in
**March 2025**, with a contract through AFCON 2027.

## The road to 2027

As co-hosts, Kenya are **already in the finals**. They still play qualifying
**Group D** - against **South Africa, Guinea and Eritrea** - for competitive
matches. Only the best-placed team other than Kenya qualifies from the group.

- **26 September 2026** - Kenya 1-1 Eritrea at Nyayo, with Ryan Ogam equalising
  in the 87th minute.
- **1 October 2026** - Guinea v Kenya in Conakry.
- **11 November 2026** - Kenya v South Africa in Nairobi.
- **March 2027** - the final qualifying matches.

The [AFCON hub](/afcon#key-dates) keeps the fixtures and results up to date,
with a button to add them to your calendar.

## The Harambee Starlets

Kenya's women's national team is the **Harambee Starlets**. The women's AFCON
(WAFCON) is a separate tournament - follow them too.

## How to support

- **Buy tickets early** through official channels. See
  [Tickets and Fan ID](/guides/afcon-2027-tickets-and-fan-id).
- **Wear the red, green and black** - national team shirts and flags are sold
  all over Nairobi before big matches.
- **Learn the chants** - start with *"Twende Harambee Stars!"* See the fan
  phrasebook on the [AFCON hub](/afcon#phrasebook).
- **Behave** - crowd trouble at CHAN brought fines and reduced capacity. The
  best support is loud and peaceful.

## Kenyan football to follow

The domestic league is the **FKF Premier League**, with big Nairobi clubs such
as **Gor Mahia** and **AFC Leopards**, whose rivalry, the **Mashemeji Derby**,
is Kenya's biggest club match. See [Sport and fitness in Nairobi](/guides/sport-and-fitness-in-nairobi).
