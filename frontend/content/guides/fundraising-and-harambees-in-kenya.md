**[[Harambee]]** - "let's all pull together" - is how Kenyans raise money
for big costs: hospital bills, school fees, funerals, weddings and
community projects. Done well, it is fast and generous. Done carelessly, it
can cause mistrust.

## When people fundraise

- **Medical bills** - especially for cancer, surgery or long hospital stays.
  See [Cancer care in Nairobi](/guides/cancer-care-in-nairobi).
- **Funerals** - see [Funerals in Kenya](/guides/funerals-in-kenya).
- **School and university fees**.
- **Weddings** - see [Attending a Kenyan wedding](/guides/attending-a-kenyan-wedding).
- **Community projects** - churches, schools, water.

## Ways to raise money

- **A family committee** - relatives and friends meet, set a target and
  assign roles.
- **M-Pesa** - a dedicated [[paybill]] or [[till number]] for the appeal,
  or a trusted treasurer's number.
- **Online fundraising platforms** - let people give by M-Pesa and card,
  share a link, and show how much has been raised.
- **A harambee event** - a gathering where people pledge and give, often
  with a guest of honour.

## Running a transparent appeal

- **Name a treasurer** and a small committee, not one person alone.
- **Use one account** for the appeal, separate from personal money.
- **Share a clear target** and what it's for - a hospital invoice, a fee
  statement.
- **Report back** - how much came in and how it was spent, with receipts.
- **Thank contributors** and share updates.

## The law

There have been proposals for a law requiring permits for public
fundraising appeals, but the bill was rejected in 2024. Charitable
organisations raising money from the public have their own rules - see
[Registering an NGO](/guides/registering-an-ngo-in-kenya).

## Giving safely

- **Check the appeal** with someone who knows the family directly.
- **Confirm the paybill or number** with the family, not from a forwarded
  message.
- **Beware of fake appeals** - stolen photos, urgent pressure, or numbers
  in a stranger's name. See [M-Pesa fraud and scams](/guides/mpesa-fraud-and-scams-in-kenya).
