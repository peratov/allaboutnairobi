Kenya's athletes are famous for a reason, and Nairobi has an active sporting
life of its own: forest trails, running clubs, rugby weekends and golf courses
in the middle of the city.

## The altitude

Nairobi sits at around 1,700 metres. If you arrive from lower ground, you will
feel it for the first week or two: take it easy, drink more water, and expect
your times to be slower at first.

## Running and walking

- **[Karura Forest](/map#place/karura-forest)** - marked trails of different
  lengths, and the most popular place to run. Small entry fee.
- **[Nairobi Arboretum](/map#place/nairobi-arboretum)** - short loops close to
  the centre.
- **[Ngong Road Forest](/map#place/ngong-road-forest)** - quieter trails
  towards Karen.
- **Ngong Hills** - for longer hikes. See
  [Day trips from Nairobi](/guides/day-trips-from-nairobi#ngong-hills).

Running clubs meet in several of these places at weekends, and are an easy way
to meet people.

## Cycling

Road cycling on the busy roads is for confident riders; most people prefer the
forest trails in Karura and Ngong Road Forest, or group rides that start early
in the morning. Hell's Gate National Park near Naivasha lets you cycle among
wildlife.

## Gyms and pools

Gyms range from neighbourhood gyms to large chains in malls and hotels. Many
newer apartment blocks have their own. Pools are mostly at hotels, clubs and
gyms; ask about day passes.

## Team sports and clubs

- **Rugby** has a big following, with club matches on weekends.
- **Football** - the Kenyan Premier League, and pick-up games in every estate.
- **Golf** - Nairobi has several long-established courses, including Muthaiga,
  Karen and Royal Nairobi. Most are members' clubs with visitor green fees.
- **Athletics** - see the [events page](/events) for the Kip Keino Classic and
  road races.

Clubs are a big part of Nairobi social life, and many offer family
memberships.

## Staying safe outdoors

- Run and walk in daylight and in groups, especially on quiet roads.
- Keep your phone out of sight on the street.
- Use the forests' own paths and gates, and follow their opening hours.

See [Staying safe in Nairobi](/guides/safety-in-nairobi).
