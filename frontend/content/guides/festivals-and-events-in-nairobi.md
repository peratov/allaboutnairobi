Nairobi's calendar is busy year-round, with sport, trade shows, music,
food and culture. Exact dates change every year, so use this guide for the
shape of the year and check organisers' announcements - or the
[events page](/events) - for this year's dates.

## Through the year

- **Early in the year** - Nairobi Restaurant Week and tech conferences.
  Golf's Magical Kenya Open is usually in February or March.
- **March** - the **Safari Rally**, Kenya's round of the World Rally
  Championship, with a ceremonial start in Nairobi and stages around
  Naivasha.
- **April to May** - the **Kip Keino Classic**, an athletics meeting in
  Nairobi.
- **June** - the **Nairobi City Marathon**.
- **Late September or early October** - the **Nairobi International Trade
  Fair** at Jamhuri Park, the Agricultural Society of Kenya's week-long show.
- **Late October** - the **Standard Chartered Nairobi Marathon**, with full,
  half, 10km and 5km races.
- **December** - festive events, concerts and Jamhuri Day celebrations on
  12 December, and an exodus [[upcountry]] for Christmas.

See [Public holidays in Kenya](/guides/public-holidays-in-kenya) for the
holidays.

## Music and culture

Nairobi hosts regular **music festivals**, outdoor concerts and picnics
featuring Kenyan and African artists, plus **art, design, film and book
events**. Many run once or several times a year, with dates announced a few
weeks or months ahead. See [Live music in Nairobi](/guides/live-music-in-nairobi)
and [Museums and galleries](/guides/museums-and-galleries-in-nairobi).

## Finding what's on

- The site's [events page](/events).
- Organisers' and venues' social media, where most events are announced.
- Ticketing platforms, which list upcoming events and sell tickets payable by
  [[M-Pesa]].
- Newspaper weekend sections and radio.

## Going to events

- **Buy tickets from official sellers** - fake tickets circulate for big
  events.
- **Plan for traffic** and road closures, especially for marathons and the
  trade fair.
- **Getting home** - book a ride before you leave, and agree with friends
  where to meet. See [Safety in Nairobi](/guides/safety-in-nairobi).
- **Bring sun cover and rain gear** for outdoor events. See
  [Weather in Nairobi](/guides/weather-in-nairobi).
