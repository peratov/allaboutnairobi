Many Nairobi households employ someone at home: a house manager (often called
a house help), a nanny, a gardener, a driver or an [[askari]]. If you do, you
are an employer, with the same legal duties as a company.

## Finding someone

- **Referrals** from friends, neighbours and colleagues are the most common
  route, and usually the best.
- **Agencies** screen candidates and often offer a replacement period. Ask
  what their fee covers and check reviews.
- **Check** ID, references (call them), and ask for a
  [[good conduct certificate]]. Agree a trial period.

## Pay

- **Minimum wage.** In Nairobi, a house worker, gardener or day watchman must
  be paid at least **KES {{ NAIROBI_MIN_WAGE_GENERAL_LABOURER|shillings }} a
  month** from 1 May 2026. Experienced nannies and house managers are
  usually paid more.
- **Housing allowance.** If they do not live in, you must add
  {{ HOUSING_ALLOWANCE_RATE|percent }}% of basic pay. If they live in, provide
  decent accommodation and food as agreed.
- **Pay on time**, by M-Pesa or bank transfer, and give a payslip.

Work out take-home pay and your total cost with the
[household staff calculator](/tools/household-staff-calculator).

## Deductions and registration

You must register as an employer and pay over, every month:

- **[[NSSF]]** - {{ NSSF_RATE|percent }}% from their pay and the same from you.
- **[[SHIF]]** - {{ SHIF_RATE|percent }}% of their gross, minimum KES
  {{ SHIF_MINIMUM|shillings }}.
- **[[Housing Levy]]** - {{ HOUSING_LEVY_RATE|percent }}% from them and the
  same from you, paid through [[iTax]].
- **PAYE** - usually nothing at these wages, after personal relief.

These contributions give your employee a pension and health cover, so they
matter to them as much as to the taxman.

## The contract

Put it in writing - it is required for any job of three months or more. Cover:

- Duties and working hours, including any live-in hours
- Pay, the pay date and how it is paid
- The weekly rest day - at least one full day a week
- Annual leave - at least {{ ANNUAL_LEAVE_DAYS }} working days a year after
  twelve months - and public holidays
- Sick leave, and what happens if they need time off to travel
  [[upcountry]]
- Notice, on both sides

See the [employment contract checklist](/docs/employment-contract-checklist).

## Ending the job

Give the notice in the contract, or at least {{ NOTICE_DAYS_MONTHLY_PAID }}
days if they are paid monthly, and pay everything owed: salary to the last day
and any leave not taken. Dismissal needs a fair reason and a chance for them to
respond. Give a certificate of service.
