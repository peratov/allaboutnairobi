To vote in Kenya's **general election on 10 August 2027**, you must be on
the register of voters. Registration is free and done in person by the
**Independent Electoral and Boundaries Commission** (IEBC).

## Who can register

- **Kenyan citizens** aged **18 or over**.
- With an **original national ID** (or Maisha Card) or a **valid Kenyan
  passport**.
- Not declared of unsound mind or convicted of an election offence in the
  past five years.

Turning 18 soon? Get your ID first - see
[Getting your first national ID](/guides/getting-your-first-national-id).

## Where to register

- **IEBC constituency offices** - every constituency has one.
- **Huduma Centres** - see [Nairobi County services](/guides/nairobi-county-services).
- **Registration drives** - the IEBC runs mass registration campaigns at
  set times, with clerks in schools, markets and other public places.

## How it works

1. Take your **original ID or passport** - copies are not accepted.
2. Fill in the registration form with the clerk.
3. Your **fingerprints and photo** are captured.
4. Choose the **polling station** where you want to vote - usually near
   where you live.
5. Keep the acknowledgement slip.

There is **no fee**. Nobody should ask you to pay.

## Already registered?

- If you registered in a previous cycle, you usually **don't need to
  register again**. Some people who registered before 2012 have been asked
  to re-register; check your status.
- **Check your details** on the IEBC's online verification service or by
  SMS with your ID number, and report any errors.

## Moving constituency

To vote somewhere new, apply for a **transfer** in person at the IEBC
office in your new constituency, before the register closes.

## Kenyans abroad

Registration and voting abroad are only available in selected countries,
usually at embassies, and only for the presidential vote. See
[Kenyans abroad: managing things from afar](/guides/kenyans-abroad-managing-things-from-afar).

## Deadlines

The register is closed some time before the election so it can be
checked. **Register early** rather than waiting for the last weeks.
