Kenyans watch TV in three main ways: **free-to-air** channels through an
aerial, **pay TV** through a decoder, and **streaming** over the internet.
Many households mix them.

## Free-to-air

Kenya's TV is **digital**. With a digital TV (or a set-top box for an older
TV) and an **aerial**, you get the main Kenyan channels for free - news,
local shows, music and some sport. No subscription needed.

## Pay TV

- **Satellite pay TV** - a dish and decoder, with packages from basic to
  premium. The premium packages carry the most **live sport**, including
  European football.
- **Digital terrestrial pay TV** - a decoder and aerial, cheaper packages,
  popular for local and African content.
- Packages are paid **monthly**, usually by [[M-Pesa]] [[paybill]].
  Prices change often; compare packages on the providers' own sites.

Decoders are sold at the providers' shops, supermarkets and electronics
shops. Installation is usually extra.

## Streaming

- **International streaming services** work in Kenya, and some offer
  mobile-only plans. Some can be paid by M-Pesa; others need a card. See
  [Paying for online services from Kenya](/guides/paying-for-online-services-from-kenya).
- **African streaming services** carry African films, series and some
  sport.
- **Free video** on social platforms and broadcasters' own channels.

## Internet for streaming

Streaming uses a lot of data. On mobile data it adds up fast - see
[Saving mobile data](/guides/saving-mobile-data-in-kenya). For regular
streaming, home fibre is much cheaper per gigabyte. See
[Home internet in Nairobi](/guides/home-internet-in-nairobi).

## Sport

Live sport is the main reason many people keep pay TV. Check which
competitions each provider has this season before you subscribe - rights
change. Otherwise, sports bars show big matches; see
[Watching football in Nairobi](/guides/watching-football-in-nairobi).

## Tips

- **Power** - a surge protector protects your TV and decoder from Nairobi's
  power fluctuations. See [Solar and backup power](/guides/solar-and-backup-power).
- **Pause rather than cancel** - many pay TV services let you pay only for
  the months you watch.
- **Avoid pirate boxes and apps** - they are illegal and often stop working.
