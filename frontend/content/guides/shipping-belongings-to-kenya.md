Whether you are an expatriate relocating to Nairobi or a Kenyan coming home,
shipping belongings means choosing a mover, getting through customs at Mombasa
or JKIA, and knowing what you will pay duty on.

## Sea or air

- **Sea freight** - cheapest for furniture and large volumes. A container
  arrives at **Mombasa** and is trucked or railed to Nairobi. Allow two to
  three months from packing to delivery.
- **Air freight** - fast and expensive, for a few boxes you need soon. It
  arrives at JKIA.
- **Excess baggage** - for small moves, often the simplest option.

Many people send a small air shipment and a larger sea shipment.

## Choosing an international mover

- **Get several quotes** after a survey of your home, in person or by video.
- **Ask what is included** - packing, shipping, insurance, customs clearance,
  port charges, delivery and unpacking in Nairobi. "Door to door" should
  include all of these.
- **Check their Kenyan agent**, who handles clearance and delivery.
- **Insure the shipment** at replacement value.
- **Membership of international moving associations** is a useful sign of a
  reputable mover.

## Customs clearance

Everything imported is cleared by **KRA customs**, usually through a licensed
**clearing agent** (your mover's agent). You will need:

- **Your passport** and immigration status - a work permit, pass, or proof of
  return.
- **A detailed inventory** of the shipment.
- **The bill of lading** (sea) or air waybill (air).
- **Your KRA PIN**.

Be present or reachable during clearance. Customs may inspect the shipment.

## Duty

- **Expatriates on work permits** may be able to import used household goods
  and personal effects free of duty, depending on their status. Ask your mover's
  agent and KRA before shipping.
- **New items and large quantities** are usually taxed.
- **Cars** are taxed separately. See
  [Importing a car to Kenya](/guides/importing-a-car-to-kenya).

## Kenyans returning home

KRA allows **returning residents** to bring household goods and personal
effects that were in use abroad, and **one car**, free of duty, if they meet
the conditions. For the car:

- You must have **owned and used it abroad for at least twelve months**.
- It must be **no more than {{ RETURNING_RESIDENT_CAR_MAX_AGE }} years old**.
- It must **arrive within 90 days** of your return (up to 360 days with the
  Commissioner's approval).
- You must **not have used the exemption in the last four years**.

You need your passport with entry and exit stamps, evidence you lived abroad
(visas or permits), the bill of lading and invoices. Check the full rules on
KRA's returning residents page before you ship.

## Things not to ship

- **Plastic carrier bags** - banned in Kenya.
- **Weapons and ammunition**, without permits.
- **Some plants, seeds and foods**, which need permits.
- **Pets** travel under separate rules. See [Pets in Nairobi](/guides/pets-in-nairobi).
