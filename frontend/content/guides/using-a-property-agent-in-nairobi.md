Agents find tenants for landlords, flats for tenants, and buyers for
sellers. Good ones save weeks. But anyone can print a business card, and fake
"agents" are behind most of Nairobi's rental and land scams. This is general
information.

## Registration

Estate agents in Kenya must be **registered with the Estate Agents
Registration Board (EARB)** under the Estate Agents Act and hold a
**practising certificate** renewed every year. Practising without one is an
offence.

To check an agent:

- search the **EARB's register** on its website, or ask the board
- ask to see the **registration and current practising certificates**
- check that the **company** they work for is real - an office, a landline,
  a KRA PIN on the invoice

Many people who show flats are not registered agents but "brokers" or
caretakers working for a fee. They can still be useful, but you have less
protection.

## Commission

Commission is **negotiable and should be agreed in writing** before any work
starts.

- **Sales.** The seller usually pays the agent a percentage of the price.
- **Lettings.** Landlords commonly pay a letting fee, often around a month's
  rent. Some agents also charge tenants.
- **Management.** An agent who collects rent and handles repairs for a
  landlord takes a percentage of the rent collected.

Ask exactly what the fee covers and when it is due - on signing, or on
completion.

## Viewing fees

Some agents in Nairobi charge a **viewing fee** to show flats. It is not a
legal requirement. If you pay one, get a receipt, agree how many properties
it covers, and never pay for viewings you cannot first see listed.

## Avoiding scams

- **Never pay a deposit or rent to an agent's personal M-Pesa or account**
  for a flat you have not seen and whose landlord you have not confirmed.
  Pay the landlord or the agency's company account, against a signed
  agreement.
- Be suspicious of **prices well below the area's rent**, agents "out of the
  country", and pressure to pay today to "hold" a unit.
- **For land, the agent is never your due diligence.** Do your own
  [search](/guides/land-search-on-ardhisasa) and use your own advocate. See
  [Buying land around Nairobi](/guides/buying-land-around-nairobi).
