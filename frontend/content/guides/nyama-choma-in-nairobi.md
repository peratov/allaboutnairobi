[[Nyama choma]] - "roasted meat" in Swahili - is Nairobi's favourite way to
eat, celebrate and pass a long weekend afternoon. It is less a dish than an
occasion: friends around a table, meat arriving chopped on a wooden board,
and nobody in a hurry.

## How it works

1. **Choose your meat** - usually **goat** (mbuzi), the classic, or **beef**
   (ng'ombe). Some places do chicken, pork or lamb.
2. **Order by weight** - at most joints you pick a cut from the butchery
   counter and pay **by the kilo**. Half a kilo per person is generous with
   sides.
3. **Choose how it is cooked** - **choma** (grilled over charcoal) is the
   standard; **wet fry** is stewed with tomatoes and onions after grilling.
4. **Wait** - good choma takes time. Order drinks and sides while you wait.
5. **It arrives chopped** on a board with salt and chilli, to share.

## What to eat with it

- **[[Ugali]]** - the essential side, eaten with your hand.
- **Kachumbari** - a fresh salad of tomatoes, onions and chilli.
- **Sukuma wiki** or other greens.
- **Mukimo**, **chips** or **roast potatoes** at some places.
- **Mutura** - Kenyan sausage, often grilled at the same joints.

Wash your hands first - most places bring a jug and basin to the table.

## Where to go

- **Local joints** - every estate has a butchery with a grill out back,
  often the best value. Ask locals for the favourite.
- **Kenyatta Market** - a busy market off Ngong Road,
  known for its choma section, open late.
- **Famous names** - large restaurants such as Carnivore on Langata Road
  serve meat on skewers carved at your table, popular with visitors and for
  celebrations.
- **Roadside spots** out of town - on the road to Kajiado, Machakos or
  Naivasha - are a weekend tradition. See
  [Day trips from Nairobi](/guides/day-trips-from-nairobi).

## Tips

- **Ask the price per kilo** before ordering, and watch the meat weighed.
- **Go with a group** - choma is meant to be shared.
- **Weekends** are busiest; Sunday afternoons especially.
- **Leave time** - a choma lunch can easily last the afternoon.
- If you don't eat meat, see [Vegetarian food in Nairobi](/guides/vegetarian-food-in-nairobi).
