Most Nairobi flats have a **prepaid meter**: you buy electricity
[[tokens]] in advance, type the code into the meter, and the units run down
as you use power. Postpaid meters, with a monthly bill, are less common.

## Buying tokens by M-Pesa

1. Open M-Pesa and choose **Lipa na M-Pesa**, then **Pay Bill**.
2. Business number: **888880** for prepaid tokens.
3. Account number: your **meter number** (on the meter, usually 11 digits).
4. Enter the amount and your PIN.

You receive an SMS with a **20-digit token**. Type it into the meter's keypad
and press Enter. The units are added.

Postpaid bills are paid to paybill **888888**, with your account number.

You can also buy through the M-Pesa app's utilities section, banking apps, or
at shops that sell tokens.

## The token did not arrive

The units are already assigned to your meter even if the SMS is late.

- **Dial \*977#** and follow the prepaid menu to have your last token sent
  again, using your meter number.
- **Use the Kenya Power self-service portal** on kplc.co.ke to see recent
  tokens.
- **Contact Kenya Power** by calling **97771** or through its official social
  media accounts, with your meter number and the M-Pesa transaction code.

Do not pay again until you have checked: you will just buy more units.

## Common meter errors

- **"Reject" or "error" when entering a token** - check you typed all 20
  digits correctly. Tokens must be entered in the order they were bought.
- **"Used"** - the token is already on the meter.
- **The meter shows no credit after a valid token** - contact Kenya Power with
  the token number.

## Why tokens seem to buy less

A token purchase includes more than power: fuel and inflation adjustments,
levies and VAT. The amount of power per shilling changes each month, and
buying very small amounts is often poorer value because some charges are
spread over each purchase. Check the breakdown in your token SMS.

## Blackouts

- **Check your neighbours** - if their power is on, the problem is likely your
  meter, units or the building.
- **Report it** to Kenya Power on **97771** or through its official accounts,
  with your meter or account number.
- **Planned maintenance** outages are published in advance by Kenya Power, by
  area.

Many buildings have backup generators; ask before you rent. For solar and
inverters, see [Setting up your home](/guides/setting-up-your-home).

## Safety and scams

- **Only Kenya Power staff** should touch the meter and its connection.
  Tampering and illegal connections are criminal offences.
- **Kenya Power will not ask** for payment to a personal number or for your
  M-Pesa PIN.
