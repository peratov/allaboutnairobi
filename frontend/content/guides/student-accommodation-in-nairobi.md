Finding a place to live is one of the first challenges for students coming
to Nairobi. Options range from university halls to private hostels and
shared rooms near campus.

## Your options

- **University halls of residence** - cheapest and closest, but places are
  limited and often go to first-years or students from far away. Apply
  early through your university.
- **Private hostels** - near most campuses, from basic shared rooms to
  self-contained rooms with Wi-Fi, security and meals.
- **Purpose-built student housing** - newer blocks with study areas, gyms
  and backup power, at higher prices.
- **Renting a room or bedsitter** and sharing with friends. See
  [Renting a room and flatmates](/guides/renting-a-room-and-flatmates-in-nairobi).
- **Living with relatives** and commuting, if family is nearby.

## Where students live

Popular areas cluster around the big campuses - near the city centre for
the University of Nairobi and others, Kahawa and along Thika Road for
Kenyatta University and colleges there, and around other campuses in
Karen, Madaraka and beyond. See
[Living in Ruaka and Thika Road](/guides/living-in-ruaka-and-thika-road).

## Checking a hostel

- **Visit in person** before paying - see the actual room.
- **Security** - guards, gates, lighting and safe walking routes to campus.
- **Water and power** - reliable supply, tanks and backup.
- **Internet** - speed and whether it's included.
- **What's included** - meals, cleaning, laundry, bills.
- **Rules** - visitors, curfews, noise.
- **Contract** - rent per semester or month, deposit, and refunds if you
  leave early. Get it in writing.

Never pay deposits to people you can't verify. See
[Rental scams in Nairobi](/guides/rental-scams-in-nairobi).

## Paying for it

- Budget for **rent, food, transport, data and study materials**.
- Pay by [[M-Pesa]] or bank to the hostel's official account, with receipts.
- Ask about **HELB** and scholarships for upkeep. See
  [HELB loans](/guides/helb-loans-repayment-and-clearance) and
  [Applying to university](/guides/applying-to-university-in-kenya).

## Staying safe

- Walk in groups at night, and use ride-hailing after dark.
- Keep laptops and phones secure.
