Nairobi has thousands of short-term rentals, from studios in Kilimani to
cottages in Karen. They suit visitors staying a few days to a few months. Many
people also run them as an investment - and the rules have tightened.

## For guests

- **Book through a platform** with payment protection, not by transferring
  money to a host directly.
- **Read recent reviews**, especially about security, water, power and Wi-Fi.
- **Check the location** against where you need to be at rush hour.
- **Ask about backup power and water**, and whether the building allows guests
  - some blocks stop short-term guests at the gate.

See [Where to stay in Nairobi](/guides/where-to-stay-in-nairobi) and
[Serviced apartments](/guides/serviced-apartments-in-nairobi).

## For hosts: the rules

### Registration

Short-term rental operators must **register with the Tourism Regulatory
Authority (TRA)**. The unit is inspected for safety before a licence is
issued. You may also need the county **business permit**. See
[Nairobi business permit](/guides/nairobi-business-permit).

### Consent

- **Tenants** need the landlord's **written permission** to sublet, usually
  in the lease.
- **Owners** must check the building's **by-laws** and the management
  company's rules. Many apartment blocks restrict or ban short stays.

### Tourism levy

Licensed operators pay a **tourism levy of 2%** of gross receipts to the TRA,
and platforms have been brought into the system from 2026.

### Income tax

Short-term rental income is taxable. Depending on how the letting is
structured, it may fall under residential rental income tax or be taxed as
business income. See [Being a landlord in Kenya](/guides/being-a-landlord-in-kenya)
and take advice from a tax adviser. Large operators may also need to register
for VAT.

## Making it work

- **Furnish for the market** - reliable Wi-Fi, backup power and water matter
  more than décor in Nairobi reviews.
- **Price for occupancy** - count on empty nights, platform fees, cleaning,
  utilities, service charge and furniture wear.
- **Security** - share clear check-in instructions with the building's
  guards, and register guests as the building requires.
