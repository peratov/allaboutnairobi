## Getting the numbers right

- **Take-home pay**, not gross. Use the
  [salary calculator](/tools/salary-calculator).
- **Rent** by area is in [Finding housing](/guides/finding-housing#rough-asking-rents).
- **The lines people forget** - deposits, furniture, family contributions -
  are in [What living in Nairobi costs](/guides/cost-of-living-in-nairobi).

A common rule of thumb is to keep rent under a third of take-home pay.
