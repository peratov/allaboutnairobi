When a job ends, the employer owes more than the last month's salary. This
adds up the legal minimums under the Employment Act.

{% tool "final-dues-calculator" %}

## What goes into it

- **Salary** for every day worked up to the last day.
- **Untaken annual leave.** You earn at least {{ ANNUAL_LEAVE_DAYS }} working
  days a year, and leave not taken is paid out when the job ends.
- **Notice.** At least {{ NOTICE_DAYS_MONTHLY_PAID }} days for someone paid
  monthly, unless the contract says more. The employer can pay it instead of
  having you work it.
- **Severance on redundancy** - at least {{ SEVERANCE_DAYS_PER_YEAR }} days'
  pay for each completed year of service.

## If you are not paid

Raise it in writing first, then with the county labour office. Claims can go
to the Employment and Labour Relations Court. See
[Working in Kenya](/guides/working-in-kenya#when-things-go-wrong).
