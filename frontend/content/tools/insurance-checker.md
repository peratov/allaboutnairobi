Most people in Nairobi find out what insurance the law requires when a police
officer, a customs agent or a hospital asks to see it. Tick what applies to
you here and you get the list first:

- the cover you must hold
- the cover to ask about
- the cover worth weighing up

Each one comes with the law behind it and what to do.

Nothing you tick is sent anywhere. Your answers stay on this device.

## Before you pay for any of it

These apply to every policy, compulsory or not:

- **Check the insurer, broker or agent is licensed** with the Insurance
  Regulatory Authority (IRA) before you pay anyone. Pay the company itself,
  to its own account or Paybill.
- **Compulsory cover has to be renewed.** Put the expiry date somewhere you
  will see it.
- **Read the exclusions and the excess** in the policy wording, not the
  brochure. The excess is the part of every claim you pay yourself.
- **Keep the policy document and the receipt.** A photo on WhatsApp is not the
  policy.

This checker covers only what the guides on this site cover. Your employer,
lender or landlord can ask for cover the law does not. A licensed broker can
tell you what your particular business needs. General information, not
advice.
