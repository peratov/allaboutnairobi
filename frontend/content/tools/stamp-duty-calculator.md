Stamp duty is the biggest government cost of buying property: {{ STAMP_DUTY_URBAN|percent }}%
of the value in towns and cities, including all of Nairobi, and
{{ STAMP_DUTY_RURAL|percent }}% on rural land.

{% tool "stamp-duty-calculator" %}

## How it is charged

- It is paid by the **buyer**.
- It is worked out on the **higher of the price and the government valuer's
  figure**, so an under-declared price does not save anything.
- It is paid through **Ardhisasa** before the transfer is registered.

The whole process, from the search to the new title, is in
[Buying property in Kenya](/guides/buying-property-in-kenya).
