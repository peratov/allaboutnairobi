Kenyan paperwork mostly goes wrong in two ways: a step done in the wrong
order, or a trip to Nyayo House or a Huduma Centre without the one thing the
counter asks for. Pick what you need to do, answer a question or two, and you
get the steps that apply to you - with where to go, what to bring and what it
costs, where a fee is published.

Tick steps off as you go. The ticks stay on this device, and nothing you choose
here is sent anywhere.

## Where these steps come from

Each plan is the short form of a guide on this site, and links to it - read
the guide when a step needs more than a line. Fees come from the same figures
the guides use; where a fee is not published, the plan says so rather than
guessing, and the eCitizen invoice has the last word.

Three things hold for almost every plan:

- **Get eCitizen and your KRA PIN sorted first.** Nearly everything else asks
  for one or both.
- **Make your name match your ID everywhere.** A spelling difference stops SHA
  registration, iTax and passport applications more than anything else.
- **Pay only through eCitizen or an official paybill.** Anyone outside an
  office offering to speed things up for cash is asking for a bribe.
