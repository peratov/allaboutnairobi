"Is KES 100,000 a good salary in Nairobi?" depends on how you want to live.
This calculator works the other way round: say how you want to live, and it
shows the salary you would need, after PAYE, NSSF, SHIF and the Housing Levy
come off.

{% tool "cost-of-living-calculator" %}

## Where the starting figures come from

The two lifestyles are the midpoints of the rough monthly budgets for a single
person in [What living in Nairobi costs](/guides/cost-of-living-in-nairobi):

- **Modest** - renting a bedsitter or one-bedroom in a budget area, cooking at
  home, and getting around by matatu.
- **Comfortable** - a one-bedroom in an upmarket area, eating out often, and
  using ride-hailing or a car.

They are rough 2026 ranges, not quotes. Rent changes the answer more than
anything else, so check [rents by area](/guides/finding-housing#rough-asking-rents)
and put in a real figure.

## How the salary is worked out

The calculator adds up your spending and your savings to get the take-home pay
you need. It then finds the gross pay that leaves exactly that after this
year's deductions:

- PAYE
- NSSF
- SHIF at {{ SHIF_RATE|percent }}%
- the Housing Levy at {{ HOUSING_LEVY_RATE|percent }}%

It uses the same arithmetic as the [salary calculator](/tools/salary-calculator).
