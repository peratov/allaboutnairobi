A night out in Nairobi can cost a few hundred shillings at a local in the
estates, or more than a week's shopping on a rooftop in Westlands. Put in the
prices from the menu and the ride-hailing app to see what the evening costs
you and the group. The planner also tells you how much to keep back for the
ride home.

{% tool "night-out-planner" %}

## Getting home comes first

- **Set aside the fare home first**, before the first round, and don't spend
  it.
- **Book the ride from inside the venue**, and check the car and plate before
  you get in. Avoid walking at night, even short distances.
- **Don't drive after drinking.** Police run breathalyser checks at night, at
  weekends and over holidays. See
  [Alcohol laws in Kenya](/guides/alcohol-laws-in-kenya).

## Keeping the bill under control

- **Agree on the bill** in unfamiliar places before ordering rounds.
- **Watch your drink.** Drink spiking happens, often to rob people.
- **Carry little** - one card and some cash, with your phone out of sight on
  the street.
- **The legal drinking age is {{ ALCOHOL_MINIMUM_AGE }}**, and bouncers may ask
  for ID.

See [Staying safe in Nairobi](/guides/safety-in-nairobi#at-night).
