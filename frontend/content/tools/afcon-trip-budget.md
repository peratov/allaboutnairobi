Coming to Nairobi for AFCON 2027? Put in your group, your nights and how many
matches you want to see. Official fees - the eTA and park entry - are filled in
from the published rates; everything else is yours to adjust.

## Where the figures come from

- **The eTA** is US$ {{ ETA_FEE_USD }} per traveller, and most African
  nationals do not need one. See [Applying for an eTA](/guides/kenya-eta-step-by-step).
- **Nairobi National Park** charges non-resident adults
  US$ {{ NNP_FEE_NONRESIDENT_USD }} a day. See
  [Nairobi National Park](/guides/nairobi-national-park).
- **The Maasai Mara** charges non-resident adults US$ {{ MARA_FEE_LOW_USD }}
  a day until 30 June and US$ {{ MARA_FEE_PEAK_USD }} a day from 1 July - the
  tournament straddles the change. See
  [The Maasai Mara from Nairobi](/guides/maasai-mara-from-nairobi).
- **Ticket prices** have not been announced by CAF. The default is a
  placeholder; replace it when sales open.

## Keeping the cost down

- **Stay near your stadium**, or near the Expressway, to cut matchday rides.
- **Book refundable rooms now** and firm up once the draw sets the fixtures.
- **Use M-Pesa** rather than changing lots of cash. See
  [M-Pesa and mobile money](/guides/mpesa-and-mobile-money).
