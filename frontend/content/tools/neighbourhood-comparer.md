Pick two neighbourhoods and see them side by side: how dear the rents run,
how far it is to the CBD, JKIA and Westlands, how crowded the area is, and
how its homes live - renting, water, sewer, cooking and the internet.

## What the figures are, and what they are not

The figures come at three sizes, and the comparer always says which.

- **The neighbourhood itself.** The rent band is our own judgement from 2026
  rental listings, one to five - a ranking, not a price, and rents inside a
  neighbourhood vary more than between bands. Distances are straight lines
  from the neighbourhood's point on the map; by road they are longer, and in
  traffic the time is what matters, so try the trip at the hour you would
  make it.
- **Its constituency**, one of {{ COMPARE.constituencies|length }}. Crowding
  is WorldPop's 2020 estimate, and relative wealth is Meta's Relative Wealth
  Index - both modelled, not counted.
- **Its census sub-county**, one of {{ COMPARE.subcounties|length }}. Homes,
  water, sewer, cooking, internet use and age are the 2019 Kenya Population
  and Housing Census, Kenya's latest, as KNBS published it. The census
  sub-counties are large, so two neighbourhoods in the same one show the
  same census figures, and the comparer says so.
