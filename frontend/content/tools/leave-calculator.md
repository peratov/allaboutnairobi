Every employee in Kenya earns at least {{ ANNUAL_LEAVE_DAYS }} working days of
paid annual leave a year, built up month by month. This shows where you stand.

{% tool "leave-calculator" %}

## The rules

- **{{ ANNUAL_LEAVE_DAYS }} working days** a year is the minimum after twelve
  months' service; a contract can give more.
- **Leave cannot be paid off** while you remain employed. Untaken leave is paid
  out only when the job ends - see the
  [final dues calculator](/tools/final-dues-calculator).
- **Public holidays** are separate from annual leave.
