The rent is not the number that catches people out. It is everything due on
the day you sign. Put in the figures from the listing to see the total.

{% tool "move-in-cost-calculator" %}

## Typical figures

- **Deposit:** usually one or two months' rent, refundable at the end.
- **Agency fee:** only when an agent is involved. Agree it, and who pays it,
  before you view.
- **Water and electricity deposits:** where the meters are in the landlord's
  name. Prepaid electricity meters usually need none.

See [Finding a place to live](/guides/finding-housing) for rents by area and
what to check before you pay, and the
[rent and deposit receipt template](/docs/rent-receipt) for the paperwork.
