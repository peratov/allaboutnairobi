company. This works out their payslip and your full monthly cost.

## The rules it applies

- **Minimum wage.** A general labourer in Nairobi - which covers house workers,
  cleaners, gardeners and day watchmen - must be paid at least KES
  {{ NAIROBI_MIN_WAGE_GENERAL_LABOURER|shillings }} a month from 1 May 2026.
- **Housing allowance.** If you do not provide housing, you must pay
  {{ HOUSING_ALLOWANCE_RATE|percent }}% of basic pay on top.
- **Deductions.** NSSF, SHIF and the Housing Levy apply to household staff just
  as to anyone else. PAYE is usually nothing at these wages, because personal
  relief covers it.
