A flat's headline yield - a year's rent divided by the price - flatters it.
Empty months, fees and tax take a large share. This shows what is left.

{% tool "rental-yield-calculator" %}

## Reading the result

- **Gross yield** is a year's rent over the price, with no costs.
- **Net yield** is what is left after costs and tax, over the price. It is the
  figure to compare with a [savings product](/tools/savings-calculator).
- Neither includes **mortgage interest**, or any change in the property's
  value.

Rental income tax is charged at {{ MRI_RATE|percent }}% of gross rent, with no
deductions. See [Being a landlord in Kenya](/guides/being-a-landlord-in-kenya)
and [Buying property in Kenya](/guides/buying-property-in-kenya).
