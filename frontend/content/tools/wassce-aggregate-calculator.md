The aggregate is the number Ghanaian university admission runs on, and it is
counter-intuitive twice: **lower is better**, and only six subjects count.

{% tool "wassce-aggregate-calculator" %}

## How it is built

{{ WASSCE_CORE_SUBJECTS }} core subjects &mdash; English Language, Core
Mathematics, and Integrated Science or Social Studies depending on the
programme &mdash; plus your best {{ WASSCE_ELECTIVES_COUNTED }} electives.

Grades score A1 = 1 through F9 = 9, so a perfect aggregate is 6.

## Credits matter separately from the aggregate

{{ WASSCE_CREDIT_GRADE|number }} &mdash; C6 &mdash; is the lowest grade that
counts as a **credit pass**. Public universities want six credit passes
*including* the core subjects. A good aggregate does not help if Core
Mathematics is a D7.

The general cut-off for a public university is aggregate
{{ UNIVERSITY_GENERAL_CUTOFF_AGGREGATE }} or better. Competitive programmes cut
off far lower &mdash; medicine, law, pharmacy and engineering are often in
single figures.

!!! note "An aggregate outside the cut-off is not the end"
    A resit, a technical university, an access or mature-student route, or a
    private university are all real paths, and Ghanaian employers care much
    less about which university than students expect.
