Lenders decide how much you can borrow mostly from how much of your monthly
pay they will let go on repayments. Start with that, and work back to a price.

{% tool "mortgage-affordability-calculator" %}

## Before you go to a bank

- **Compare offers** on the total cost, not only the rate: arrangement fees,
  valuation, legal fees and insurance add up.
- **Ask whether the rate is fixed or variable.** Most Kenyan mortgages are
  variable, so repayments can rise.
- **Budget for stamp duty** - {{ STAMP_DUTY_URBAN|percent }}% in Nairobi - and
  legal fees on top of the deposit. See the
  [stamp duty calculator](/tools/stamp-duty-calculator).
