Most wasted trips to a bank come from asking for the wrong account or leaving
a document at home. Answer a few questions to get the kind of account to ask
for, and one list of everything to bring.

Nothing you choose is sent anywhere. Your answers stay on this device.

## Before you go

- **Every bank sets its own list.** These are the documents banks typically
  ask for. Check the bank's own list before you go, especially for a
  business or chama account.
- **Ask about fees in writing** - monthly charges, card fees and the cost of
  moving money to M-Pesa differ a lot between banks.
- **Your employer's bank** can make a salary account easier to open.

General information, not financial advice.
