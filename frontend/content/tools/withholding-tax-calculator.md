When a Kenyan company pays a consultant or freelancer, it usually keeps back
part of the fee as **withholding tax** and pays it to KRA on your behalf. This
shows what actually reaches your account.

{% tool "withholding-tax-calculator" %}

## The rates

- **{{ WHT_PROFESSIONAL_RESIDENT|percent }}%** on management, professional,
  consultancy and training fees paid to Kenyan residents. Payments to one
  person totalling KES {{ WHT_PROFESSIONAL_THRESHOLD|shillings }} or less in a
  month are exempt.
- **{{ WHT_PROFESSIONAL_NONRESIDENT|percent }}%** for non-residents, sometimes
  reduced by a tax treaty.

## For residents it is a credit, not a final tax

Ask every client for the **withholding tax certificate**, and check it appears
on your [[iTax]] account. On your annual return, the tax already withheld is
credited against what you owe, and any excess can be refunded.
