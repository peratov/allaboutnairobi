## Where to find the value

The unimproved site value is on your **rates bill** and in the county's
valuation roll, against the plot's **land reference number**. It is not what
the property would sell for: buildings are ignored, and the roll's values
date from 2019.

Some areas pay a **flat rate** per plot instead. Flat owners usually pay
through the **service charge** rather than directly.

## Arrears

Unpaid rates carry **penalties and interest** on top of what the calculator
shows, and stay with the land. You need a **rates clearance certificate** to
sell or transfer, so arrears always have to be settled in the end - and buyers
should make sure the seller clears them first.
