Ghana's rules change without announcing themselves. A fee moves and the sign on
the wall does not. An office relocates and the only notice is the queue outside
the old one. A levy is repealed in a budget and you are still being charged it
three months later because nobody told the person at the counter.

This newsletter is one email a month about what actually changed.

## What it will carry

**What changed.** Fees, forms, offices, deadlines. When a passport fee moves or
a permit stops requiring a document it required last year, that is the whole
reason this exists.

**What a thing costs now.** Every figure on this site carries a source and an
expiry date, and when one is re-checked against the GRA, SSNIT or PURC, the new
number goes here.

**Which guides were rewritten**, and why. If the law moved, you should know
that the guide moved with it.

**One thing worth knowing about the month ahead** &mdash; the rains, the
harmattan, the school terms, the holidays that shut every office for a week
whether or not you had an appointment.
&mdash; [Weather and seasons ➞](/guides/weather-and-seasons)
&mdash; [Public holidays ➞](/guides/public-holidays-ghana)

## What it will not carry

No sponsorships. No affiliate links. No "10 best neighbourhoods" written by
somebody who has not been to any of them. Nothing sold, and your address
forwarded to nobody.

If a month passes with nothing worth saying, you will get a short email saying
that, or no email at all. A newsletter that has to fill a quota stops being
worth opening.

## Where the site is, as of this month

- **116 guides**, from the rent advance to registering a birth
- **10 calculators**, including the business viability checker, import duty, vehicle import duty, withholding
  tax and the WASSCE aggregate &mdash; all running in your browser, none of
  them sending what you type anywhere
- **110 glossary entries**, from SSNIT to *chale*
- **An interactive map** of Ghana's 16 regions and Greater Accra's 29 district
  assemblies, with all 34 constituencies, 45 landmarks, and layers from the
  2021 census &mdash; who lives where, and what work they do
  &mdash; [See it ➞](/map)

Newest this month: [reporting broken
infrastructure](/guides/reporting-broken-infrastructure) &mdash; who owns the
street light, the pothole and the billboard, and how to report each so that
something happens.

## The honest bit

There are **58 figures on this site past their re-check date**. The build knows
about every one of them and says so, which is the point of dating them in the
first place. Working through that list is the main job for the coming months,
and each batch that gets verified will be reported here.

If you find a figure that is wrong before I do, that is the single most useful
email this site can receive.
&mdash; [Corrections ➞](/contact)

## One thing for the month ahead

September is the start of Accra's minor rainy season, and the drains that will
flood in October are blocked now. If your street has one that is choked, this
is the month to report it &mdash; a blocked drain reported in September is a
report; the same drain in October is a rescue.

&mdash; [How to report it, and who to ➞](/guides/reporting-broken-infrastructure)
