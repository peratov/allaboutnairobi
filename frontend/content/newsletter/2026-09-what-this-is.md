Kenya's rules change without much warning. A Finance Act moves the tax bands,
the NSSF limits rise every February, SHA changes what SHIF covers, and an
eCitizen form grows a new required field. This newsletter is one email a month
about what changed, so you do not find out at the counter.

## What each issue will carry

- **What changed** - rates, fees, limits and procedures that moved this month,
  and the guides updated because of it.
- **What it costs now** - where prices in Nairobi have shifted noticeably.
- **New guides** - one or two, usually on something readers asked about.

## What it will not carry

No sponsored content, no affiliate links, and nothing sent more than once a
month.
