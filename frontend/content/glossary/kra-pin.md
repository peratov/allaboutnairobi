Your personal tax number. Employers, banks, landlords, car sellers and the land registry all ask for it.

Register free on [[iTax]]. Foreign residents usually need a work or residence permit, or an alien ID, first. See [the paperwork guide](/guides/ecitizen-kra-pin-and-ids).
