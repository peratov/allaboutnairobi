The most useful message this site can receive is short and specific: *"I did
this last week and the fee was different"*, or *"that office has moved"*.

## Report something wrong

Email **[{{ CONTACT_EMAIL }}](mailto:{{ CONTACT_EMAIL }})**. Please include:

- the page you are writing about
- what it says now
- what it should say
- where you got that, if there is a source or you saw it yourself

You do not need to be polite about it. A blunt correction is worth more than a
diplomatic one.

Or say it on X: **[@{{ X_HANDLE }}]({{ X_URL }})**. It is the only account this
site has. Anything else using this name is not us.

## Things worth telling us

- **A fee changed.** Especially at the NIA, the DVLA, Ghana Immigration or the
  Registrar of Companies, where published schedules and counter reality drift
  apart.
- **A process changed.** An office moved, a form went online, a step was
  dropped or added.
- **A guide is missing.** If you spent a week working something out the hard
  way, that is exactly the guide that should exist.
- **A term is missing from the [glossary](/glossary).** Especially Twi, Ga or
  pidgin that everyone uses and nobody translates.

## What we cannot do

We cannot answer individual questions about your case &mdash; your visa, your
landlord, your tax assessment. There is one of us and a great many of you, and
an answer given badly to a specific situation does more harm than no answer.

If a guide does not cover your situation, that is useful information in itself.
Tell us, and the guide gets better for the next person.
