Under the Employment Act 2007, a job lasting three months or more must have a
written contract. Go through this before you sign. Two offers with the same
headline figure can be worth quite different amounts.

## The pay structure

- [ ] **What is the basic salary?** Not the gross; the basic.
- [ ] **What are the allowances**, itemised? House, transport, airtime.
- [ ] **Is the figure gross or net?** Offers are usually quoted gross.

Why it matters: [[NSSF]] is charged on pensionable pay, while PAYE, [[SHIF]]
and the [[Housing Levy]] are charged on gross pay. Work out what you will
actually take home with the worked example in
[PAYE, NSSF, SHIF and the Housing Levy](/guides/paye-and-taxes).

## The basics

- [ ] **Job title and duties**, specific enough to mean something
- [ ] **Start date**, and the end date if it is a fixed-term contract
- [ ] **Place of work**, and whether you can be transferred
- [ ] **Reporting line**
- [ ] **For foreigners: who applies for, and pays for, the work permit**

## Time

- [ ] **Working hours**, and whether overtime is paid. The general wage rules
      pay overtime at one and a half times the normal rate, and double on rest
      days and public holidays.
- [ ] **Annual leave.** At least {{ ANNUAL_LEAVE_DAYS }} working days a year.
- [ ] **Sick leave.** At least 7 days on full pay and 7 on half pay a year,
      after two months' service.
- [ ] **Maternity leave.** {{ MATERNITY_LEAVE_MONTHS }} months on full pay.
- [ ] **Paternity leave.** {{ PATERNITY_LEAVE_WEEKS }} weeks on full pay.

## Ending it

- [ ] **Probation.** Up to {{ PROBATION_MAX_MONTHS }} months, extendable once by
      up to six more with your agreement.
- [ ] **Notice period**, both ways. At least {{ NOTICE_DAYS_MONTHLY_PAID }} days
      for someone paid monthly.
- [ ] **Payment in lieu of notice** - is it allowed?
- [ ] **Redundancy.** The legal minimum severance is 15 days' pay for each
      completed year of service. Does the contract offer more?

## Benefits

- [ ] **Medical cover.** Is private insurance provided on top of SHIF? Does it
      include your family? What are the inpatient and outpatient limits?
- [ ] **Pension.** Is there an employer pension scheme on top of NSSF, and how
      much does the employer put in?
- [ ] **Bonus or commission.** Contractual or discretionary? Discretionary
      means it may never arrive.
- [ ] **Relocation, housing and school fees**, if you are moving for the job.

## The clauses to read twice

- [ ] **Restraint of trade.** How long, what scope, where? Overbroad restraints
      are often unenforceable, but arguing that is expensive.
- [ ] **Confidentiality and intellectual property.** Who owns what you create?
- [ ] **Training bonds.** If the employer pays for training, must you repay it
      if you leave early?
- [ ] **Deductions.** What can be taken from your pay, and on what authority?

## Before you sign

- [ ] Read the whole thing
- [ ] Ask about anything unclear **in writing**, and keep the reply
- [ ] Check that everything promised verbally is in the document
- [ ] **Keep a signed copy**

## After you start

- [ ] **Check your first payslip** against the offer
- [ ] **Check NSSF and SHIF contributions are arriving** in your NSSF and SHA
      records. Employers occasionally deduct and fail to pay over.
- [ ] **Get your P9** at the end of the year for your tax return
