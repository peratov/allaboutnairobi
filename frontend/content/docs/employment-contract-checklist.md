A contract of employment for six months or more must be in writing under the
Labour Act 2003 (Act 651), within two months of starting.

Go through this before you sign. Two offers with the same headline figure can
be worth materially different amounts.

## The pay structure

This is the one people miss.

- [ ] **What is the basic salary?** Not the gross — the basic.
- [ ] **What are the allowances**, itemised? Transport, rent, fuel,
      responsibility.
- [ ] **What is the ratio between them?**

Why it matters:

- **[[SSNIT]] is charged on basic salary only** — {{ SSNIT_TOTAL_RATE|percent }}%
  in total, of which {{ SSNIT_EMPLOYEE_RATE|percent }} points come from you and
  {{ SSNIT_EMPLOYER_RATE|percent }} from your employer.
- **[[PAYE]] is charged on basic plus allowances.**

An employer offering a low basic and large allowances pays less SSNIT and
shrinks your eventual pension, while your tax bill is unchanged. Ask for the
split in writing and run it through the
[salary calculator](/tools/salary-calculator) before you accept.

## The basics

- [ ] **Job title and duties**, described specifically enough to mean something
- [ ] **Start date**
- [ ] **Place of work**, and whether you can be transferred
- [ ] **Reporting line**
- [ ] **Permanent, fixed term, or casual** — this changes your notice rights

## Time

- [ ] **Working hours.** The statutory maximum is {{ MAX_WEEKLY_HOURS }} hours
      a week.
- [ ] **Overtime.** Is it paid, and at what rate? The Act sets no multiplier,
      so the contract decides entirely.
- [ ] **Annual leave.** At least {{ ANNUAL_LEAVE_DAYS }} working days by law.
      It cannot be paid off while you remain employed.
- [ ] **Sick leave.** **There is no statutory minimum in Ghana.** Whatever the
      contract says is what you get. If it says nothing, ask, and get the
      answer in writing.
- [ ] **Maternity leave.** {{ MATERNITY_LEAVE_WEEKS }} weeks by law, or
      {{ MATERNITY_LEAVE_WEEKS_EXTENDED }} after a caesarean or the birth of
      two or more children.
- [ ] **Paternity leave.** Not statutory. Some employers offer it anyway.

## Ending it

- [ ] **Probation length.** {{ PROBATION_MAX_MONTHS }} months is the common
      maximum; the Act requires only that it be reasonable.
- [ ] **Notice period**, both ways.
      {{ NOTICE_PERIOD_MONTHS_PERMANENT }} month is the statutory minimum for
      contracts of three years or more.
- [ ] **Payment in lieu of notice** — is it permitted?
- [ ] **Redundancy terms**

## Benefits

- [ ] **Health insurance.** Is private cover provided? Does it include
      dependants? What is the annual limit, and does it cover dental and
      optical?
- [ ] **Tier 2 provider.** Which corporate trustee manages it?
- [ ] **Tier 3.** Is a voluntary scheme offered? Contributions are tax
      deductible up to {{ TIER_3_MAX_RELIEF_RATE|percent }}% of basic salary.
- [ ] **Bonus or commission.** Discretionary or contractual? Discretionary
      means it may never arrive.
- [ ] **Training and study leave**

## The clauses to read twice

- [ ] **Non-compete.** How long, what scope, what geography? Overbroad
      restraints are often unenforceable, but arguing that is expensive.
- [ ] **Confidentiality**
- [ ] **Intellectual property.** Who owns what you create? This matters a great
      deal in technology and creative roles.
- [ ] **Bonding or training repayment.** If the employer pays for training, are
      you required to stay, and what must you repay if you leave early?
- [ ] **Deductions.** What can be taken from your pay, and on what authority?

## Before you sign

- [ ] Read the whole thing
- [ ] Ask about anything unclear **in writing**, and keep the reply
- [ ] Verify that anything promised verbally appears in the document. If it is
      not in the contract, it did not happen.
- [ ] **Keep a signed copy**

## After you start

- [ ] **Check your first payslip** against what you were promised
- [ ] **Confirm SSNIT contributions are actually reaching SSNIT** — register on
      the self-service portal. Employers deduct and fail to remit more often
      than you would like, and you discover it decades later.
- [ ] **Check PAYE is computed after SSNIT is deducted**, not on gross pay
