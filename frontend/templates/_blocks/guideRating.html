{# The rating a guide has earned, and the prompt that collects it. Two separate things, deliberately in one file because they must not disagree about what a rating means. The score is only rendered once enough readers have answered. A guide showing "5.0 from 1 rating" is the same failure as a hole in a heatmap looking like data, or a nightlife venue carrying confidence 0.98 on a price nobody checked: a number presented as a finding when it is noise. Below the threshold this block renders nothing at all, which on three hundred pages is quieter than three hundred "no ratings yet" lines. There is no schema.org aggregateRating here, and that is on purpose. Google treats review markup a site applies to its own content as self-serving, and it is a documented reason for a manual action rather than a route to stars in the SERP.
