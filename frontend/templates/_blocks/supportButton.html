{# The persistent support button. Deliberately not Buy Me a Coffee's official widget script. That widget sits on the blocklists most ad blockers ship with, so on a good number of machines it renders nothing at all - which defeats the entire point of a button meant to be visible wherever you are on the page. A plain link is almost never blocked, costs no third-party JavaScript, needs no CSP grant, and works with JavaScript switched off. It keeps Buy Me a Coffee's yellow so it still reads as what it is, and uses Boa Me Na Me Mmoa Wo - "help me and let me help you" - which is the adinkra symbol for precisely this transaction. Shown by default and hidden by JavaScript only if the reader has dismissed it before. The other way round would mean no button at all without scripts. #}
